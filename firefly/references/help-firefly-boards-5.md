## Convert 2D images into 3D assets

### Convert a 2D image into a 3D element so it can be viewed from different angles and perspectives.

When an image is on the Firefly Boards canvas, you can convert that 2D image into a 3D version and then inspect it from different perspectives, angles, and positions.

For example, you can import a chair photo and convert it into a 3D asset. That lets you view the chair from several sides and place it in a virtual room layout without leaving the project.

In the left panel on the Firefly homepage, choose Ideate.

Use New board to create a blank canvas.

Upload or generate the image you want to convert into a 3D element, then add it to the canvas.

Select the image, choose Convert, and then choose Image to 3D.

Use Image to 3D when you need to change an object's perspective, distance, or angle in a scene.

Image to 3D controls help alter how scene objects are viewed.

The converted 3D object appears on the canvas, and these controls let you adjust its perspective and angle.

- Orbit or Rotate: Adjust the viewpoint around the object.

Rotate the image to change the view of the 3D object.

Use rotation to view the 3D object from another angle.

Pan or Move: Shift the view horizontally or vertically.

Pan or move the image to change the object's vertical and horizontal perspective.

Use Pan or Move to reposition how the object is seen across the view.

- Zoom: Move closer to or farther from the object.

Zoom to move closer and change the object's view in the image.

Move in or out with Zoom to adjust how the object is seen.

You can use the Reset view option to return the view to its default angle.

Use the Snapshot option to capture an image of the scene, then either upscale it for a refined version or save the basic snapshot.

Use Snapshot to save a copy of the adjusted scene.

Snapshot saves a copy of the scene after your adjustments.

Select the icon to access these actions:

- Open copy in Adobe Express: Send a duplicate of the current image to Adobe Express for quick design updates.
- Copy link: Create a shareable link to the current project or image for collaboration or outside review.
- Cut, copy, duplicate, or paste image elements.
- Rearrange, delete, and flip the image.

## Upscale images with Topaz Bloom

Use Topaz Labs Bloom in Firefly Boards to enlarge and improve generated images, with selectable creativity settings and resolution choices.

Firefly Boards includes Topaz Labs Bloom as an AI upscaling option for generated images. Use it when you want better image quality and a higher-resolution result for creative work produced on a Boards canvas.

Use this workflow with images made from Firefly or partner models after those images have already been placed on the canvas.

Before you begin:

Generate at least one image, then place it on your Firefly Boards canvas.

Select the image on the canvas so its context menu appears.

From that context menu, choose Upscale with Topaz Bloom.

Use the upscale icon to display the upscale image menu.

Open the upscale image menu by selecting the upscale icon.

In the Upscale image menu, set the creativity level you want:

- Subtle Creativity: keeps the result restrained and refined.
- Low Creativity: makes only a small shift toward artistic variation or experimentation.
- Medium Creativity: introduces new ideas with a moderate amount of originality.
- High Creativity: produces a more expressive result with distinctive visual ideas.
- Max Creativity: uses the widest range of imagination and experimentation.

At higher creativity settings, the output gains more AI-enhanced detail and can move farther from the starting image.

Stronger creativity choices add extra AI-enhanced detail, while also increasing the chance of visible differences from the original.

Choose 1080p or 4K as the output resolution.

Use the selected output resolution and creativity level to see the required consumable credits.

The credit cost is calculated from the output resolution and creativity level you select.

Then select Generate to start upscaling.

When the cursor appears, move it to the place on the canvas where the upscaled image should go, then release it to place the image.

## Upscale videos with Topaz Astra

### Use Astra by Topaz Labs to adjust generated or uploaded videos in Firefly Boards.

Topaz Astra brings AI video upscaling into Firefly Boards for generated videos. Apply the video upscaling option when you want to improve creativity, resolution, and overall quality for videos made in Boards or added to Boards.

This workflow works with videos generated by Firefly or partner models, as well as uploaded videos placed on the canvas.

Before you begin:

Generate or upload a video, then add it to your Firefly Boards canvas.

Select the video on the canvas to show its context menu.

From the context menu, choose the Upscale with Topaz Astra icon.

Select the upscale icon to display the upscale video menu.

Open the upscale video menu by selecting the upscale icon.

In the Upscale video menu, pick the Enhancement type you want:

- Precise: improves resolution and clarity while keeping the original video's appearance and details intact.
- Creative: adds or invents detail for a more stylized, visually enhanced output that can differ from the source video.

Pick an enhancement type when you want to sharpen and clean the video, or when you want more detail and style.

Use the enhancement type to decide whether the video should be cleaned up or pushed toward stronger detail and style.

Changing the optimization type also changes Details. Select one of the following details based on the Optimize option you chose.

- Precise: choose Speed or Quality as the parameter to upscale.
- Creative: choose Subtle or Bold as the creativity level for upscaling the video.

Tune speed and quality, or set how much creativity the result should use.

Set whether optimization favors speed and quality, or define the desired creative strength.

Choose 1080p or 4K as the output resolution.

Select Generate to start the upscaling process.

When the cursor appears, move it to the canvas location where the upscaled video should be placed, then release it to place the video.
