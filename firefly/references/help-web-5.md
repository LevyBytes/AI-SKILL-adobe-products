## Firefly AI Assistant overview

Explore Firefly AI Assistant (beta), a conversational creative agent for planning, creating, and editing content with simple natural language.

Firefly AI Assistant (beta) combines ideation, generation, and editing in one place, helping you move from an idea to a finished output more quickly and intuitively. Instead of moving between tools or performing each step manually, describe the result you want, and the assistant brings Adobe creative app power and precision into a single conversational interface to help reach it.

By drawing on Creative Cloud apps such as Photoshop, Lightroom, Illustrator, Premiere, Firefly, and more, the assistant connects capabilities across interfaces. That makes it possible to work with images, visual designs, and video in one unified experience.

### How it works

Firefly AI Assistant (beta) turns creative intent into action through conversation:

- Start with a goal: choose a creative skill, or describe the thing you want to make in the prompt bar. Examples include "make these assets into a social campaign" or "resize and retouch these images".
- The assistant plans the workflow: wait while the agent interprets the request, breaks it into steps, and chooses suitable tools and actions.
- Generate and edit in one flow: create new content, adjust existing assets, and iterate inside the same interface and conversation.
- Refine with guidance: approve or reject suggested improvements, and step in whenever you need to refine the output, redirect the work, or change details.

### Key capabilities

- Conversational process: describe the desired result in plain language, then let the assistant determine the path, whether that means generating assets, editing visuals, or combining actions into a workflow.
- One workflow from idea to output: Firefly AI Assistant lets creators state the outcome in their own words while the assistant prepares and runs complex, multi-step workflows with tools from the Adobe creative suite.
- Output across multiple formats: create in formats such as images, designs, and video, supporting end-to-end content creation from one place.
- Guided revision and variation: use natural conversation to revise, explore variations, and refine outputs instead of making every adjustment manually.
- Firefly web integration: work in the Firefly web interface, where generative tools, assets, and workflows are connected, automated, and repeatable.

You can also use thumbs-up or thumbs-down, together with the feedback option, to tell us what worked or did not work.

## Adobe Brand Intelligence overview

Learn about Adobe Brand Intelligence and how brand knowledge can become scalable content that stays on brand throughout creative content workflows.

Adobe Brand Intelligence is a brand intelligence platform that keeps learning, giving teams a faster and lower-effort way to create and validate content that matches the brand. The platform is headless and available through APIs, which lets it connect to existing first- and third-party apps instead of operating as a standalone app.

Across content workflows, it interprets your brand, applies standards automatically, and lowers the amount of manual review required.

With Adobe Brand Intelligence, you can:

- Maintain brand consistency for every asset
- Find problems before content is published
- Increase content production while preserving quality

Rather than depending only on written guidelines, the system learns from your assets, prior reviews, and team decisions, which helps apply brand standards consistently each time.

### What is Adobe Brand Intelligence?

Adobe Brand Intelligence turns an organization's brand knowledge into a structured format that can be used operationally. It combines brand guidelines, design systems, creative assets, human feedback, and approvals.

In many enterprises, formal documentation covers only a limited part of what people know about the brand. Most of that knowledge lives as team expertise that is not written down. Adobe Brand Intelligence closes this gap by:

- Recording both explicit rules and implicit human judgment
- Using guidelines to apply brand standards consistently across all content workflows
- Lowering dependence on manual reviews and subjective interpretation
- Letting organizations grow content production while protecting brand integrity

### Key skills

Adobe Brand Intelligence supports core capabilities that connect across Adobe apps, third-party tools, and generative AI models.

### Validate

Before content is published, the Validate skill automatically determines whether it follows brand standards.

It reviews visual design, layout, composition, typography, and brand policies.

This review reduces manual-review time and effort, supports consistent brand compliance across projects, and helps designs reach market faster. Integrations can include apps such as Workfront and Frame.

### Instruct to Assemble

A campaign brief is converted by the Instruct to Assemble skill into on-brand content variants, using suitable creative elements that are combined into production-ready outputs.

It helps you by:

- Reading the brief to identify audiences, channels, formats, and campaign goals
- Choosing approved elements and the best-matching base creatives
- Preparing assets for production with creative edits and validation checks
- Building multiple design variants for different channels and audiences

This accelerates content production, cuts down manual work for asset selection and assembly, helps teams produce more on-brand design variations at scale, and raises output quality through continuous learning. It can be integrated easily into apps such as Figma.

## Instruct to Assemble

See how Adobe Brand Intelligence can automatically assemble on-brand & personalized variants.

Instruct to Assemble is an Adobe Brand Intelligence skill that generates on-brand and personalized design variants automatically. In Figma, you can use Instruct to assemble through the Adobe Brand Intelligence plugin to quickly create multiple on-brand variants from predefined templates and brand guidelines.

Enterprise customers can use this feature to streamline content creation by converting brand context into production-ready variants. The system automatically manages complex workflow steps such as image selection, cropping, color application, and brand guideline validation.

### Before you begin

Before starting, confirm these items:

- You can sign in to Adobe Brand Intelligence with enterprise credentials
- Your dedicated Forward Deployed Engineering team has loaded global and campaign-specific data into Adobe Brand Artifactory

### Configure and generate variants

Open the Instruct to Assemble panel in your workspace.

From the dropdown menu, choose the appropriate Context for your campaign or brand requirements; examples include channel, audience, and product data.

Assembly options are set up for each organization according to its needs, so they may appear differently in your environment.

Assembly options are set up for each organization according to its needs, so they may appear differently in your environment.

Choose Assembly options to open the configuration section. Adobe Brand Artifactory configures these options around your brand and campaign identity.

In Variant preference, fill in Number of variants with the count to create for each design.

Choose Assemble to begin generation.

Adobe Brand Intelligence chooses a plan, uses the best images, and applies brand and campaign-level guidelines.

Adobe Brand Intelligence chooses a plan, uses the best images, and applies brand and campaign-level guidelines.

Wait as the system processes the request and creates the assets.

Tip

When testing a new context configuration, begin with 2-3 variants to check output quality before creating larger batches.

### What happens during assembly

While your variants are being generated, the system carries out several automated operations:

- Determines which variants to create
- Chooses optimal images from your DAM
- Crops assets and changes their size for designated templates
- Uses colors and gradients from your brand guidelines that are specific to the campaign
- Checks output against brand compliance rules
- Automatically resolves any validation issues that remain

Processing duration depends on how many variants you requested and how complex your brand guidelines are. During generation, the system shows progress indicators.

### Review generated assets

After assembly finishes, the system shows all generated creative assets inside the context you selected.

Review the assembled results to make sure they satisfy campaign requirements. If necessary, you can manually adjust individual assets or regenerate variants with different assembly options.
