To add media to the timeline, first bring video, audio, and image assets into the project. After import, place those assets on the timeline and begin arranging, trimming, and layering them into the video sequence.

## Connect clips

### Connect clips to the timeline while editing video in Firefly video editor.

In Firefly video editor, the Connect option links secondary layers, including footage, images, titles, or audio, to a primary clip, usually the clip at the bottom of the layer stack. The connection keeps linked media aligned with that primary clip. In effect, secondary media stays attached to the primary clip, so timeline adjustments or movement of the primary clip move the connected items too. This keeps edits synchronized and prevents elements from shifting unexpectedly.

Connected layers can still be moved individually along the timeline. If the primary clip moves, all layers connected to it move automatically as a group.

Before you begin:

Begin with a new project that already has media files or text elements on the timeline.

Decide which clip should be primary for the connected layers, then move that clip to the bottom of the layer stack in the timeline.

Open the main menu at the upper-left of the page, then choose Connect under the Clip option.

The Connect option links the primary clip with other layers, including footage, images, titles, or audio.

The Connect option links the primary clip with other layers, including footage, images, titles, or audio.

Repeat these steps for each layer that should connect to the primary clip.

After connection, those layers stay synchronized with the primary clip, helping the timeline remain organized and the edit stay consistent.

Tip

To disconnect a layer from the primary clip, select the layer to remove from the connection. Then go to main menu > Clip > Disconnect.

## View and edit clip properties

### View and edit properties for videos placed on the timeline.

When editing video in Firefly video editor, selecting a timeline clip shows properties you can review and change, including size, position, speed, opacity, and orientation. These values determine the clip's appearance and behavior in the project. The Properties panel updates for the selected clip type, making it straightforward to tune videos for the intended creative result.

### Before you begin

Start a new project, then place a clip on the timeline.

In the video editor page, choose the timeline video clip whose properties you want to change.

The Properties panel turns on and shows the video's duration, along with these adjustable properties:

- Flip horizontal: mirrors the clip from left to right.
- Flip vertical: turns the clip upside down from top to bottom.
- Rotate clockwise: rotates the clip 90° clockwise each time you click.
- Align: snaps the clip to the center or frame edges for accurate placement.
- Position: shifts the clip horizontally or vertically inside the frame.
- Scale: changes the clip size to be larger or smaller.
- Rotate: sets the clip to any angle.
- Opacity: changes the clip's transparency or opacity.
- Speed: controls video and audio playback speed with the slider.
- Volume: if the uploaded clip has audio, the slider control changes its volume.

Use the Properties panel to change clip settings such as position, size, opacity, speed, and more.

Use the Properties panel to change clip settings such as position, size, opacity, speed, and more.

Property changes appear in the preview as you make them.

Note

- At any time, select a clip in the timeline and choose the Properties icon to view its details.
- For audio clips, the Properties panel offers volume and speed adjustment sliders as the available settings.

## Add transitions to your videos

Learn how to apply transitions such as Dissolve and Fade to visual timeline items for smoother scene changes.

Transitions blend scenes or add dramatic pauses, helping videos look smoother and more professional. Firefly video editor (beta) provides simple transitions that can be applied directly to clips already on the timeline.

These effects work best after media clips are arranged in the timeline and you are ready to improve how scenes connect. Prerequisites for applying transitions:

- Adobe Firefly account that can use Firefly video editor (beta)
- Open project in Firefly video editor (beta)
- Video clips already placed on your timeline

### Add transitions to your clips

Go to Firefly video editor (beta) and open a project so its timeline is visible.

In the left rail, choose the Transitions icon.

The Transitions icon opens the panel for reviewing available transitions.

The Transitions icon opens the panel for reviewing available transitions.

In the timeline, select the visual track items that should receive the transition.

Tip

You can apply transitions to visual track items without selecting them first by dragging the desired transition onto the timeline.

From the Transitions panel, apply one of these transitions:

- Dissolve: blends two shots gradually as one clip changes into the next. This transition can be placed only near the end of video clips, and it cannot be used on the final clip in the timeline.

- Fade: lowers the clip's opacity until it is fully transparent, commonly for gentle scene transitions or subtle pauses. Apply it to the start of the first clip and/or the end of the last clip for an opening fade-in or ending fade-out.

Note

Dissolve applies only between two adjacent video clips on the main track in the timeline, not to other freeform tracks. Fade can also be used on freeform tracks and other visual track items, including text, images, or videos.

To change a transition's duration, select it in the timeline, right-click to show more options, and choose Edit duration. You can also select the transition in the timeline and drag its handles.

To remove an applied transition, select it in the timeline and press the Delete key. Another option is to select the transition, right-click to show more options, and choose Delete.

Expand the timeline so the transition is easier to see and select.

Expand the timeline so the transition is easier to see and select.

To replace a transition, choose the clip and pick a new Transition, or drag a Transition onto the clip you want to change.

Transitions on the main track can be replaced when the required conditions are satisfied. For example, a Dissolve between two video clips can be swapped for a Fade on each adjacent clip. Likewise, a Fade at the end of a clip can be swapped for a Dissolve between adjacent clips.

## Use built-in Adobe Stock panel

Learn how to search, filter, and download stock videos, images, and audio from the built-in Adobe Stock panel.

In Firefly video editor, the Adobe Stock panel speeds up the workflow by giving direct access to millions of stock assets without leaving the editing environment.

The Adobe Stock panel can supply videos, images, or audio tracks for projects. Search filters narrow results by technical details such as resolution, duration, and format. Downloaded assets appear automatically in the Your media panel, or on the timeline, based on where and how the Stock panel was opened.

### Search for Stock assets

Open the Adobe Stock panel from one of these places:

- On the Get started page, select Import media > Adobe Stock.
- Use the hamburger menu > Import > Adobe Stock.
- From the Your media or Timeline panel, choose Select files > Adobe Stock or Add > Adobe Stock.

Enter a keyword for the asset in the search bar, then pick Videos, Images, or Audio to set the kind of asset to return.

After the panel has been used for a search, a Recent Searches tab appears beside the avatar icon.

The panel also adds a Recent Searches tab next to the avatar icon after you run a search.

For additional technical filtering, choose the Filters icon.

- Exclude GenAI content: turns off AI-generated assets in the results.
- Only free content: removes paid assets from the result set.
- Resolution: filters video quality with All or Available in 4K.
- Duration: sets the shortest and longest clip lengths with the slider.
- Orientation: limits matches by project shape with Horizontal, Vertical, Panoramic, or Square.
- Frame Rate: narrows results to a frame rate such as 24, 30, or 60 fps that fits the timeline properties.

- Exclude GenAI content: removes AI-generated images from the image results.
- Only free content: hides paid items from the result set.
- Subcategory: filters by Photos, Illustrations, or Vectors for the asset category.
- People: uses All, Include people, or Exclude people according to whether people should appear.
- Orientation: limits the image shape to Horizontal, Vertical, Panoramic, or Square.
- Local artists only: refines contributor location with All or Local artists only.
- Background: helps compositing by returning Transparent or Isolated assets.
- Color: matches the visual style by choosing a dominant color.

- Exclude GenAI content: omits AI-generated audio from the results.
- Type: filters audio with All, Music, or Sound effects.
- Duration: defines the audio track length with the slider.
- Tempo: sets a BPM range that fits the pacing of the edit.
- Style: uses moods or genres to narrow results by tone and category.

For audio filters, multiple Moods and Genres can be selected together for tighter matching.

Selecting more than one Mood and Genre at the same time gives audio searches more precise results.

Choose Search to apply the filters, then review the matching assets and select the one you want. Results appear under the search bar as preview thumbnails. To view more information or preview an item, hover over a result.

Tip

- When downloading vector assets to the video editor, choose PNG or JPEG.
- Let video previews load completely before expecting the watermark to disappear.

In the results or collections, find the asset you want and choose the action that matches your Stock plan.

- Users with Stock plans: for licensed assets, choose the download icon; for unlicensed assets, choose the Download button. Enterprise accounts download immediately, while Individual plans show the credits that will be used.
- Users without Stock plans: use the Download button for free assets and Free trial for paid assets. Paid assets are marked with a crown icon; selecting one asks you to sign up for a plan.

Run another search when you need to find and download several assets in one session.

To collect more than one asset at the same time, search again and download the additional results.

On a free plan, choosing the Download button for a paid asset starts Stock plan signup.

Note

Available Stock credits can be checked from the account menu inside the Stock panel.

Downloaded assets are placed in the timeline or in the Your media panel based on the location used to open the Stock panel:

- Get started page: opens a new project and places the assets directly on the timeline.
- Timeline: places the assets on the timeline and also in the Your media panel.
- Your media panel: downloads the assets into the Your media panel so they can be added to the timeline later.
- Hamburger menu: places the assets directly on the timeline and in the Your media panel.

The Stock panel also supports browsing curated collections, so discovery does not require a search. Select a collection or category to inspect its assets, then use filters to tighten the result set.
