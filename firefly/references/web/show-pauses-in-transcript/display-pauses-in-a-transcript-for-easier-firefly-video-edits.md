# Display pauses in a transcript for easier Firefly video edits

Making pauses visible in the video transcript helps editors find natural speech breaks for trimming, rearranging, or other edits.

In Firefly video editor, visible transcript pauses show where music, text, visuals, or other video elements may fit. This saves time and can produce smoother, more natural pacing for a polished final video.

Before you begin:

Place a video file with dialogue on the timeline in a new project.

In the right panel, select the Text-based editing icon.

Choose Show pauses from the icon menu.

Turn on Show pauses so every pause is emphasized in the generated transcript.

Show pauses highlights all pauses in the generated transcript.

Once Show pauses is enabled, pauses appear as continuous dots and behave like transcript text.

Within the transcript, continuous dots mark pauses and can be selected to open editing options.

Continuous-dot pauses in the transcript can be selected like text to access edit controls.
