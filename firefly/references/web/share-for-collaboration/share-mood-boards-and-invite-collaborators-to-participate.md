# Share mood boards and invite collaborators to participate.

Sharing a mood board in Firefly Boards helps collaborators align on the project vision early in the creative process. It brings in different ideas and viewpoints, supports feedback directly on the canvas, refines the visual result, and may raise the board's quality overall.

Preparation

Place the needed visuals on the artboard, then arrange them according to your creative vision.

When the mood board is ready, choose Share from the menu bar.

The menu bar's Share option starts the collaboration-sharing flow for the mood board.

In the Share file dialog box, add collaborator email addresses with the Add people or groups field.

Enter one or more email addresses to invite collaborators to contribute to the mood board.

Set whether collaborators can edit or comment with the dropdown menu.

Choose whether an invited collaborator has comment-only access or can also edit the mood board.

Add an invitation message and choose Invite to edit. The invited collaborator receives an invite with a link to the mood boarding project.

You can also define who has file access and use Copy link to send a direct project link.

Copy the mood board link, send it directly to collaborators, and begin working together.

Invited collaborators receive an email link for the mood board, plus a Creative Cloud app notification.

Tip

Expand Who has access to see the collaborator list and each collaborator's permissions.
