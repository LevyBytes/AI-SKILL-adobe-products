# Generate Firefly Boards images with Custom Models.

Firefly Custom Models let you train with your own images and create variations that fit your brand. In Boards, you can use Custom Models with Adobe and partner models when they are available in your account.

In the left panel on the Firefly homepage, choose Ideate.

Select Create a board, then use New board on the Boards page.

At the bottom of the page, select Generate image.

Enter a text description in the prompt bar, then use Models to open the model list. From that list, select Custom Models.

Besides Firefly and partner models, choose Custom Models to generate images with your trained model.

Custom Models can be selected with Firefly and partner models when generating images from your own trained model.

Adjust the image generation settings, such as aspect ratio and content type, then select Generate.

Drag the generated image onto the canvas. To select and move several images at once, hold Shift while you drag and drop them onto the canvas.
