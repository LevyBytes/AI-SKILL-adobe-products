# Share your project with Adobe support

Use these steps to share your project:

Open Projects on Adobe home.

Find the project you want to share.

Select the more options icon, then choose Share.

Select Share

Select Share

In Add people or groups, type the email address supplied by Adobe Support.

One project can include a single collaborator or multiple collaborators.

A project may be shared with one collaborator or with several collaborators.

Press Enter or Tab to confirm the email.

Select Invite to project.

Collaborator access can also be set to Can edit and Can comment.

You can also assign collaborator access as Can edit and Can comment.
