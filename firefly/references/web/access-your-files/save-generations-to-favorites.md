# Save generations to Favorites

Keep preferred generated images, videos, and audios in Adobe Firefly as Favorites so the content you like most stays easy to find and manage.

In Firefly, any content generation can be marked as a favorite. After you favorite an item, Firefly places it in the dedicated Favorites section under Your stuff, which makes retrieval simpler. This helps when you have produced several variations and need a reliable way to track the options you prefer.
