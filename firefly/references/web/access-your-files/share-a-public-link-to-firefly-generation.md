# Share a public link to Firefly generation

Use these steps to make shareable links for Firefly-generated content.

Public links let you send Firefly generations to collaborators, clients, team members, or anyone else. Anyone who has the URL can view the Firefly generation and its related details, including prompts and reference materials.

Recipients need to sign in with an Adobe account before viewing the shared generation. A free Adobe account is enough to access shared content; a paid subscription is not required.
