# Create and share a public link

Open the Adobe Firefly homepage and choose Your stuff.

Choose Generation history to display the generations you have saved.

Right-click the generation to share, or choose the three-dots control to open the context menu.

Choose Share public link.

Use Share public link to copy the link to the clipboard automatically.

The public-link command places the copied link on the clipboard.

Wait until the confirmation message appears before sharing the copied URL.

Note

After a generation is shared publicly, its link continues to work until the original asset is deleted. Delete the original generation from your history to revoke access through that public link.
