# Access your generation history

On the Firefly homepage, choose Your Stuff from the left panel.

Open the Generation history tab.

Generation history lists the content you have generated previously.

That tab is where previous generated content is collected.

Use the dropdown menu to filter the view to images, videos, audio, or all generated content.

Hover over any image, audio, or video in generation history.

Open the context menu by right-clicking or choosing the three dots.

Choose from these options:

- Open: opens the selected generation in the editor view for editing.
- Favorite: adds the item to Favorites for quick access.
- Download: saves the selected generation output to your local device.
- Add to: puts the generation in a specific project, or saves it to a folder in Your files.
- Open in boards: sends the selected generation to a new or existing Firefly board.
- Share public link: makes a public link for sharing the generation with anyone.
- Delete permanently: removes the item from generation history.

Video generations also include Open in video editor (beta).

For video generations, Open in video editor (beta) is also shown.

For images, additional options appear:

- Open in Photoshop web: creates a new Photoshop on the web file from the selected image.
- Open in Adobe Express: creates a new Adobe Express file from the selected image.

Note

Generation history is stored in Adobe Cloud storage; like other cloud-saved files, it uses your allocated storage space.
