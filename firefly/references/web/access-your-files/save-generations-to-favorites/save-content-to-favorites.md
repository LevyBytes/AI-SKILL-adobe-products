# Save content to Favorites

Open Your stuff > Generation history, then find the generation that should become a favorite. You can also choose one of the generation results directly.

Hover over the content and choose the star icon, or choose the three dots to display the menu.

Save the file as a Favorite for quick access later.

Favorites keeps the file easier to locate again.

In the menu that appears, choose Favorite.

Allow the confirmation message to appear, confirming that the content has been saved to favorites.

Tip

To open saved favorites, use Your stuff > Favorites.

Note

Images produced with Custom models and Object composites are not automatically saved in the Generation history tab. Adobe plans to add this enterprise-feature capability in a future release.
