# Partner models in Adobe products

Use this topic to understand partner models in Adobe products and review common questions about them.

Note

Adobe does not build partner models. For each project, decide whether a specific model fits your needs, and review information about models from Adobe and its partners.

The newest update adds partner models to Generate images, Generate video, Firefly video editor, Boards, and Firefly Graph in Adobe Firefly. Partner models are also introduced for Adobe Express, Adobe Illustrator, and Adobe Photoshop.
