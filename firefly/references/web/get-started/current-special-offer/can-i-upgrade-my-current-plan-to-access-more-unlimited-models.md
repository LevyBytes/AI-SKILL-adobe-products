# Can I upgrade my current plan to access more unlimited models?

Yes. During the first-year term, upgrading is available at any point if you want additional models covered by the special offer. The promotional end date stays the same after an upgrade. For example, Firefly Pro Plus adds Nano Banana 2 (1K/2K) and Kling 2.5 Turbo. Firefly Premium adds Veo 3.1 Fast and Nano Banana Pro at 4K resolution.
