# Save to Favorites from generation history

Even when an image or video was not favorited while it was being created, generation history still lets you locate it and add it to Favorites afterward.

From the Adobe Firefly homepage, choose Files.

Open the Generation history tab, then narrow the list to images or videos with the dropdown menu.

After finding the image or video you want to keep, choose the icon or open the More menu and choose Favorite.

The selected image or video can be favorited from its context menu.

Use that item's context menu when you want to add it to Favorites.

When the confirmation message says the image or video has been saved to favorites, open the Favorites tab to see it.

Favorites lists every image and video you have marked as a favorite.

Favorites lists every image and video you have marked as a favorite. Note

To take an item out of Favorites, locate that image or video, then choose the Remove favorite icon, or open the More menu and choose Remove favorite.
