# Generate sound effects using text prompts

Generate original sound effects from text descriptions with Text to sound effects.

Text to sound effects creates sound effects from straightforward text descriptions. In the Firefly web app on desktop and mobile browsers, it can speed up work for videos, social posts, and other projects that need generated audio effects.

Watch Text to sound effects in action to see sound effect generation from simple text descriptions.

You can create sound effects or upload video and audio files, then layer additional sounds for a richer audio-visual composition. The feature produces sound effects and ambient sounds, but it is not for music or speech creation.

Tip

Your own voice can also guide sound effects generation.
