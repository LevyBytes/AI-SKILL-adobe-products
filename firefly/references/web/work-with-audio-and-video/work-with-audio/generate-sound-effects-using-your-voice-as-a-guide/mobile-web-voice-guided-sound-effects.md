# Generate sound effects using your voice with the Firefly mobile web app

Open the Firefly website in any supported browser.

Choose the Audio module, then tap Voice to sound effects.

On Generate SFX, use Upload to add the video or audio clip to the timeline where sound effects will be applied.

Use Upload to add a video to the timeline before generating a sound effect.

Move through the timeline and set the playhead at the exact place where the sound effect should be generated and inserted.

Tap Generate sound effects, then choose Add timing from the available options.

Use Add timing to record or upload an audio clip that guides sound-effect generation for the video.

In the prompt bar, describe the sound effect you want, then choose one of these options:

- From Device: upload an audio clip containing a voice recording for sound effect generation.
- Record Timing: record your voice directly for sound effect generation.

Use Record Timing to record your voice directly so the generated sound effect follows the recording's timing.

After recording the voice, play it for review, record it again, or select Generate to begin sound-effect generation.

Review the recorded voice clip, then either re-record it or generate the sound effects.

Preview the generated variations, select the one you want, and tap Add sound effects.

Review the generated variations and add one generated sound effect to the timeline.

To create another sound effect on the same audio track, move the playhead and generate it from the prompt bar. For layered audio, select the icon to add a new track, then generate a new sound effect on that track.

Preview the finished composition, then tap the menu icon to reach the download option.

Download the composed video clip with Download.
