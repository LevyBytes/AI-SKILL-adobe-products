# Add transitions to your clips

Go to Firefly video editor (beta) and open a project so its timeline is visible.

In the left rail, choose the Transitions icon.

The Transitions icon opens the panel for reviewing available transitions.

In the timeline, select the visual track items that should receive the transition.

Tip

You can apply transitions to visual track items without selecting them first by dragging the desired transition onto the timeline.

From the Transitions panel, apply one of these transitions:

- Dissolve: blends two shots gradually as one clip changes into the next. This transition can be placed only near the end of video clips, and it cannot be used on the final clip in the timeline.

- Fade: lowers the clip's opacity until it is fully transparent, commonly for gentle scene transitions or subtle pauses. Apply it to the start of the first clip and/or the end of the last clip for an opening fade-in or ending fade-out.

Note

Dissolve applies only between two adjacent video clips on the main track in the timeline, not to other freeform tracks. Fade can also be used on freeform tracks and other visual track items, including text, images, or videos.

To change a transition's duration, select it in the timeline, right-click to show more options, and choose Edit duration. You can also select the transition in the timeline and drag its handles.

To remove an applied transition, select it in the timeline and press the Delete key. Another option is to select the transition, right-click to show more options, and choose Delete.

Expand the timeline so the transition is easier to see and select.

To replace a transition, choose the clip and pick a new Transition, or drag a Transition onto the clip you want to change.

Transitions on the main track can be replaced when the required conditions are satisfied. For example, a Dissolve between two video clips can be swapped for a Fade on each adjacent clip. Likewise, a Fade at the end of a clip can be swapped for a Dissolve between adjacent clips.
