# Text-based editing panel

The Text-based editing panel is for working with the transcript of the video clip currently selected in the timeline. Through that transcript, you can move around the timeline and edit text while building and refining the video.

To open it, first choose a speech-containing video clip. Then use the right panel's Text-based editing icon. Firefly video editor transcribes the audio automatically, and the transcript appears in this panel so the video can be edited through text.

Use the Text-Based Editing panel to revise video content from the generated transcript.
