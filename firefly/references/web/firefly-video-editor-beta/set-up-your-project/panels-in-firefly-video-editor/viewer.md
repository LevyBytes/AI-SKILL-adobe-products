# Viewer

The Viewer sits in the center of the editor and shows timeline content while you edit. It gives you a live preview of the video so you can judge how clips, edits, and effects combine. The Viewer also supports direct interaction with visuals for precise changes, including edits to text overlays. With this panel, you can:

- Zoom in or out to inspect details or see the full frame
- Pan around the frame with the hand tool at the top-left corner of the timeline
- Refine particular clip areas for more precise visual edits

The view box on the bottom right of the viewer can also be used to pan through the generated video or adjust its crop.

Use the view box on the viewer's bottom right to move through the generated video or change the crop.
