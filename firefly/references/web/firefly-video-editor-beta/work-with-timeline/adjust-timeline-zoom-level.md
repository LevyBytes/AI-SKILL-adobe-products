# Adjust timeline zoom level

Zoom the timeline in or out during video editing to gain precision and work more efficiently.

Timeline zoom gives more control while editing. Zooming in supports precise work, such as trimming clips to exact frames for cleaner transitions or synchronizing text layers. Zooming out reveals the full project, which helps with organizing the video's flow and structure. Moving between zoom levels lets you switch easily between detailed edits and the broader project view.

Preparation:

Create a new project, then put media files on the timeline.

Move the zoom slider handle toward the Zoom out icon to show more of the timeline. This broader view helps you see the full project and all of its layers.
