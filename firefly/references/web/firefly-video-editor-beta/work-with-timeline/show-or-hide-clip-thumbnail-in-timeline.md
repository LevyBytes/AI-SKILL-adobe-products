# Show or hide clip thumbnail in timeline

Show and hide thumbnails for media that has been added to the editor's timeline.

Timeline thumbnails in Firefly video editor give a fast visual reference when working with one or more clips. Instead of depending on filenames or playing each video, thumbnails let scenes be identified quickly, which makes editing smoother and easier to understand.

Thumbnails also help during trimming, cutting, and clip arrangement because scene and frame details remain visible. That visibility improves accuracy when matching clips, syncing audio, or positioning text at the correct moment.

Turn on visual previews for clips to make editing feel smoother and more intuitive.

Clip visual previews can be enabled to make the editing process easier and more fluid.

Thumbnails is enabled by default. To hide thumbnails or turn them back on, follow these steps:

Upload the media files, then place them on the timeline.

Use the icon in the screen's upper-left corner to open the menu. Then choose Timeline and select Thumbnails to enable clip visual previews.

In the Timeline menu, the Thumbnails option toggles clip previews on or off.

Selecting Thumbnails from the Timeline menu shows or hides clip previews.
