# Credit Usage

Review how credits behave while you work with Firefly AI Assistant.

No. While the beta is active, entitled users can create in Firefly AI Assistant without drawing down the credits they already have.

During the beta, entitled users receive free daily credits reserved only for Firefly AI Assistant. After the daily allotment is exhausted, the count resets at midnight GMT.

This daily allowance does not change the credit balance you have outside AI Assistant. Credits included with a paid plan are not transferable into the AI Assistant, and the complimentary AI Assistant credits can not be spent in other places.

After you use the full daily amount, generation is unavailable again until the credits reset at midnight GMT.
