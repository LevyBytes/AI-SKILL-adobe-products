# Understand generative credits and how they function.

Generative AI refers to artificial intelligence that can turn simple text prompts into creative output. In Adobe apps, Adobe Firefly generative AI technology is intended for play, experimentation, dreaming, and making extraordinary work.

Early coverage centered on images, text effects, and vectors:

- Adobe Photoshop: Generative Fill and Generative Expand
- Adobe Firefly: Generate Image
- Adobe Illustrator: Generative Recolor
- Adobe Express: Generate Text Effects
- and more

Firefly-powered generative AI now also reaches 3D, animation, and video through Adobe Firefly features such as Generate Video and Translate and Lip Sync.

Plans for Creative Cloud provide a monthly generative credit allocation. Those credits cover generative AI content creation across features in Creative Cloud, Adobe Express, and Adobe Substance 3D applications. Credit allotments differ by plan, and usage varies with both the generative AI feature selected and the type of subscription.

See details for Generative credits and the amounts included with specific plans.

You can also buy credits through the Adobe Firefly Standard and Firefly Pro plans.
