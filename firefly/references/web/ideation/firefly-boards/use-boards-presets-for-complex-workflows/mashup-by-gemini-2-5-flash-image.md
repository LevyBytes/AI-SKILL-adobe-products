# Mashup by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

When several images are on the canvas and the goal is one new image that draws from all of their visual elements, use the Mashup preset.

Upload or add the images to be mashed up directly on the canvas.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Mashup.

Select Image 1 to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the desired canvas image. Repeat for each additional image field so every field has the matching image assigned.

Choose canvas images for the Mashup by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

When the assignments are complete, choose Generate.

Image generated with the Mashup by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
