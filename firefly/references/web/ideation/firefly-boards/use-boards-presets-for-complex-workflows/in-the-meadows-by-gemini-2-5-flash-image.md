# In the meadows by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Upload the image containing the subject you want to set in the meadows.

At the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose In the meadows.

Select the Subject field to enable the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.

To provide a style reference for generation, select the Style field to turn on the eyedropper tool, then choose the reference image on the canvas.
