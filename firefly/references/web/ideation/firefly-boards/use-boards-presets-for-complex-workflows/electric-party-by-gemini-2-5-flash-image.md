# Electric party by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

The Electric party preset stylizes an image with iridescent clothing and bright reflections, giving the result a futuristic, dramatic appearance through shifting color and light.

Upload or add the image that should receive the effect to the canvas.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Electric party.

Select Subject to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the subject image on the canvas.

Choose a canvas image for the Electric party by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Electric party by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
