# Add to scene by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use the Add to scene preset with both scene and subject images to place a product inside the selected scene image.

Upload the product image and the scene image you want to use to the canvas.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Add to scene.

Use the Scene field to turn on the eyedropper tool, then choose the canvas image you want as the product backdrop.

Select the Product field, then choose the product image on the canvas.
