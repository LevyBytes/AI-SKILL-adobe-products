# Change perspective by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use the Change perspective preset to alter the viewing angle of an existing image so it appears from a new viewpoint.

Upload the image whose perspective you want to adjust onto the canvas.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Change presepetive.

Select the Image field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
