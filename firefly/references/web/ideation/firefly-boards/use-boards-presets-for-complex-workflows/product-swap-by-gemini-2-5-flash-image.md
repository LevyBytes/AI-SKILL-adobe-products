# Product swap by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use Product swap when specific items in an image need to be updated or replaced, such as changing a product, object, or character while preserving the scene's realism and overall coherence.

Add an image of the scene, plus an image of the product you want to place in that scene.

At the bottom of the Boards page, choose Presets, then choose Product swap from the Image tab.

Select Scene to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the canvas image containing the scene.

Select Product, then choose the canvas image containing the product.

Choose canvas images for the Product swap by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Product swap by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
