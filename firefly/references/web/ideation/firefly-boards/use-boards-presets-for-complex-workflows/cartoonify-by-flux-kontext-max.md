# Cartoonify by FLUX Kontext Max

Upload the subject image that should become a cartoon-style image.

At the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Cartoonify from the Image tab.

Select the Image field to turn on the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
