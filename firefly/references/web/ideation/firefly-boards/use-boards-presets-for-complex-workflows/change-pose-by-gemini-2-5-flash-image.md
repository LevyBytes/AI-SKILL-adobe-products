# Change pose by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

For an image whose subject needs a different pose, use the Change pose preset and provide a short text description of the posture you want.

Upload or add images of the subject whose pose will be changed.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Change pose.

Select Image to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the canvas image containing the subject.

Describe in text the pose the subject should take.

Choose a canvas image for the Change pose by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Change pose by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
