# Candid by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use the Candid preset to make an image feel natural and everyday, like an unposed moment captured quickly.

Upload an image to the canvas and change its style into a candid shot.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Candid.

Select the Subject field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
