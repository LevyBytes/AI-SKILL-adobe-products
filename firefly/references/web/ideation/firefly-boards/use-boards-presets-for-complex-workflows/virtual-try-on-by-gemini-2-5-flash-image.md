# Virtual try-on by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Different styles or outfits can be previewed on a character quickly, making it easier to compare looks and explore alternatives without rebuilding the image.

Upload or add the subject image and clothing image you want to use onto the canvas.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Virtual try-on.

Select Subject to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the subject image on the canvas.

Select Clothing, then choose the clothing image from the canvas.

Choose canvas images for the Virtual try-on by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Virtual try-on by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
