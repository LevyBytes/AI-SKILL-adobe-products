# Street style by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use the Style option to produce an urban fashion version of an image, with a city setting and bold street attitude.

Upload an image of the subject that should receive a strong street-style aesthetic.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Street style.

Select the Subject field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
