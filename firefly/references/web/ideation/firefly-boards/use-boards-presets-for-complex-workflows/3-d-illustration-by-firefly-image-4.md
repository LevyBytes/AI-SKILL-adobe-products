# 3D Illustration by Firefly Image 4

Use the 3D Illustration preset to change an image into stylized artwork with a three-dimensional look.

Upload the image that you want to turn into a stylized three-dimensional illustration.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose 3D illustration.

Select the Image field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.

Pick an aspect ratio from the options shown.
