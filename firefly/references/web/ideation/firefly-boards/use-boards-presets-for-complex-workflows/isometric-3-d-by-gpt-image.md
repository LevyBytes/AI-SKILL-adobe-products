# Isometric 3D by GPT Image

Use Isometric 3D to turn an image into an isometric 3D scene. Depth, perspective, and angular forms emphasize the design elements.

Upload the image you want converted to an isometric style.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Isometric 3D.

Select Image to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the image from the canvas.

Choose a canvas image for the Isometric 3D by GPT Image preset.

Pick one of the available aspect ratios.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Isometric 3D by GPT Image preset.
