# Add images

Add images to a board by importing files, using stock assets, or generating images with generative AI models.

Start ideation and moodboarding by collecting visuals that match your theme and creative direction. Photographs, illustrations, color swatches, textures, and patterns help communicate the overall aesthetic and mood. Firefly Boards supports importing your own images, adding stock assets, and creating images with generative AI models, including Firefly and partner models.

From the Firefly homepage, choose Ideate in the left panel.

When images are ready to add, choose Upload files; to begin without existing files, choose Create a board.

On the Boards page, choose New board.

Bring an image onto the canvas through the Import option.

To add images from Adobe Stock, select the icon in the right toolbar, then browse with the Search panel.

Open the Search panel with Search to browse stock images on Adobe Stock.

For text-prompt image creation, choose Generate image at the bottom of the page and place the result on the canvas.

Enter a description in the prompt bar, then choose among available Firefly, Custom Models, and partner models.

You can generate images with a range of Firefly models and partner models, then add them to the canvas.

Choose Aspect ratio to pick from the available image dimensions.

When a canvas image should be used as a reference, choose the Sample from canvas icon and use the available options.

Choose a canvas sample as a reference for style and composition, or use extracted sub-prompts.

Before generating, open Generation settings if you need to revise reference images, sub-prompts, or other generation choices.

Open Generation settings for fast access to image generation options when creating new variations.

Choose Generate to receive image variations in the film strip.

Drag a generated image onto the canvas. To choose and move several images, hold Shift while dragging and dropping them on the canvas. You can also use Place on canvas.

Place selected images by dragging them to the canvas, or use Place on Canvas.

Tip

- After multiple images are selected on the canvas, Remix can combine parts of those images with generative AI models to create distinctive board visuals.
- Select an image, open the More menu, and choose Find similar inspiration to browse related Adobe Stock images.
- Open the More menu after selecting one or more images or videos, then choose Open in Firefly video editor (beta) to start a Firefly video editor (beta) project. The chosen media is added automatically to the timeline in selection order.
