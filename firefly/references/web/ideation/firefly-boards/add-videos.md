# Add videos

Import videos, or create and add videos to a Firefly Boards canvas with generative AI video models, including models from outside Adobe.

Build out a mood board with videos that fit the intended theme and visual direction. Clips, animations, and motion graphics can establish tone and style. In Boards, you can import your own videos, use stock footage, or generate new videos with generative AI models from Firefly and non-Adobe providers.

On the Firefly homepage, choose Boards.

On the Boards page, choose Create new board.

To add a video file from your device, choose the Import option in the left panel and place it on the canvas.

For Adobe Stock videos, choose the icon in the right toolbar, then use the Search panel to find, browse, and add videos to the canvas.

To create videos from a text prompt and place them on the canvas, choose Generate video at the bottom of the page.

From the list of Firefly and partner models, pick the video model to use for generation.

You can choose either Adobe or a partner model to create a video and add it to the board.

Adobe and partner model choices are available for generating a video that can be placed on the board. Note

The selected video model determines which video generation settings are available. For details about generating videos with partner video models, see the related documentation.

If Firefly Video is selected as the video model and you have an image to use as the generated video's first frame, add it under First frame.

You can also provide a Last frame image along with First frame for video generation. When both are supplied, the two images act as fixed endpoints that guide the video to begin and finish with them.

When images are already on the canvas and one should be used as the first frame, last frame, or sub-prompt, choose Sample from canvas, then select the image with the eyedropper tool.

Use an existing canvas image as the first or last frame for video generation.

For video generation, select an image already on the canvas to serve as the first frame or last frame.

Choose the aspect ratio for the generated video: Landscape, Portrait, Square, or Widescreen.

For more controls, choose the Generation settings icon beside the Aspect ratio options. The right-side panel opens with these extra settings:

- Sub-prompts: Browse available sub-prompts, including lighting and style choices. Selecting a sub-prompt preview thumbnail adds that item to the prompt bar.

Browse sub-prompts and select the ones that should be added to the video-generation text prompt.

Choose sub-prompts to include in the text prompt used for video generation.

- Seed: Enter a seed number to initialize generation and influence how random the AI output is. Reusing the same seed, prompt, and control settings can produce similar video clips.

Write the desired description in the prompt bar.

The prompt bar is where you supply the description, select an image or video generation model, and choose the aspect ratio.

Use the prompt bar for the description, model selection, and aspect ratio settings.

Drag the generated video onto the canvas, or hover over the video and choose Place on canvas.

Choose Place on Canvas to put the generated video on the canvas.

Place on Canvas adds the generated video to the canvas.

After the video is on the canvas, hover over it to show controls for play, download, rearrange, flip, duplicate, and common actions such as copy and paste.

Use the editing options to position and organize the generated video on the canvas.

Arrange the generated video on the canvas with the available editing controls. Note

After the video is on the canvas, select it, open the More menu, and choose Open in Firefly video editor (beta) to open that video directly as a new project in the video editor. You may select more than one media file; the video editor adds the selected items to the timeline automatically, preserving the order in which you selected them.
