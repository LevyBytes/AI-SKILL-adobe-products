# Learn and generate simultaneously

Open Firefly Boards and scroll to Discover workflows for Boards.

Pick a guide that matches what you need, then select View workflow.

Select the Quick guide workflow that fits your specific requirement.

Choose the Quick guide workflow based on the result you need.

Select Open in Boards to show the guide on the canvas.

You can copy the guide link and share it somewhere else.

The guide link can also be copied for sharing outside Boards.

Study the guide by zooming in, reading its steps, and learning the workflow.

Find the read-only workflow banner, then select Remix.

Remix opens an editable version of the guide in a new canvas.

Selecting Remix creates an editable copy of the guide on a new canvas.

When the editable canvas appears, find the banner labelled Start here to try it out.

The guided steps make it easier to begin producing your own versions.

Use the guided steps as a simplified path for creating your own variations.

Follow the guide's steps to generate your iterations.

Tip

Quick Guides are learning workflows. Once you complete a guided workflow, you can reuse the techniques in your own projects without continuing to follow the structured version.
