# Add shapes to the canvas

Choose Shapes from the left panel of the canvas.

Pick one of the available shapes, then draw it on the canvas with the cursor.

Tip

Keep Shift pressed while drawing a shape to preserve its proportions, such as when making an exact circle or square.

Open the Properties panel with the Properties icon to change shape attributes such as color, opacity, and blend mode, plus rotation, position, size, and other settings.

Adjust the shape on the canvas with either its context menu or the Properties panel.

Selecting two or more shapes on the canvas exposes the same arrange, align, and editing actions used for images and videos, plus two additional options that apply specifically to shapes.
