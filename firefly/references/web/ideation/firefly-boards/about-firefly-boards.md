# About Firefly Boards

Use Firefly Boards for early idea exploration, moodboards, storyboards, and pitch materials.

Note

Anyone with an Adobe account can open Firefly Boards. Users who have premium feature access can create unlimited boards, while other plans are capped at 3 boards.

Generative AI features, including image generation, background removal, generative fill or expand, and video generation, require generative credits. Premium features, such as using partner models to generate images and videos, require a Creative Cloud subscription with premium feature access.
