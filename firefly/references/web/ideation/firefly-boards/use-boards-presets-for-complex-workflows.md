# Use Boards Presets for complex workflows

Review the available Boards presets for transforming images and generating new visuals that fit your creative direction.

When images are on the canvas, Firefly Boards provides a quick way to use prepared presets and specialized workflows for creating or transforming visuals around your creative goals. The preset set covers tasks such as colorizing images, placing products into scenes, building compositions from multiple elements, and applying stylized treatments. Using these workflows reduces the manual setup normally needed for ideation and moodboarding.
