# How guided workflows help you

Each guide turns broad AI capabilities into repeatable, concrete workflows. Every guide includes clear directions, the reason behind each step, and a way to create iterations. Quick Guides help by:

- Supplying tested workflows for common creative work, for example placing a logo on a product or altering a character's expression.
- Moving you smoothly from the initial idea, through inspiration, and into the final output.
- Building practical AI skills that you can apply directly to real creative projects.

Any guide can be browsed, copied, and remixed to match what you need.
