# Add and edit shapes

Add shapes to an artboard, then adjust their properties and appearance to support the mood board.

Shapes can strengthen a mood board visually and help call attention to important ideas or themes. They can also organize content into a structured layout and give the board a unified visual direction.

When a shape is selected on the canvas, Boards shows a context menu and Properties panel. Those controls let you adjust the shape's properties and appearance for the mood board.

Choose Shape from the left toolbar; the available shapes are Rectangle, Ellipse, Line, and Arrow.

Refine and customize canvas shapes with the shape editing controls and Properties panel.

Draw the shape with the cursor, then change its properties and arrangement on the canvas.

Open the Properties panel with the Properties icon to change shape attributes such as color, opacity, and blend mode, plus rotation, position, size, and other settings.

Selecting the shape expands its properties panel for adjustment.
