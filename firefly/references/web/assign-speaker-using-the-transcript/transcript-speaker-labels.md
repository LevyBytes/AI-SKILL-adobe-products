# Identify speakers and assign labels from the generated transcript

When the transcript clearly names speakers, it becomes simpler to find quotes or sections from a given person. That supports accurate cuts, rearrangements, and highlights without changing or removing the wrong speaker's words.

With Text-based editing in Firefly video editor, transcript generation includes speaker labels based on detected voices. Firefly assigns speakers automatically, and you can update or reassign labels when needed.

Before you begin:

Place a video file with dialogue on the timeline in a new project.

In the right panel, select the Text-based editing icon.

Each speaker is labeled in the generated transcript, with spoken dialogue synchronized to the matching text.

If transcript text needs a new speaker or an assignment correction, highlight the text and select the Assign speaker icon.

Select part of the transcript and choose Assign speaker to move it to another speaker or create a new speaker.

Use Assign speaker on the highlighted transcript portion to reassign the speaker or add a new one.

Speaker labels make speaker-specific video segments easier to find and remove directly from the timeline.
