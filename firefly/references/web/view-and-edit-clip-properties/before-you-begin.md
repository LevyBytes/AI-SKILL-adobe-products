# Before you begin

Start a new project, then place a clip on the timeline.

In the video editor page, choose the timeline video clip whose properties you want to change.

The Properties panel turns on and shows the video's duration, along with these adjustable properties:

- Flip horizontal: mirrors the clip from left to right.
- Flip vertical: turns the clip upside down from top to bottom.
- Rotate clockwise: rotates the clip 90° clockwise each time you click.
- Align: snaps the clip to the center or frame edges for accurate placement.
- Position: shifts the clip horizontally or vertically inside the frame.
- Scale: changes the clip size to be larger or smaller.
- Rotate: sets the clip to any angle.
- Opacity: changes the clip's transparency or opacity.
- Speed: controls video and audio playback speed with the slider.
- Volume: if the uploaded clip has audio, the slider control changes its volume.

Use the Properties panel to change clip settings such as position, size, opacity, speed, and more.

Property changes appear in the preview as you make them.

Note

- At any time, select a clip in the timeline and choose the Properties icon to view its details.
- For audio clips, the Properties panel offers volume and speed adjustment sliders as the available settings.
