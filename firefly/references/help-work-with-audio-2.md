## Generate soundtrack (beta)

### Generate original soundtracks by setting vibe, style, purpose, energy, and tempo.

Generate soundtrack (beta) creates soundtracks from selected creative inputs, including vibe, style, purpose, energy, and tempo. These inputs guide soundtrack composition toward particular moods, genres, and use cases, such as background music, game soundtracks, podcast audio, or meditation soundscapes.

When creating soundtracks, you can upload a video and get a suggested prompt to customize, or start from scratch with a custom prompt that does not include a video.

On the Firefly homepage, select Generate from the left panel, then open the Audio tab and choose Generate soundtrack.

Note

On Generate soundtrack, the left panel shows a sample prompt whose colored text represents different soundtrack generation attributes.

Compose the prompt by adding or removing the preloaded tags for each attribute.

Tip

To remove every preloaded tag, select Clear Prompt.

Add tags to the prompt from these available attributes.

- Vibe: shown as purple text in the prompt. Use it to set the overall mood or emotional tone for soundtrack generation.

- Style: shown as blue text in the prompt. Use it to set the genre or instrumentation for soundtrack generation.

- Purpose: shown as green text in the prompt. Use it to set the intended use case or context for soundtrack generation.

For custom tags in any attribute, use descriptive adjectives.

Choose a specific style so the generated soundtrack better matches the intended use case.

Choose a specific style so the generated soundtrack better matches the intended use case.

Set the soundtrack energy by choosing one of these options:

- Low: gentle and calm
- Medium: even and steady
- High: lively and fast

Choose an energy level that matches the soundtrack to the emotional tone of the use case.

Choose an energy level that matches the soundtrack to the emotional tone of the use case.

Set tempo to control the soundtrack's speed or pace for generation. These options are available:

- Slow: calm at a low pace
- Medium: moderate, steady, and balanced
- Fast: brisk and energetic

Use Tempo to control the pacing and rhythm of the generated music.

Use Tempo to control the pacing and rhythm of the generated music.

Change the generated soundtrack length in the Duration section.

Select Generate. Four soundtrack variations are returned.

Preview the soundtrack variations, highlight the one to download, and select Download.

The soundtrack downloads in WAV format.

Tip

If the soundtrack does not match expectations, conflicting input terms may be the reason. For example, using a calm Vibe with a heavy metal Style can produce unpredictable results and may favor one input over the other.

Adjust Vibe so it better matches the desired mood, then refine Style so it complements that mood, and avoid combining terms with opposite emotional tones or musical genres.

## Generate speech from text

Use text prompts in Firefly to generate audio clips with different voices, tones, and accents.

Generate Speech (beta) creates natural-sounding audio clips and voiceovers. Controls such as accent, language, speed, and pitch let you tailor voice characteristics to your needs.

On the Firefly homepage, select Generate in the left panel, then choose Generate speech.

On Generate speech, paste the text to turn into speech, or choose Import text from a file and upload a DOCX or TXT file.

After the text is added, open the Speech settings panel on the left and use Model to choose Firefly Speech.

Tip

A partner model, such as ElevenLabs Multilingual v2, can also generate speech from text.

Use the Voice dropdown menu to choose a voice. Under Voice, adjust accent/language, pitch, and speed to give the voice a distinct style.

Choose the voice that best fits your project's requirements and creative goals.

Choose the voice that best fits your project's requirements and creative goals. Note

The voice list loads only after you sign in to your Firefly account.

Use the Choose a language dropdown to select a language and delivery accent from the language list, such as English (US) and English (India).

Adjust accent, speed, and pitch to customize the selected voice.

A. Choose a language B. Speed C. Pitch

Adjust accent, speed, and pitch to customize the selected voice. To change Speed and Pitch for generated speech, adjust these speech settings:

- Speed: drag the speed bar right to make spoken audio faster, or left to make it slower.
- Pitch: drag the pitch bar right to raise the spoken audio pitch, or left to lower it.

Tip

- At the bottom of the left panel, select the icon to play a sample of the voice with the selected speed, pitch, and accent.
- Select the icon to add the voice to favorites.

In the main text editor window, more changes can be made to the entered text:

- Play: previews selected text in the uploaded content before generation.

Use Play to quickly hear how the text sounds with the selected voice settings.

Use Play to quickly hear how the text sounds with the selected voice settings.

- Fix Pronunciation: corrects pronunciation and adds guidance for how specific words should sound.
- Find & Replace: selects words and replaces them.
- Add Tone: adds tonality and defines intonation for the generated speech.

To add more script text, insert a pause, or open writing tips, use the Generate bar and choose from these selections:

- Add Text: imports a TXT or DOCX file to add more text to the uploaded content.
- Add Pause: adds pauses so the audio track sounds more natural.
- Show Tips: opens writing tips for producing the best possible results.

## Fix pronunciation in the speech text

### Fix pronunciation by changing how selected words sound in generated speech.

In Firefly Generate Speech (beta), some words may not be pronounced the way you expect. Proper names, technical terms, and words with several pronunciations can require adjustment. Fix Pronunciation lets you change those word sounds so the speech matches your preference.

Before you begin:

Add speech text or upload a text file, then set the voice, accent, and other options.

In the text editor, find and highlight the words that require pronunciation adjustment.

From the context menu, select Fix Pronunciation.

Use Fix Pronunciation to control how selected words sound in generated speech.

Use Fix Pronunciation to control how selected words sound in generated speech.

In the text box, type the word phonetically, matching how it should sound. For example, type "uhn-fer-GET-uh-buhl" to pronounce "unforgettable" correctly.

For accurate output, make sure the phonetic text is correct.

For accurate output, make sure the phonetic text is correct.

Select the icon to play and preview the adjusted pronunciation.

If the word sounds right, select Fix to update only the selected instance.

To use the adjusted pronunciation for every occurrence of the word in the audio, select Fix all.

Tip

After fixing pronunciation, generate a new version and play the whole audio back to confirm the updated pronunciation is used in the rest of the generated speech.
