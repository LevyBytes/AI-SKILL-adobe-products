# Benefits & Best Practices - Benefits of Listing in Our Marketplace

1.  Through the Creative Cloud desktop app or the Adobe Exchange webpage, millions of Creative Cloud users can reach your plugin(s) directly.

1.  Installation is handled by the Creative Cloud desktop app, so users get a simple one-click install flow.

1.  Because acquisition is associated with an AdobeID, users can reinstall the plugin on another machine or different hardware more easily.

1.  Plugin update notices are sent automatically to users, and applying those updates is straightforward.

1.  Developer profiles give users a single page where they can find all of your plugins.

1.  Built-in monetization is available, with Adobe using FastSpring to handle payments.

1.  Marketplace plugins go through a formal review process, giving users confidence that listed plugins are secure and usable.

1.  You may promote the plugin with official "Designed for"-Badges.

1.  Installation-related help is available to users through Additional Support.

1.  A free Creative Cloud subscription is provided to developers. (For a free seat please contact us at <ccintrev@adobe.com>).
