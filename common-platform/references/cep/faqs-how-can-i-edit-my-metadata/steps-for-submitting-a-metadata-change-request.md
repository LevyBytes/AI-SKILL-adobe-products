# Steps for submitting a Metadata Change Request:

1. Sign in to the [Exchange Developer Portal](https://partners.adobe.com/exchangeprogram/creativecloud) and open the [Dashboard.](https://partners.adobe.com/exchangeprogram/creativecloud/appslist.html)

2. In the "Published" tab, choose the listing that needs changes.

3. Select Edit.

4. Revise whichever tab needs changes: Details, Images, Upload & Docs, Notes & EULA.

5. Move down the page and click "Save & Next" (or back to switch between tabs).

6. After finishing the edits, click the "Save" button and preview the listing.

7. If the previewed changes are correct, click the "Submit" button.

8. A success message appears in the Exchange Portal after the changes are submitted. Submission email is sent from ccintegrationsreview@adobe.com and noreply@salesforce.com.
