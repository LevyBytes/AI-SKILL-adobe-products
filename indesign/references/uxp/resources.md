# Resources

At this point, you have likely tried a simple script or plugin by working through the getting-started guides.

These resources help you go deeper into UXP and build something more useful for real-world scenarios. Most topics apply to both scripts and plugins, though small differences can appear.

Begin with [fundamentals](fundamentals.md), then move to [recipes](recipes.md) for bite-size examples covering common use cases. After that, explore the APIs in the [References](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AdobeDocs/uxp-indesign/cba672dce97bcb5805baaa7558d83abbabd825f6/src/pages/changelog/index.md) section.
