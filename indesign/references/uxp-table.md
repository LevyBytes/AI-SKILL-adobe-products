> Mixed **contents**
>
> Provides cell contents as an array: item one fills the top left cell, item two fills the cell to its right, and the sequence continues across the first row before moving to the second row's left-most cell, and so on. Note: When the array supplies fewer strings than the table has cells, cells without supplied entries stay empty. Returns: Array of Strings or SpecialCharacters enumerators. Also accepts: Array of Strings, SpecialCharacters enumerators or NothingEnum enumerators.
