# Create and manage workspaces

Customize panel layouts in Adobe InDesign and move among saved arrangements to streamline your workflow.

Workspaces let you place windows, panels, and tools in InDesign to match your design habits and working style. A workspace saves the current panel positions and sizes under a name, making it easy to return to a preferred setup. InDesign provides several presets for specific tasks, and you can add as many custom workspaces as different projects or workflow stages require. Switching is available whenever needed, and changes to panels are saved automatically in the active workspace.
