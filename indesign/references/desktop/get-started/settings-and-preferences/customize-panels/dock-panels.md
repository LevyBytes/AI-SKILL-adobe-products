# Dock panels

Panels gathered into one vertical stack along a workspace edge make up a dock. Docking keeps related controls in the same place, while undocking lets a panel be used on its own. As you drag panels, InDesign marks valid destinations with blue-highlighted drop zones. Workspace changes remain in the active workspace until you reset it or choose another workspace.

Choose the panel for relocation by selecting its tab.

Move the panel tab toward the dock you want until a blue drop zone is shown.

Place the panel at the top, at the bottom, or between existing panels in that dock.

Let go of the pointer when the blue highlight marks the intended spot.
