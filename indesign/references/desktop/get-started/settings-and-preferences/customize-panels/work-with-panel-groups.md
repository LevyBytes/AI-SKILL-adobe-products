# Work with panel groups

Add a panel to a group by dragging the panel tab into the highlighted drop zone inside that group.

Rearrange panels in a group by moving a panel's tab to another position in the group.

Remove a panel from a group by dragging it outside the group by its tab.

Move a whole group by dragging the title bar above the tabs.

Add panels, remove unused ones, and reorganize individual or grouped panels so essential tools stay quick to reach while documents are open. These controls let you shape a workspace around your design needs and workflow preferences.
