# Undock panels

Press and hold the tab for the panel that should leave its dock.

Pull the panel away from the dock where it currently sits.

Move it into a blank area of the workspace and keep it out of any blue drop zones.

Release the pointer to turn it into a free-floating panel. When a dock has no panels left, it disappears. To build another dock, move either a panel or a panel group toward the workspace's right edge and stop when a drop zone is displayed. To move a panel group without splitting it, drag from the title bar instead of from a single tab.
