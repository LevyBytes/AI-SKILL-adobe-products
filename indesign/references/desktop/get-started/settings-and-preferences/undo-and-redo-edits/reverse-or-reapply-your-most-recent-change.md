# Reverse or reapply your most recent change

To reverse your last action, choose Edit > Undo or press Ctrl + Z (Windows) or Cmd + Z (macOS).

To apply again an action you just undid, use Edit > Redo; the keyboard shortcuts are Ctrl + Shift + Z (Windows) and Cmd + Shift + Z (macOS).

The menu names include the action involved, such as Undo Delete or Redo Move.

Note

Some operations cannot be undone, including scrolling the document view or saving files.
