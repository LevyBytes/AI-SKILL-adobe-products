# Adjust the position or shape of curved segments

With the Direct Selection tool, choose a curved segment, or choose an anchor point at either end of that curved segment. If direction lines exist, they appear.

To reposition the segment, drag it. Shift-drag limits the adjustment to 45° increments.

For shape changes around the selected anchor point, move either the anchor point or its direction point. Hold Shift while dragging to limit movement to 45° increments.
