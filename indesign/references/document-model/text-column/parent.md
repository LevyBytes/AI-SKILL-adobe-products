# parent

> _Read Only_
>
> Mixed **parent**
>
> Identifies the TextColumn parent, which may be one of these object types: XmlStory, TextPath, TextFrame, EndnoteTextFrame, Text, InsertionPoint, plus TextStyleRange, Paragraph, TextColumn, Line, Word, Character, and also Story, Cell, XMLElement, Endnote, Footnote, Note, Change, or finally HiddenText.
