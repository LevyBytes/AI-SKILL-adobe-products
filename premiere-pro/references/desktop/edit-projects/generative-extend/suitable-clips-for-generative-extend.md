# Suitable clips for Generative Extend

In Adobe's broad testing, many clip types have worked, including humans, animals, landscapes, and other subjects. Adobe recommends shots with limited contextual change. Its FAQ also cites fast-sweeping camera movement without explaining how that example satisfies the recommendation; evaluate the continuity of the generated result for the actual clip. Audio with steady noise or ambience also tends to perform well.

Current weaker cases include archival footage with heavy film grain or other noise. Adobe welcomes feedback to learn more.
