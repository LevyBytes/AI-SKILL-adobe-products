# Connecting to Team Projects service for cloud collaboration and editing

Before you begin

Review Team Projects and how it can support efficient collaboration.

To use Team Projects and turn on cloud-based collaboration for Premiere projects, make the connection to Team Project service.

Open Adobe Premiere or After Effects.

The compatible releases are October 2018 and later for Premiere, After Effects, and Adobe Media Encoder.

Sign in to Creative Cloud with your Apple ID, Adobe ID, Google, or Facebook account.

After sign-in succeeds, Team Projects can be used to create new collaborative projects, accept invitations, and begin cloud editing collaborations.
