# What is Media Replacement?

Media Replacement lets a Motion Graphics template accept your own assets in place of particular media elements. A static template becomes reusable across projects while the same design system and animated behavior stay intact.

With Media Replacement, you can:

- Use your own graphics or photos instead of placeholder images
- Put your own footage in place of background videos
- Refresh logos and branding elements across multiple projects
- Keep animations and effects consistent while the visual content changes
