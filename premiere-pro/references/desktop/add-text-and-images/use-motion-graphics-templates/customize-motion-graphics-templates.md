# Customize Motion Graphics templates

Adjust Motion Graphics template properties for your video-editing requirements in Adobe Premiere.

Open the Graphics Templates panel, then switch to the My Templates tab.

Choose the template you plan to use and drag it onto a video track in the sequence.

Click the template in the sequence timeline so the Properties panel opens.

Depending on how the Motion Graphics template was built, you can:

- Pick color, source text, or animation settings from choices exposed by the template's author.
- Change properties including fonts, font size, faux styles, and other available controls.

Customize the Motion Graphics template through the Graphics Templates panel together with its exposed controls.

Use those exposed controls in the Graphics Templates panel to make further Motion Graphics template adjustments.

To alter a Motion Graphics template's duration, select it in the timeline, then drag the trim head at either side to lengthen or shorten playback time.
