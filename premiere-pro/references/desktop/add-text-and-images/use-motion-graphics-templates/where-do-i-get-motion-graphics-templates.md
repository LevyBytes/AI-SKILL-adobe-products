# Where do I get Motion Graphics templates

Access, install, and customize Motion Graphics templates so professional animations and graphics can be added to your videos.

Beginning with Premiere version 25.0, the Graphics Templates panel replaces the Essential Graphics panel for browsing and managing Motion Graphics templates. MOGRTs are prebuilt animations that may contain customizable text, colors, media placeholders, and other elements, letting you adjust them to fit the needs of the project.
