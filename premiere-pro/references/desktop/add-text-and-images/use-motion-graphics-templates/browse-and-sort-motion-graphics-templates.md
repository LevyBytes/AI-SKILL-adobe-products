# Browse and sort Motion Graphics templates

Browse and arrange Motion Graphics templates in the Graphics Templates panel in Adobe Premiere.

When several Motion Graphics templates are installed, sorting by title, type, or another field helps you find one and place it into a sequence with less searching.

The Graphics Templates panel can organize and sort Motion Graphics templates from local storage, Creative Cloud Libraries, and Adobe Stock.

Search from the Graphics Templates panel when you need a specific item. Use the filter checkboxes to narrow results to templates stored locally or available through your libraries.

Select the Sort icon to order Motion Graphics template results by Recently Used or Title.

For templates with video thumbnails, preview their animation with hover scrub.

Mark Motion Graphics templates as favorites by selecting their Star icon, then use the Favorites filter to show the full favorite set together.

Tip

In Info view, rename Motion Graphics templates and add tags so organization and later search are faster.
