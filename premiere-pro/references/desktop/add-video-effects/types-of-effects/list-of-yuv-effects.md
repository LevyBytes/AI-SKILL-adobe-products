# List of YUV effects

Use the YUV Effects icon in the Effects panel to locate and apply the effects.

Premiere includes these YUV effects for maintaining dependable color.

- Alpha Adjust
- Auto Reframe
- Black & White
- Crop
- Edge Feather
- Gaussian Blur
- Horizontal Flip
- Luma Key
- Lumetri Color
- Metadata & Timecode Burn-in
- ProcAmp
- Simple Text
- Track Matte Key
- Video Limiter
