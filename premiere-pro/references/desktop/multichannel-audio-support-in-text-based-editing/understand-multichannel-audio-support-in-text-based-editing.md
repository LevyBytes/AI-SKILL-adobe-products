# Understand multichannel audio support in Text-Based Editing.

Text-Based Editing can transcribe a selected channel from an audio file or use a mix of all channels when multichannel audio support is available.

Separate microphones may be recorded onto different channels within some audio files. With multichannel audio support in Text-Based Editing, transcription can use one chosen channel or a mix of every channel. The feature applies to audio files in a multichannel mono format.

Use the Channel selection option to choose the microphone channel where the audio was recorded, or choose Mix when two speakers were recorded on two different microphones.

While transcribing an audio file, choose the needed channel or the Mix option.

Pick among available channels or use Mix according to the transcription need for the audio file.
