# Titles and closed captioning

Build professional titles and handle closed captions with offerings from EZTitles, NewBlueFX, and other vendors. These are plug-ins and tools for titles and closed captioning.

Boris Graffiti

Boris Graffiti continues to be a leading title-animation tool for everything from inventive lower thirds to smooth credit rolls and current type-on animations. Built-in text paths, jitter, and randomization help create distinctive effects. Its 3D features include realistic extrusions with reflections, bump maps, and lights, and text can also wrap around a 3D sphere. Designers can build original looks or start from a large preset and style library.

Boris RED

Boris RED delivers integrated 3D compositing, titling, and effects for Adobe Premiere as a plug-in application. Built for post-production and broadcast work, RED places many capabilities directly on the Adobe Premiere timeline: professional titling, transitions, image restoration, paint, rotoscoping, vector-object creation and extrusion, true 3D shapes and animation, and an extensive image-processing filter suite.

EZTitles

The EZTitles plug-in imports subtitles into Adobe Premiere 2.0 and later. With EZTitles, subtitles can be burned into a sequence quickly without managing thousands of tiny subtitle files on the timeline. EZTitles places every subtitle in one clip. Supported subtitle formats include EZTitles (EZT), SubRip (SRT), MicroDVD (SUB), PAC, 890, EBU (STL), and ASCII. It also accepts Unicode with timecode (TXT) and Rich Text Format with timecode (RTF), plus Cheetah CAP and NCI CAP (CAP). Windows only.

Heroglyph

Heroglyph V4 opens a large set of creative options. A single tool can produce professional video titles, lower-thirds, credits, travel routes, and handwriting animation. Use it to add final polish across projects, with many combinations of effects, animations, and distinctive fonts that deliver professional-grade results with a simpler workflow.

NewBlue Titler Pro

NewBlue Titler Pro is designed around the time pressure professional editors face. Editors can use its fast, direct interface to create refined title animations quickly. The native GPU-accelerated Titler Pro package includes starter plug-ins, styles, and templates. Custom titles can use multilayer true 3D space, environmental mapping, video or photo backgrounds, and additional controls.

Manifesto

Manifesto creates static titles, title rolls, and title crawls. For simple one-line titles, the static title generator lets you create and adjust text without leaving the parameter inspector. More detailed titles and style work can be entered in a separate text editing window.

Motype

Motype supplies a flexible toolset for building and animating text throughout the editorial process.
