# Boris FX

Boris FX Sapphire

The Essential Visual Effects Suite

Boris FX Sapphire supplies high-end effects used by leading visual effects and motion graphics artists. It can create photorealistic looks, add Hollywood lighting effects and glows, avoid white flashes with 50 drag-and-drop transitions, and produce glitchy or digital damage effects. Integrated Academy Award-winning Mocha handles fast tracking and masking. Sapphire's Effect and Transition Builder creates custom looks. The suite contains more than 270 effects plus 3000+ presets, and those presets can be saved and shared among supported video host applications for an integrated post-production workflow. Both After Effects and Premiere are supported by Sapphire for Adobe.

Boris FX Continuum

The Most Complete Plug-in Toolkit

Continuum spans editing, motion graphics, and visual effects as a broad Boris FX plug-in toolkit. Image restoration tools, including Academy Award-nominated Primatte keying tech, help repair footage quickly. It can import 3D models directly from Cinema 4D and create resolution-independent animated 2D and 3D titles and lower thirds through Title Studio. Particle Illusion produces real-time particle effects, and integrated Academy Award-winning Mocha planar tracking supports fast tracking and masking. Continuum also adds transitions, more than 250 effects, and 4000+ presets. Saved presets can move among supported video host applications for a consistent post-production workflow. Both After Effects and Premiere are supported by Continuum for Adobe.

Boris FX Mocha Pro

Academy Award-Winning Planar Tracking & Masking

For advanced visual effects and finishing, Boris FX Mocha Pro supplies planar tracking and masking that saves time for editors, artists, and compositors. It solves difficult motion tracking shots, including material with blurred or occluded objects. The GPU-accelerated Remove Module can automatically remove unwanted items such as wires. Advanced masking tools reduce rotoscoping effort by requiring fewer keyframes. It can replace screens, stabilize shaky footage, and correct lens distortion. Mono and stereoscopic 360/VR optimized tools help accelerate immersive video post-production. Mocha Pro for Adobe supports both After Effects and Premiere and provides an integrated workflow.

Particle Illusion

Boris FX Particle Illusion is a real-time 64-bit particle and motion graphics generator for creating polished animations quickly. It can add preset-driven photorealistic particle effects to titles, lower thirds, and background graphics. The Emitter Library includes over 3000 drag & drop presets. Integrated Academy Award-winning Mocha supports fast particle tracking and masking. Presets can be stored and reused across supported video host applications for a connected post-production workflow. Particle Illusion is bundled with Continuum and the Continuum Particles Unit, and a render-engine standalone release is planned. Both After Effects and Premiere are supported by Particle Illusion for Adobe.
