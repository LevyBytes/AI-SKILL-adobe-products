# RE:Vision Effects

RE:Fill

Fill user-specified image regions intelligently to repair holes.

DE:Noise

Reduce noise with feature-sensitive spatial filtering and time-based optical flow methods.

RE:Match

Automatically color match one clip view to another so the footage appears to have been captured with the same camera and settings.

FieldsKit

Provide improved deinterlacing and additional workflow options for interlacing and pulldown.

ReelSmart Motion Blur

Track each pixel in a sequence automatically and blur the scene from the calculated motion.

Shade/Shape

Turn 2D elements into 3D rendered artwork.

Twixtor

Slow down, speed up, and frame-rate convert image sequences intelligently.

SmoothKit

Smooth imagery with a full toolset that combines user-directed controls and feature-sensitive methods.

Video Gogh

Convert pictures and videos into painted-looking artwork.
