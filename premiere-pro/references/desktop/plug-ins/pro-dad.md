# proDAD

Adorage

Adorage is a large special effects library with more than 17,000 effects across 13 effect volumes and a short learning curve. After the user selects an effect, Adorage handles the rest. Particles/lights, smoke, explosions, diamond effects, and photo-realistic effects can produce results for weddings, vacations, family, sports, and documentaries. Adorage works on the Adobe Premiere timeline and provides HD/SD effects in volumes 10 and higher. Windows only.

Mercalli V2 Pro

Unwanted shake can be removed directly on the Adobe Premiere timeline with the Mercalli V2 Pro video stabilizer and rolling-shutter correction plug-in from proDAD. Its 3D stabilization corrects the X, Y, and Z camera axis while limiting the need to zoom in. Its fully automatic rolling-shutter correction removes skew, wobble, and jello that CMOS-type camcorders/cameras can introduce during fast pans. It is available for Windows and macOS.

VitaScene V2

proDAD's VitaScene V2 is a professional-grade library of video effects and filters, with 600-plus fully customizable HD effects such as tilt-shift, vintage film, de-focus, sophisticated light effects, particle effects, and more. VitaScene V2 is GPU-accelerated for fast rendering. It is sold as VitaScene V2 Pro, with all effects and customization, and VitaScene V2 LE, with over 100 fixed effects. Windows only.
