# ISP

ROBUSKEY for Video

After Effects and Adobe Premiere can run ROBUSKEY for Video. It adds high-quality chroma-keying to green background or blue background video, including hair strands moving in a breeze and frizzy hairstyles. Background color spill captured with the subject is automatically adjusted for the composite, and uneven-lighting variations and shadows can be removed cleanly. Rendering is designed to remain natural on curved glass, including wine glasses, and on transparent liquids.

ISP 3D Title

ISP 3D Title is compatible with Adobe After Effects and Premiere. It adds stereoscopic 3D effects to text subtitles made for stereoscopic 3D video. Parallax is adjustable and the operation is simple. Text and any other 2D graphics can be turned into stereoscopic 3D video. ISP 3D Preview is included so users can quickly check how the effect will appear.

ISP Film Color Anti FalseColor

ISP Film Color Anti FalseColor is compatible with After Effects and Adobe Premiere. It removes false color associated with video shot on DSLR cameras with large sensors. False colors can be produced for many reasons, but applying this plug-in corrects them to true color, including in night scenes.

ISP Film Color Like HDR

ISP Film Color Like HDR is compatible with After Effects and Adobe Premiere. Applying the plug-in adds an HDR effect to video. Users can also adjust only three parameters to explore looks ranging from supernatural to superartificial.
