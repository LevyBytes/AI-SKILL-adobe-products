The Cinegy Video Codec Plugin Pack brings in Cinegy's Daniel2 GPU codec support together with NVIDIA-accelerated H.264 and HEVC export.

Daniel2's parallel processing architecture can combine with modern multi-CPU systems or NVIDIA graphics cards to speed ultra-high resolution cinema and post-production pipelines. Compared with other production codecs, Daniel2 can process UHD, 4K, 8K or higher resolution video at much higher speeds. For edit-while-recording work, Daniel2 uses MXF OP1A. Alpha channels are handled efficiently as well. Cinegy's NVIDIA GPU-powered H.264 and HEVC export speeds export jobs substantially while freeing CPU resources for other work.

ProEXR

Use ProEXR for OpenEXR import and export. OpenEXR is widely used in visual effects production and serves as the basis for the emerging IIF/ACES standard. In Adobe Premiere, ProEXR handles timecode, multichannel images, and every available OpenEXR compression option. Adobe Premiere's native floating-point color pipeline preserves maximum image fidelity.

Drastic's MediaReactor plug-in set offers the broadest range of input and output file types. MediaReactor imports major camera raw formats (Cine/CFX/VRW/KRW/MLV/CRM), along with broadcast formats including GXF, LXF, and Grass Valley K2 XML. It also replaces the deprecated QuickTime layer by covering most legacy codecs and current broadcast codecs such as XAVC, AVCi, LongG, MPEG-2, H.26,4 and HEVC. During edit-while-recording sessions, MediaReactor can also use real-time index (RTIN) record/capture files.

On export, MediaReactor expands Adobe's existing format coverage with Sony XAVC-S MP4 and MOV files using common production codecs. Additional export targets include AS-02, unencrypted DCP, IMF, HDF, P2 DVHD/AVCi and AS-11. The set also supports DPX, YUV and YUV streams. DASH fragmented MP4 and HLS/M3U8 h.264/ACC stream sets are also available.

MediaReactor Lite is a lower-cost plug-in bundle focused on the MediaReactor plug-ins most often needed for QuickTime replacement, camera raw, MPEG-4, and Sony XAVC-S workflows. Through the native PRM interface, the Lite edition lets Premiere directly use formats including Phantom Cine, Magic Lantern MLV, and Panasonic VRW. It also supports Kinefinity KRW, Codex CDX, and Canon CRM. MediaReactor Lite also supplies exporters aimed at broadcast, post production, and multimedia MOV formats. MP4 export is included as well, with XAVC-S available for compatibility across broad workflows.

SurCode Dolby Digital 5.1

SurCode for Dolby Digital is a 5.1 surround Dolby Digital (AC-3) encoder plug-in included with the new Adobe Premiere digital video editor. The included SurCode for Dolby Digital trial encoder is closely integrated with Adobe Media Encoder. Adobe's latest flagship nonlinear editor can now encode and multiplex MPEG-2 video with AC-3 surround (or stereo) audio at the same time.
