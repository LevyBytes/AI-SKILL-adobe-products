# Navigate the panels

Cycling through panels in Adobe Premiere with a shortcut instead of selecting every panel manually.

Before you begin

Open and arrange the panels your project needs from the Window menu in Premiere.

Rightward panel focus:

- Windows: Ctrl + Shift + . (period).
- macOS: Control + Shift + . (period).

Leftward panel focus:

- Windows: Ctrl + Shift + , (comma).
- macOS: Control + Shift + , (comma).
