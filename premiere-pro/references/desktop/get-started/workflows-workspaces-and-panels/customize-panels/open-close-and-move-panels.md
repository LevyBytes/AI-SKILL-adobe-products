# Open, close, and move panels

To open a panel, select Window, then choose the name of the panel you want.

Close a panel with Ctrl+W (Windows), Command+W (macOS), or the Close button.

To see every panel in a panel group, use the horizontal scroll bar to reveal all panel tabs.

To bring a panel forward within a panel group, select the panel tab you want in front, hover over the tab area, and drag tabs horizontally to reorder them.

Closing a panel group in the application window causes the remaining groups to expand into the freed space. Closing a floating window also closes the panels it contains.
