# Display any panel full-screen

Expanding any panel into full-screen mode and toggling back to the normal view.

When editing in Premiere, you can expand a panel full-screen when your workflow needs extra room. The command can act on the panel with focus or on the panel under the mouse pointer.

Put the pointer over the panel to maximize.

Use the accent key (`) once to maximize the panel, then press it again to restore the panel size.

Tip

On many keyboards, this key is just left of the numeral one (1) key. It may also be described as the back quote key or the tilde key.

Another menu path is Window > Maximize Frame for the selected panel, followed by Window > Restore Frame Size when you need the previous size back.

For a computer with a non-U.S. keyboard, Adobe Premiere may bind Maximize or Restore Frame Under Cursor to something other than the accent key. The Keyboard Shortcuts dialog box shows the key assigned to Maximize or Restore Frame Under Cursor and to Maximize or Restore Active Frame. To find it, choose the Adobe Premiere Defaults set, choose Application from the menu, move past the Help heading, locate Maximize or Restore Frame Under Cursor, and assign the shortcut that works best.
