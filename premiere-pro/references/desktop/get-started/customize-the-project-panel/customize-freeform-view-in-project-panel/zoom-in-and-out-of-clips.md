# Zoom in and out of clips

Change clip zoom with any of these controls:

- Scroll the mouse wheel while holding Option (macOS) or Alt (Windows)
- Use a trackpad pinch gesture
- Press the + and - keyboard shortcuts
- Adjust the zoom slider control; double-clicking it restores default zoom

Snap the edges of a clip to another: Hold Alt (Windows) or Option (macOS) during the drag so the clip edge snaps against another clip edge.

This places one clip directly beside another so the two connect without a gap.

Align all or selected clips to grid: Select the clips, open the context menu with right-click, then choose Align to Grid.

Selected clips are arranged neatly and lined up automatically to an even grid.

Adjust the size of clips: Select clips, right-click, choose Clip Size, and then select a size option. Medium is the default clip size.

Changing clip size makes clips larger or smaller in the workspace, which can improve visibility or fit more clips into the view.

Hover scrub the clips: Scrub by moving the mouse pointer left and right over a clip. Use I and O to mark the In point and Out point.

Moving the cursor over a clip gives a quick preview and lets you set editing start and end points.
