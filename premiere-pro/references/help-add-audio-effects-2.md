## Specify default audio transitions

Learn how Adobe Premiere uses default audio transitions and a default duration, and how to set them.

### Specify the default audio transition

For either Constant Gain or Constant Power in the Effects panel, open the context menu with right-click (Windows) or control-click (macOS).

From that context menu, choose Set Selected As Default Transition.

### Set the default duration for audio transitions

Open Edit > Preferences > Timeline (Windows) or Premiere > Preferences > Timeline (macOS).

Set Audio Transition Default Duration in the Preferences dialog box.

### Adjust or customize an audio transition

Choose any of these actions:

- To edit an audio transition, open it by double-clicking the transition in a Timeline panel, then use the Effect Controls panel for the adjustment.
- For customizing the rate of an audio fade or crossfade, modify the clip's audio volume keyframe graph rather than using a transition.

## Audio crossfade transitions

### Create natural-sounding audio in Adobe Premiere projects by using crossfade effects to smooth the change between clips.

In Adobe Premiere, audio crossfade transitions smooth the sound between clips and improve the listening experience in a video project. They help remove abrupt audio changes so the soundtrack flows more evenly.

### Constant Gain

The Constant Gain crossfade raises and lowers audio at a constant rate while moving between clips. This approach can create an audible volume dip near the middle of the transition.

### Constant Power

The Constant Power crossfade blends audio smoothly, much like a video dissolve. For the outgoing clip, volume falls gently at the beginning and then drops faster as the transition closes. For the incoming clip, volume rises quickly at first and then increases more gradually near the end.

### Exponential Fade

Exponential Fade lowers the first clip along a smooth logarithmic curve while raising the second clip with the same type of curve. Use the Alignment control menu to set where the transition is positioned.

Use Premiere's three main audio crossfade transitions when you need different fade behaviors.

Note

Although Exponential Fade can sound close to Constant Power, its change is gentler overall.

## Use Adobe Stock audio in your project

### Find, preview, and use high-quality Adobe Stock audio inside Premiere projects.

Adobe Stock brings royalty-free music and sound effects into Premiere. From the editing workspace, you can search, audition, and use professional tracks without switching applications.

### Why use Adobe Stock audio?

- Browse a large curated library of high-quality tracks.
- Search and preview audio from inside Premiere instead of leaving the edit.
- Use built-in licensing to keep usage rights covered.
- After purchase, swap preview tracks for high-quality versions without rebuilding the sequence.

Before you begin:

Confirm first that Adobe Stock is included with your active Adobe Creative Cloud subscription.

Open your Premiere project.

Use Window > Workspaces > Audio to switch to the Audio workspace.

Open Browse in the Essential Sound panel.

Search with the search bar, or browse by mood, genre, and other filters.

Audition tracks by selecting the Play button beside each audio waveform.

Turn on Timeline sync when you want playback aligned with your timeline.

For a preview download, drag the track you chose from the Essential Sound panel into your sequence.

Premiere downloads a low-bitrate M4a preview version so you can test it in the project.

When the preview works for the edit, use the Essential Sound panel and select the License icon beside the track.

Select Purchase to finish licensing.

Premiere then replaces every preview instance across the project with the licensed high-quality version, delivered as uncompressed WAV files using 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz sample rates.

## About Adobe Stock audio licensing and usage rights

### Adobe Stock audio usage terms.

Adobe Stock audio can be used in many contexts with a standard or extended license, including training material, online ads, wedding films, paid advertising, social media, and more.

For film, broadcast, SVOD, apps, games, or point of sale, Adobe Stock audio requires an extended license. The track must be synced with visual material or with other audio, such as a podcast. Stand-alone use is not allowed.

Small changes such as pitch shifting or time compression are permitted. Remixes, mashups, and changes intended to create a new song are not permitted.

After an audio track is licensed, you can use it in Adobe Premiere and in other apps, including Adobe Audition and Adobe Premiere Rush.

For restriction details, check the License Terms page or the Terms of Use.

If YouTube issues a copyright claim for a licensed Adobe Stock track, dispute it with the license ID supplied by Adobe Stock. That ID is available in your license history at stock.adobe.com.
