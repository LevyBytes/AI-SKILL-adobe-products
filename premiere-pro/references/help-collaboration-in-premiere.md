# Collaboration In Premiere

> Adobe Premiere Pro reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

### Explore Adobe Premiere collaboration features for working with teammates from any location.

## How can I edit collaboratively with my colleagues?

Premiere supports collaborative editing, and After Effects supports collaborative graphics creation, so teammates can contribute to a shared project together.

- Team Projects: Work with teammates in one shared project. Invite the people you need and collaborate directly in the timeline. Cloud-saved updates are shared with the team, allowing everyone to work together. Best suited to small teams, businesses, or individual users.
- Productions: Use bin locking to let larger teams work in separate project files for one shared feature. See what others are doing while avoiding conflicts. Keep ownership of your timeline or pass it to teammates when it is ready. Best suited to larger features.
