# Apply Lumetri Looks, Display Lumetri Scopes

> Adobe Premiere Pro reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

## Overview

Covers: Apply Lumetri looks; Display Lumetri Scopes.

## Apply Lumetri looks

### Apply presets in Adobe Premiere and adjust them until the desired creative look is reached.

The Creative section of the Lumetri Color panel contains Looks for fast preset-based color adjustments on clips. Premiere includes several creative looks, and custom LUTs can also be created and saved so they appear in this panel for quick access.

Once a look is applied, refine settings such as vibrance and saturation.

The Lumetri Color panel includes a Looks Preset Thumbnail viewer. Browse creative looks through the dropdown list or use the arrows on the preview to move among different Looks, then select the image to apply the look to the clip.

### Looks

Apply Looks when the goal is a video with a professionally shot film appearance. A look can stand alone, or it can be placed before or after a custom grade. Premiere also includes preset film stock and camera looks in Lumetri Presets inside the Effects panel.

- Intensity: controls how strongly the look is applied. Dragging right increases the applied effect, while dragging left reduces it.

Drag right to increase the look intensity, or drag left to reduce it.

Increase or decrease the look intensity by dragging the control right or left.

### Adjustments

Use the Adjustments section in the Creative section when a clip needs color and lighting refinement.

In the Creative section, use Adjustments to refine the clip's color and lighting.

- Faded Film: creates a faded film treatment on the video. Move the sliders left or right until the vintage result looks right.
- Sharpen: changes edge definition for a crisper video image. Move the slider right for stronger edge definition and left for less. Higher edge definition makes video detail stand out more, so avoid pushing sharpening so far that the image appears unnatural.

Tip

Set the slider to zero (0) to disable sharpening.

- Vibrance: changes saturation while reducing clipping as colors approach full saturation. It affects lower-saturated colors more than highly saturated colors and helps keep skin tones from becoming oversaturated.
- Saturation: changes every color in the clip evenly across the range from 0 (monochrome) to 200 (double the saturation).
- Tint wheels: control tint values in shadows and highlights with the Shadow Tint and Highlight Tint wheels. Empty wheel centers show that no tint has been applied. To add tint, select the wheel center and drag the cursor until the wheel fills.
- Tint Balance: offsets excess magenta or green in the clip.

## Display Lumetri Scopes

Monitor Adobe Premiere color and exposure with visual scopes so brightness, contrast, and tone can be balanced.

Lumetri Scopes provides resizable built-in video scopes: Vectorscopes, Histogram, Parade, and Waveform. Use these scopes to judge clips and guide color correction.

Open Lumetri Scopes from Window > Lumetri Scopes in the menu bar.

To show one scope, right-click inside the Lumetri Scopes panel and choose a scope from the drop-down. The selected scope appears in the panel.

For scope configuration, use the dropdown menu in the lower-right corner and choose the mode that matches the footage: 8-bit, 10-bit, float, or HDR. With HDR footage, the scale adjusts automatically to show 0-10000 Nits.

Enable the Clamp Signal checkbox to clip any signal above 100 IRE or below 0 IRE. This clamping changes only the scope display and does not change the timeline or exported media.

Tip

To see all scopes at once, right-click the Lumetri Scopes panel and choose Presets > All Scopes RGB or All Scopes YUV/YC.
