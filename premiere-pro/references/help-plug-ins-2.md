# Plug Ins (part 2)

> Adobe Premiere Pro reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

## Effects and transitions

Cosmo is a professional cosmetic cleanup tool for HD close-ups, glamor shots, and age reduction on onscreen talent. It can make a 30-year-old model, a 40-something actress, or a 50-plus executive look excellent, even beyond expectations, in video footage. Powered by Colorista II technology, Cosmo uses simple beauty sliders to smooth skin tones, soften problem areas, and repair blemishes.

Magic Bullet Frames

Magic Bullet Frames applies sophisticated algorithms to ordinary interlaced video. The result is a smooth, professional, deinterlaced 24p film look. Users can choose motion-adaptive or field-blended methods for high-quality footage at high speeds directly in the editing timeline.

Magic Bullet Instant 4K

Instant 4K up-converts HD video to multiple 4K and UHD video formats. Preset resolutions help integrate HD video into a 4K production. During high definition (HD) to 4K/Ultra HD (UHD) scaling, Instant 4K algorithms generate missing pixels, adding sharpening and anti-aliasing for clear output inside the video application.

Magic Bullet Quick Looks

Quick Looks applies a chosen style to footage in seconds, whether the target is urban grunge, a desert sunrise, or film stocks from classic movies. It is a lighter version of the full Looks tool, with the same speed and quality at a lower cost. Users can define the video or film style from 100-plus look presets for moods ranging from wedding footage to Sundance-style projects.

Magic Bullet LUT Buddy

Magic Bullet LUT Buddy is built for professional colorists and connects desktop applications with high-end systems and hardware boxes. With Adobe Premiere, LUT Buddy can output LUTs and communicate with higher-end systems from DaVinci Resolve to Panasonic displays. For workflows already using 1D or 3D LUTs, the tool helps move values among compatible software and devices.

Magic Bullet Colorista Free

Magic Bullet Colorista Free is a free product that encourages users to set their colors free through a simplified, elegant color corrector that can be shared and used broadly. Core controls designed by experienced colorists include the 3-Way Wheels from Colorista II and numeric sliders for exact numerical adjustment. For advanced workflows, Colorista Free is able to exchange Color Decision List (CDL) values with CDL-compliant host apps and align color work to industry standards.

Presto

Red Giant Presto saves time by turning a live presentation event into a high-quality video. It imports a timeline with presenter footage into the editing application and aligns it with the original presentation slides. Presto handles production tasks such as tracking presenter movement, switching slides at the correct moment, and rearranging the layout instantly. The finished result is a professionally designed video that can be shared soon after the presentation ends.

PluralEyes

PluralEyes 3 automatically synchronizes audio and multi-camera video, preparing sync in seconds instead of days. This stand-alone application works directly with Premiere. Users can monitor sync through an interactive timeline and visual feedback, then apply built-in fine tuning controls. Version 3 is up to 20x faster than PluralEyes 2 and supports many file formats and codecs. PluralEyes 3 is intended to make post-production audio/video sync faster, less tedious, and more accurate.

Trapcode 3D Stroke

3D Stroke lets paths, logos, or lines glow, taper, and move through 3D space in complex ways. A built-in camera captures the action from any angle. It is used for animated logos and sophisticated write-on effects in broadcast TV work.

Trapcode Shine

Shine creates light effects from inside the editing timeline without the render times of specialized 3D applications. It can produce sunlight through clouds, headlights through fog, or horror movie titles. Users can select from 22 powerful presets or build custom light ray effects. 16-bit and 32-bit support helps maintain high-quality light effects for any project.

Trapcode Starglow

Starglow is a multidirectional glow effect designed for fast rendering. It can make multicolored, star-shaped glints like those produced by a camera filter. Each customizable glow can include up to eight directions, and every direction can use its own color map and streak length for many possible looks.

Red Giant Warp

Warp plug-ins provide control over shadows, reflections, glows, and corner point warps. The Shadow tool creates shadows for text or greenscreen subjects, while the Reflection tool builds mirror-like reflections. Radium Glow adds refined glows and glimmers. The Corner Pin tool improves realism for warped images, can import tracking data from mocha for After Effects, and supports secondary transforms and motion blur.

Red Giant Text Anarchy

Text Anarchy treats text as a major compositional element, whether it is used for visual background noise, texture, or flashing words that reinforce a theme. It can generate effects from Bezier paths, particle arrays, and matrix-style patterns while retaining fine typographic control. The package also includes the Geomancy Shapes plug-ins.

Red Giant ToonIt

ToonIt turns video into a cartoon look quickly with algorithms for cartoon shading and line generation. Its five plug-ins avoid time-consuming methods such as rotoscoping and hand painting while still letting users control styles, shading, and outlines precisely. Version 2 adds the Heat Vision plug-in, which mimics the look of the Predator movies, plus 14 new styles, additional presets, and faster output.

Universe

As a free community, Red Giant Universe provides access to fast free tools for editing, visual effects, and motion design. Every effects and transitions tool in the Universe library is GPU-enabled for speed and works in multiple host applications on both Mac and Windows. The library keeps expanding with new effects and transitions, and existing tools are updated often.

## Codecs and file formats

Add broader format support through plug-ins for extra video and audio codecs, including fnord software and MediaReactor. The following entries cover codecs and file-format tools.

PICVideo

For Windows only, this Video for Windows codec adds Motion JPEG codec support for AVI files.

The Cinegy Video Codec Plugin Pack brings in Cinegy's Daniel2 GPU codec support together with NVIDIA-accelerated H.264 and HEVC export.

Daniel2's parallel processing architecture can combine with modern multi-CPU systems or NVIDIA graphics cards to speed ultra-high resolution cinema and post-production pipelines. Compared with other production codecs, Daniel2 can process UHD, 4K, 8K or higher resolution video at much higher speeds. For edit-while-recording work, Daniel2 uses MXF OP1A. Alpha channels are handled efficiently as well. Cinegy's NVIDIA GPU-powered H.264 and HEVC export speeds export jobs substantially while freeing CPU resources for other work.

ProEXR

Use ProEXR for OpenEXR import and export. OpenEXR is widely used in visual effects production and serves as the basis for the emerging IIF/ACES standard. In Adobe Premiere, ProEXR handles timecode, multichannel images, and every available OpenEXR compression option. Adobe Premiere's native floating-point color pipeline preserves maximum image fidelity.

Drastic's MediaReactor plug-in set offers the broadest range of input and output file types. MediaReactor imports major camera raw formats (Cine/CFX/VRW/KRW/MLV/CRM), along with broadcast formats including GXF, LXF, and Grass Valley K2 XML. It also replaces the deprecated QuickTime layer by covering most legacy codecs and current broadcast codecs such as XAVC, AVCi, LongG, MPEG-2, H.26,4 and HEVC. During edit-while-recording sessions, MediaReactor can also use real-time index (RTIN) record/capture files.

On export, MediaReactor expands Adobe's existing format coverage with Sony XAVC-S MP4 and MOV files using common production codecs. Through the native PRM interface, the Lite edition lets Premiere directly use formats including Phantom Cine, Magic Lantern MLV, and Panasonic VRW. It also supports Kinefinity KRW, Codex CDX, and Canon CRM. MediaReactor Lite also supplies exporters aimed at broadcast, post production, and multimedia MOV formats. MP4 export is included as well, with XAVC-S available for compatibility across broad workflows.

## Titles and closed captioning

Build professional titles and handle closed captions with offerings from EZTitles, NewBlueFX, and other vendors. These are plug-ins and tools for titles and closed captioning.

Boris Graffiti

Boris Graffiti continues to be a leading title-animation tool for everything from inventive lower thirds to smooth credit rolls and current type-on animations. Built-in text paths, jitter, and randomization help create distinctive effects. Its 3D features include realistic extrusions with reflections, bump maps, and lights, and text can also wrap around a 3D sphere. Designers can build original looks or start from a large preset and style library.

Boris RED

Boris RED delivers integrated 3D compositing, titling, and effects for Adobe Premiere as a plug-in application. Built for post-production and broadcast work, RED places many capabilities directly on the Adobe Premiere timeline: professional titling, transitions, image restoration, paint, rotoscoping, vector-object creation and extrusion, true 3D shapes and animation, and an extensive image-processing filter suite.

NewBlue Titler Pro

Manifesto

Manifesto creates static titles, title rolls, and title crawls. For simple one-line titles, the static title generator lets you create and adjust text without leaving the parameter inspector. More detailed titles and style work can be entered in a separate text editing window.

Motype

## Workflow tools

Improve Premiere workflow with automation and integration tools from companies such as Intelligent Assistance and Pond5. The following plug-ins and tools are available to enhance the Premiere workflow.

ProductionLink

ProductionLink exports Premiere renders and bin files into an Avid Interplay and ISIS environment. It creates correct DNxHD MXF OP-atom files and AAF files for direct use in Avid without additional transcoding. Premiere can import the AAF and OP-atom files, and ProductionLink finishes the roundtrip by exporting renders back into the workflow.

Sequence Clip Reporter

Sequence Clip Reporter generates a formatted Excel spreadsheet report covering the video clips, audio clips, titles, filters, transitions, and markers used in an edited Adobe Premiere sequence. It removes the tedious manual work involved in clip usage reports, music use reports, filter reports, and marker reports by sending the data straight to Excel without EDL workarounds or copy/paste methods.

prEdit

Paper cuts without the pain! prEdit gives transcript-driven story editing and first-edit work a new approach. Starting from transcribed media files, prEdit helps you subclip the strongest material from the transcript, add log notes with tools such as auto-complete and auto-increment, build and refine stories that preview inside prEdit, and send the result to Adobe Premiere as a cut sequence for continued editing.

Transcriptize

Use Transcriptize when transcriptions need to move out of Adobe Premiere and into Avid Media Composer, Excel, or Apple Final Cut Pro. It can name speakers, export a plain text file for a producer or for import into the Media Composer ScriptSync engine, or create an Excel spreadsheet where the words per row are configurable. Well suited to producer workflows.

Xto7 for Final Cut Pro

Xto7 for Final Cut Pro converts the new Final Cut Pro X Project XML (FCPXML) into Sequence XML, which can then be imported into Adobe Premiere or other applications. From Adobe Premiere, dynamic links can connect to After Effects, Adobe Audition(R), Encore (for real Blu-ray authoring), and additional XML workflow tools for Final Cut Pro 7. One example is Sequence Clip Reporter, which supports reports for stock footage and music cue sheets.

Pond5 Plug-in, for Adobe(R) Premiere(R) Pro

Pond5 provides a unique panel that lets video editors access the largest library of royalty-free stock videos, plus hundreds of thousands of music tracks and sound effects, entirely within Adobe Premiere.

Red Giant PluralEyes

Automatic synchronization without timecode.

PluralEyes synchronizes audio and video clips automatically, with no need for timecode, clappers, or special setup. Multicamera, dual-system audio, and multitake projects become much easier to assemble. Working with Adobe Premiere, PluralEyes quickly syncs multicamera video and audio tracks, avoiding complex camera setups, timecode handling, and long manual synchronization work. The result is less wasted time and cost, lower frustration, and more focus on creative editing.

Red Giant Presto

Fast and easy creation of presentation videos.

Presto combines sharp, bright slide images with a tightly tracked inset of the live presenter to produce an engaging presentation video for sharing. This removes the need for repetitive editing and manual presenter tracking. It saves time and money, lowers frustration, and lets you concentrate on the creative presentation of the video.

Quick 3D

Vision III Imaging, Inc., (v3(R)) focuses on parallax visualization technologies for military and entertainment markets. v3's QuickS3D(TM) plug-in adds fully integrated stereoscopic capability to Adobe Premiere. Precision tools support exact alignment of 3D source imagery in every vector. For Adobe's Pro Video tools, QuickS3D provides an S3D post-production solution.
