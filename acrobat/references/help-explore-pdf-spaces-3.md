## Customize PDF Space experiences

Shape the way people who receive your PDF Space encounter it from the overview page.

Owning a PDF Space lets you gather a set of files together and decide how that content is arranged and shown. Reshape the layout, revise AI-generated content, set your own theme and link preview, and bring in extras like audio, presentations, or images.

### Arrange the items

Decide what greets recipients first and strip out anything you don't want shown — that's how you shape both the layout and the reading flow of a PDF Space.

Launch the PDF Space so the Overview page is in front of you.

Want to shuffle items around? Float the pointer over one until the hand cursor shows up, then grab it and drag it wherever you'd like it to sit.

### Add and manage items

Bring podcasts, text items, and notes into your PDF Space to make it feel richer and more on-brand.

Float beneath any item already there and choose Add content.

Pull in and arrange content — text, images, files, notes, or summaries — so everything related stays grouped together.

Pick the kind of content you'd like to drop in from the toolbar:

- Text: Add headings, body copy, or supporting context
- Files: share documents, spreadsheets, or other attachments for recipients to download
- Notes: Include internal annotations or additional guidance for recipients
- Podcast: Embed an audio file or podcast episode
- Image: Upload a photo, diagram, or visual to support your content
- Summary: Generate an AI-written overview of your content
- Logo: Upload your brand logo to personalize the space

Walk through the prompts to wrap up the insertion. Whatever you just added shows up right under the item you had selected.

If you like, drag the new item somewhere else, or click into it to keep editing and refining.

Decide both the visual style of your PDF Space and the way it shows up when you hand it off as a link.

## Create PDF Spaces in Acrobat web

Walk through setting up PDF Spaces in Acrobat on the web, where content stays organized and both analysis and content creation are AI-powered.

Several files and links can be brought together so you get AI-powered insights and keep your research within one conversational knowledge hub. Drawing on the files and links you've added, it builds summaries, fields questions, brings key insights to the surface, and turns out podcasts and presentations.

### Create a PDF Space

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose PDF Spaces.

Choose Create a PDF Space.

When the dialog box appears, pull in files by way of these options:

- Choose files stored on Adobe cloud storage.
- Upload supported file types.
- Import from third-party storage. Select Add cloud files to connect to the third-party storage accounts.
- Add text using the Pasted text option.
- Add webpage URLs using the Web link option.

Note

The Supported file formats and limitations of files in PDF Spaces topic has more on this.

Pulling a range of sources into your PDF Space gathers every bit of relevant content in one place, which sharpens insights and speeds up output.

Pulling a range of sources into your PDF Space gathers every bit of relevant content in one place, which sharpens insights and speeds up output.

Choose Add to PDF Space.

When your PDF Space finishes loading, it carries these elements:

- An Overview with a few default items
- Auto-generated files and audio summary
- Files in the left panel
- Auto-generated podcasts in the Creations tab
- AI Assistant chat panel

If you like, the dropdown beside the PDF Space name lets you spin up or switch between PDF Spaces, rename a file, save a copy, delete it, or send it over to the desktop app.

### Explore the PDF Space interface

As many as three panels can stay open together.

- Left panel: Shows whichever item you've selected — Files, Creations, or the Customize panel. Podcast and presentation outputs generated by AI play right from this tab. Any panel can be expanded for focus or shut.
- Center panel: Shows the Overview, complete with an auto-generated title, an audio summary, and a files summary. Expand it for focus or close it.
- Right panel: Through the AI Assistant panel you pose questions, call for insights, and have content generated off your files.

Across a three-panel PDF Space layout, manage and view creations, go over summarized content, and work with AI Assistant.

Across a three-panel PDF Space layout, manage and view creations, go over summarized content, and work with AI Assistant.

### Create content from your PDF Space

Through the Create tab, fresh AI-powered material can be produced straight from whatever files live in your PDF Space. The options you can build include:

- Podcasts: A spoken, AI-built discussion drawn from your documents. After they finish generating, podcasts land in the Creations tab. Listen to them right inside the interface, where controls cover speed, volume, skip, and a transcript view.
- Presentations: An AI-built slide deck that pulls together the main points from your files. Each one shows up as a visual preview and can be stored in the Creations tab.
- Notes: A written recap, or a collection of notes, pulled from your documents.

### Customize your PDF Space

To tailor your workspace, pick Customize at the foot of the left navigation bar.

- Theme: Pick a background theme and it takes effect on the spot.
- Link preview: Decides how the PDF Space looks once shared, showing the title plus the domain. Swap out the preview image when you need to.

Being the PDF Space owner, you get to shape how your content is introduced and taken in before sharing. That covers polishing AI‑generated summaries, laying out items on the Overview page, and folding in elements such as notes, podcasts, or branding.

The first time recipients open your shared PDF Space, they arrive at a structured Overview that quickly conveys what matters, how the content is arranged, and what to do next.

## Interactive experience sharing with PDF Spaces

With PDF Spaces, knowledge gets shared and circulated while the sender steers recipients toward the results and outcomes they have in mind.

Files, context, AI-generated audio plus text summaries, and an AI Assistant can all be brought together and laid out so the important parts stand out. The effect is to lead recipients along and keep the material approachable no matter the device or format.

Whenever your message has to register fast and unmistakably — think learning materials, executive briefings, policy rollouts, or client-facing communications — you get to tailor what the recipient encounters.

### What you control

Tailoring a PDF Space puts these choices in your hands:

- What recipients see first
- How content is structured and ordered
- How content is consumed (read, listen, or explore)

Owning a PDF Space means you can rearrange and edit its items, tune the AI‑generated responses, and set how the material gets taken in. The payoff is an experience for recipients that lands faster, reads clearer, and points them toward action.

### Key capabilities

### Customizable Overview (entry point)

Editable content items are the building blocks of your PDF Space experience. Drag items into a new order or take them out so the flow follows your intent instead of the default file sequence. Pick a background color theme to set the look of your PDF Space, and pass it along with a rich link preview that previews the contents for recipients before they ever click in.

Out of the box, a PDF Space spins up a headline, files, an audio summary, and a text summary. Should the underlying content shift, those pieces refresh on their own — unless you've already edited them by hand.

### Editable AI‑generated content

Every AI‑generated summary and response stays open to editing. Before you share, dial in the tone, context, and accuracy so the result carries your intent. You can rework the audio summary transcript too, turning it into a personalized message.

### Multiple content formats

When it comes to taking in information, PDF Spaces experience sharing hands recipients several routes:

- AI-generated text summary for quick understanding
- AI-generated audio summary with editable transcripts
- Podcast presented in a two‑speaker, conversational format
- Custom text, notes, and other content items

Drop any of these onto the Overview page and switch them on while the experience plays out.

Note

For the moment, every user gets audio summaries at no extra charge. Anything that later changes about availability or pricing will get its own announcement.

### Recipient engagement insights

How recipients work through your content is something you can follow without chasing anyone down. You'll find these engagement insights only in Acrobat on the web and the newest Acrobat desktop app, surfaced right through the recipient profile icons sitting beside the Share tool. That's where you tally total views, identify who opened the space, confirm the timing of each visit, and look over comments or interactions.

Lean on the engagement insights to size up reach, decide which follow-ups come first, and read impact in a hurry.

Lean on the engagement insights to size up reach, decide which follow-ups come first, and read impact in a hurry. Note

Only PDF Spaces built and shared from the newest Acrobat build carry engagement insights; existing PDF Spaces and older desktop versions are out of scope.

### What recipients experience

A shared link is the way in for recipients, dropping them onto a guided Overview page that gets them oriented right away.

From that page they can grasp the point through headlines and summaries, scan the material visually with file thumbnails, play the audio summaries, or drill into the individual files. Where it's turned on, the AI Assistant is also there for exploring insights or posing questions.

Notes, images, podcasts, and other extra formats open up fuller ways to interact with the material. And to keep things safe and trustworthy, the Overview page lets recipients flag abuse without leaving it.

### Limitations

- Block limits: PDF Space owners can add 1 to 25 items per Overview page
- Branding: Limited to logos

  - Auto-updates for unedited content
  - Pauses after manual edits
  - Deleted items lose prior edits

- Language support: Audio summary and Podcasts are limited to supported languages

## Review PDF Spaces

Discover how to review shared PDF Spaces within Adobe Acrobat, contributing comments, annotations, and feedback that match the permission level you've been granted.

Collaboration runs smoothly when you review shared PDF Spaces, since it lets you weigh in on files, notes, and insights. What you're allowed to do during review hinges on the permission level assigned to you: Viewer, Reviewer, or Contributor. A Contributor enjoys full access, which covers managing files and notes, contributing fresh content, and deleting or editing whatever already exists inside the PDF Space.

Learn more about collaborating on PDF Spaces.

Bring up the invitation email and select Open PDF Space. Sign in afterward if you're asked to.

Note

A PDF Space set to public access can open with no sign‑in needed. When a PDF Space is restricted to an organization or shared privately, anyone receiving it has to sign in first.

Take a look at the PDF Space and interact with it. The things you can do:

- Walk through the Overview to grasp what the PDF Space is for and how it's laid out
- See and engage with every piece of shared content, including files, audio summaries, podcasts, and notes
- Read through comments and notes already present
- Put questions or summary requests to the AI Assistant
- Grab the PDF Space link and pass it along to others

Note

Run into content in a file that's offensive or inappropriate? Select More options > Report abuse and complete the form so it gets flagged for review.

Look over the project files. The things you can do:

- Open every shared file and read it.
- Move from one file to another within the project.
- See comments and notes already present.

Note

Run into content in a file that's offensive or inappropriate? Select More options > Report abuse and complete the form so it gets flagged for review.

(Reviewer or Contributor access only) Go over the content and attach comments or annotations.

Bring up a file or note, highlight the text with the Select text tool on the Quick action toolbar, and then settle on an action in the floating toolbar:

- Add a comment
- Highlight text
- Strikethrough text
- Underline text
- Copy text
- Save as note

Note

Anyone with project access sees every comment. A Comments icon marks any file or note that carries comments, and selecting it lets you read them and reply.

(Contributor access only) Handle the notes and look after the project files:

- Add notes: From the left pane, choose Notes, after which you select Create a note.
- Add files: Either drop them onto the Files panel, or reach for Select a file to bring in whatever belongs there.
- Rename files: Go to More options > Rename file.
- Remove files or notes: Use More options > Remove file.

Tip

Taking a file out of the PDF Space leaves the original untouched.
