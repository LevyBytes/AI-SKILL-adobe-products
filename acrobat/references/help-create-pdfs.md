## About presentations in Adobe Acrobat

Take a look at how the Generate presentation tool in Adobe Acrobat turns prompts, files, and whole PDF Spaces into polished, eye-catching presentations within minutes.

Pairing generative AI with Adobe Express design capabilities, the Generate presentation tool in Adobe Acrobat assembles structured, ready-to-edit presentations out of straightforward prompts or documents you already have. Be it a sales pitch, a business proposal, or a university presentation you're gearing up for, the tool helps you turn out presentations that read clearly and stay visually consistent.

### How the tool works

Your ideas or documents get turned into refined, ready-to-edit slides in short order by the Generate presentation tool in Adobe Acrobat. Hand it a prompt or upload some files, and out comes a structured presentation carrying smart layouts and visuals. Building slides by hand isn't necessary. Spell out what you're after, then customize and refine. Learn how to create an AI-powered presentation.

Copyright compliance for AI features in Acrobat: Creating, uploading, or sharing content that infringes on third-party copyrights is prohibited. If you’re unsure whether your content violates copyright, seek legal advice before proceeding. Learn more about content usage and data handling.

### Access and availability

- Access points: Both the web and desktop apps surface the presentation tool, yet launching it on the desktop hands you off to the web interface, since generation happens in the cloud. Entry is possible from the homepage, the Create tab, PDF Spaces, and while viewing a document.
- Languages & regions: Offered in the supported languages across the countries that carry Adobe Acrobat.
- Availability: The Generate presentation feature is reachable by Individual, Teams, and Enterprise subscribers whose plan covers Acrobat Studio, AI Assistant, or Acrobat AI capabilities. We keep the option open to charge generative credits for the Generate presentation feature down the road and may revise usage limits whenever we choose.

Support for additional languages and regions is being rolled out by Adobe in stages.

### Ways to begin

There are a few starting points for building a presentation:

- Text prompts only: Begin from a blank slate by defining the audience, the length, and the structure. The prompt can run as long as 1,000 words.
- File uploads: Bring in your material across several formats — PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and web links — then spell out the presentation you have in mind. As many as 10 documents are allowed, totaling 150 pages.
- PDF Spaces: Kick off a presentation straight from your PDF Space. Every file gets analyzed, the tool draws out the key insights, and editable slide content is produced.

Key elements

### Visual sources in generated presentations

The visuals that show up in a generated presentation may come from these sources:

- Adobe Stock images: Based on each slide's content and theme, the feature proposes Adobe Stock images. Right-click an image and pick Source info to review where it came from and its license.
- Images from your source files: The files you upload can supply images. Turn them off by heading to Customize > Settings and clearing the Include file images checkbox; with it cleared, only Adobe Stock or AI-generated images remain.
- Third-party AI models: For image generation the feature relies on Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (Nano Banana). These visuals show up once the presentation has been built.

### Image usage rights

- Usage of the Generate presentation feature in Adobe Acrobat is capped in certain ways; a small number of free generations may be granted so users can sample the service, and continuing past that requires buying a plan that entitles Acrobat AI Assistant features. Adobe keeps the right to revise usage limits, or to throttle or suspend activity beyond those limits, at any moment.
- The Adobe Stock image library and the usage rights available to Acrobat Studio subscribers match those of Adobe Express Premium users, although the library does not cover every Adobe Stock image.
- Right now there is no extra charge for AI-generated visuals produced with third-party models. That could change later through usage limits or subscription requirements.
