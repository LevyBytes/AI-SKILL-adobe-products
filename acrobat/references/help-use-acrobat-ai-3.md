## Overview of prompt writing for AI Assistant

Get familiar with the ideas behind composing clear, effective prompts for the AI Assistant in Acrobat.

Prompts that are written well lead to summaries that hold up, surface the points that matter, and tighten document text with less back-and-forth. When a prompt is specific and unambiguous, the AI Assistant has an easier time grasping what you're after, which cuts down on rework and yields stronger output.

### Components of a strong prompt

It helps to picture a prompt as instructions you'd hand to a capable assistant: the tighter the brief, the steadier the results. A solid prompt spells out the action to take, the material it draws on, the points the reply should cover, and the shape the reply should take.

Tip

Build dependable, repeatable prompts around these four parts.

Strong prompt = Task + Context + Expectations + Format

Task

Open with a direct instruction. Example: Create a product feedback summary.

Context

Supply the details that steer the result. Example: Based on recent customer reviews.

Expectations (optional)

Lay out what the reply ought to address. Example: Identify key themes, sentiment, and recurring issues.

Format (optional)

State the form the information should take. Example: Provide a concise summary suitable for a meeting.

### Guidelines for improving prompt quality

A handful of habits that make prompts work well in AI Assistant:

- Spell out what you're trying to achieve.
- Favor plain, direct wording.
- Pin down limits like length or the points to concentrate on.
- Supply background where it's useful.
- Tune the tone or who the audience is.
- Split big jobs into smaller pieces.
- Sharpen things over several passes with follow-up prompts.

### Expand responses step by step

To go deeper or tighten a result, keep asking follow-up questions. Every fresh prompt extends the answer before it and nudges the AI Assistant toward output that's more thorough or more specialized.

Example

Prompt A:

- What strategic drivers does [ company ] have that our business unit for enterprise could solve for?

Follow‑up prompt: Prompt B

- Write some discovery-based questions based on these identified strategic drivers.

Follow‑up prompt: Prompt C

- Share more about how [ X ] questions should be asked about the healthcare industry.

### Common mistakes to avoid

Steering clear of the following pitfalls keeps results clearer and more dependable:

- Vagueness: Lean on precise, specific wording so the reply comes back stronger.
- Inappropriate or unethical requests: Stay within the law and policy, and honor what others are entitled to.
- Slang or informal language: Hold a professional tone so you don't draw weak or unsuitable replies.
- Conflicting instructions: Make sure nothing in the prompt contradicts itself or stays unclear.
- Overcomplication: Hold prompts to something simple and within reach.
- Assuming prior knowledge: Hand over the context that's needed and spell out your expectations every time.

When prompts are strong, the AI Assistant reads your intent more reliably and returns answers that are accurate, on point, and well organized.

## AI Assistant chat history

Get to know how chat history behaves in Acrobat AI Assistant and where its limits are.

In Acrobat, chat history holds the prompts you typed and the replies they produced, whether for a single document or for a PDF Space made up of several documents. AI Assistant may also draw on that chat history as context when it answers your prompts.

### Access chat history

You can reach chat history for a given file or PDF Space from the recent files list. That chat history may still carry content from documents that have since been taken out of a PDF Space.

### Understand chat history storage

Where your chat history lives depends on how you access Acrobat:

- For the desktop and mobile apps, the data stays on your own device.
- For Acrobat on the web, it resides in Adobe cloud storage.

### Review export and sharing limits

There's no built-in way to export a chat history or pass it along to someone else. As a workaround, paste any generated responses you want to keep into a text editor by hand.

### Clear chat history

To wipe a chat history, open the Options , menu — this works either for a single document or for a PDF Space. Once cleared, the related prompts, the responses that were generated, and any document overviews all go away with it.

### Review chat history controls

There's no switch for individuals to turn chat history off; that control rests with enterprise admins, who manage it from the Adobe Admin Console.

### Disconnect third-party cloud storage

Disconnect a third-party service such as Google Drive or OneDrive and you lose your way into the files it held — along with any chat history those files had built up inside Acrobat.

## Edit PDFs via chat overview

Find out more about editing PDFs through chat with Acrobat AI Assistant — which tools and actions it covers, and where it's available.

Inside Acrobat, AI Assistant lets you handle PDF tasks just by describing, in everyday language, what you'd like done. It works out what you mean, picks whichever tool or tools fit, and runs them — sparing you from hunting through menus or tracking down a feature on your own. Among its main capabilities:

- Multi-tool workflows: one request alone can set several actions going — take "Compress this file and share it with my team," or this: Add a watermark on pages with pricing information."
- Content and structure awareness: because AI Assistant reads how your document is organized and what it says, it can act on it directly. Point it at a particular section, topic, or detail and you won't have to supply exact page numbers — say, "move the pricing section before the case studies."

Ask it to "Move the pages with the pricing information," for example, and it will pinpoint those pages and request your confirmation before touching anything. And once a supported action has run, you're still free to roll the changes back.

## Edit PDFs via chat not working in Acrobat

Find out how to work through problems that crop up when you edit PDFs by chat in Acrobat AI Assistant.

When a chat-driven edit to a PDF doesn't behave as expected, the cause might trace back to your subscription plan, the platform in play, the type of file, your language settings, or the way the prompt was worded.

Quick checks:

- Confirm you're on Acrobat for desktop (open to all users) or Acrobat on the web (individual users only), with your app language set to English.
- Load your prompt with specifics — page numbers, file names, precise content references, or a description of what's on the page — so Acrobat can pin down the right action. For instance: "Delete pages with financial information."

Where the problem sticks around, match your situation to one of the scenarios below and work through its steps.

### Action is unavailable for the file type

A few file conditions can stop actions from running.

- Check that the file isn't password-protected or otherwise restricted. When it is, strip that protection off before you run a conversational action.
- Check that the file isn't a scanned image lacking OCR. If so, put it through Scan & OCR first, then try your prompt again.
- When a file is out for review or has been signed, certain editing actions may be off the table.

### Prompt is not recognized or returns unexpected results

When AI Assistant can't make sense of your prompt or hands back something unrelated, the wording might be muddled, or the tool you're after may not yet work in conversation.

- Recast your prompt in sharper terms. Rather than "Fix the pages," reach for "Delete the last three pages" or "Rotate page 5 clockwise."
  If your prompt leans on document content, spell it out as fully as you can. Prefer "Remove all pages that contain pricing information" over "Remove those pages." Should AI Assistant miss the pages you mean, fall back on naming the page numbers outright.

- When AI Assistant comes back with guided steps in place of running the action itself, that tool may not support direct execution yet. Use the steps it gives you to finish the task by hand.

### Action is unavailable in your language

Right now, editing PDFs by chat is English-only. Should your Acrobat app sit on a non-English locale, AI Assistant might not manage these tasks at all.

Go to Edit > Preferences > Language (Windows) or Acrobat > Preferences > Language (macOS).

Switch the app's language over to English.

Restart Acrobat and retry your prompt.
