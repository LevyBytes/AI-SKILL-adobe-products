# Assigning AI Assistant to Acrobat and Acrobat Reader

- Credential sign-in for Acrobat Reader for enterprise is something admins can configure.
- Their enterprise ID is what users sign in with to reach the AI Assistant license they've been assigned.
- Sign-in capabilities only come alive once users have been brought into the Admin Console.
- Their enterprise ID is what users sign in with to reach the AI Assistant license they've been assigned.
- Sign-in capabilities only come alive once users have been brought into the Admin Console.
- Holding an enterprise or federated ID, administrators can switch on automatic account creation drawn from an existing directory.
- Following license assignment, an email reaches users carrying a link to open Acrobat Reader on the web and sign in with their enterprise, federated, or business ID.
- Signing in to the desktop build of Acrobat Reader is an option too. See how to download and install the Acrobat Reader desktop.
- For users who aren't signed in, switch on the AI Assistant by updating the registry key where required.
