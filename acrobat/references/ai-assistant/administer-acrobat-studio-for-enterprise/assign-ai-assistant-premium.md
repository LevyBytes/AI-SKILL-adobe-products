# Assigning AI Assistant to Acrobat Premium and Acrobat Reader Premium

- In the Admin Console, admins have to place users on Acrobat Reader Premium licenses.
- Without redeploying the Unified Installer, users on free Reader can sign in and put the AI Assistant to use.
- By default, free Reader asks for no sign-in. To compel sign-in for users granted Reader Premium offers, admins may push a registry key.
- Picture a user on Reader 32-bit handed a Premium offer that has both PDF Services and AI Assistant switched off: sign-in won't go through. Here, only the Unified Installer brings sign-in support.
- With Reader Premium purchases, the paid Acrobat tools sit hidden in the UI.
