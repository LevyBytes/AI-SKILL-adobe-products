# Purchase licenses

Open Acrobat and sign in with an admin account.

Within the AI Assistant for Acrobat section, pick Subscribe now.

When the dialog box shows up, pick Continue.

Already bought the AI Assistant for Acrobat add-on subscription? Then pick Assign a license to hand licenses out to users from the Admin Console.

When the Add license page comes up, enter your payment details, then pick Place secure order.

Pick Refresh on the AI Assistant to verify that your subscription has gone live.
