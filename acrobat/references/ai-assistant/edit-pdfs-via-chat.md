# Edit PDFs via chat

Walk through how to edit PDFs with the AI Assistant chat feature in Adobe Acrobat.

Before you begin: only individual Acrobat Pro and Studio plan users get multi-tool and content-aware prompts; Teams and Enterprise plans aren't covered yet. Look over the supported tools and actions so you know what your plan offers.

With your PDF open in Acrobat, the chat panel comes up when you select AI Assistant from the top bar.

Into the chat field, describe what you need in plain language — Delete the last two pages, say, or Compress this file — or browse the prompt examples for one that matches your task.

From your request, AI Assistant works out the task and reports the executed action back in the chat panel.

Note

When more than one tool is involved, AI Assistant lays out a plan before it acts. Review what it shows, then confirm those actions by typing Yes.

(Optional) To roll the change back, type Undo.

Tip

When an action doesn't behave as you expected, feed your prompt more detail — page numbers or file names, for instance. For tools that chat doesn't yet handle, AI Assistant also offers step-by-step guidance. Should the trouble linger, look over the common issues and fixes.
