# Report inappropriate content

Look over the AI-generated reply you intend to report.

Following the reply, select Dislike.

Within the What went wrong? dialog box, pick one or more reporting categories or type details into the text box.

Reporting lets you flag things like murky information or material touching on violence, copyright, or trademark concerns.
