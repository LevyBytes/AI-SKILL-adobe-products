# Summarize a Zoom meeting transcript

From the left panel on the Acrobat homepage, under Third-party apps, select Add meeting transcripts.

Under Zoom, select Learn more.

Follow the prompts on screen: log in at the Zoom web portal and pull down the meeting transcript.

Download a Zoom meeting transcript and open it in Acrobat.

Download a Zoom meeting transcript and open it in Acrobat.

Choose Select file to pick the transcript and open it in Acrobat.

In the right panel, select Ask AI Assistant or Generative summary.

Go over the AI-generated overview, which carries section headings and the meeting's key points.

Note

A meeting transcript can also be opened in Acrobat on its own. Turn a VTT transcript file into a PDF, then select Ask AI Assistant or Generative summary to build a summary.
