# Generative summary in Acrobat

See how AI-generated summaries in Acrobat make long or intricate PDFs easier to grasp.

A generative summary is an AI-driven capability: it studies a PDF and turns out a short, organized recap of the document's central points. By calling out the major themes and the sections that matter, it lets you get the gist of long or intricate files in a hurry. Lean on that recap to choose which parts deserve a closer read, to set documents side by side, or to gear up for meetings and research.
