# Administer Acrobat Studio for enterprise

Get to know Acrobat Studio for enterprise and the way admins handle licensing, access, and security.

Bundled into Acrobat Studio are Acrobat Pro, PDF tools, Acrobat AI Assistant, PDF Spaces, and fresh creation capabilities driven by Adobe Express.

By gathering content from PDFs, Microsoft 365 files, slides, and links into one AI-powered workspace, PDF Spaces makes your work flow more smoothly. It carries generative AI features onto web, desktop, and mobile platforms, paired with centralized license management and enterprise-level support. Through AI Assistants such as Analyst, Instructor, or ones you tailor yourself, teams pick up instant insights and ready-made starting points, including summaries, outlines, and draft reports, which keeps them moving quickly and on the same page.

The design tools in Adobe Express let teams turn out on-brand content without much effort. Templates, brand kits, and straightforward image editing tools make staying consistent, standing out, and moving faster simple.

Among the main facets of deployment are:

- Deploying licenses for Acrobat Studio
- Managing access to Acrobat Studio
- Managing user access and licensing
- Setting identity types and authentication
- Data handling and security
