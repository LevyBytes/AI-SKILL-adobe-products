## Generative AI overview

See how the generative AI built into Acrobat apps lets you make sense of documents fast, pull answers to your questions, and produce content with less effort.

Inside Acrobat apps, generative AI reshapes the way you handle documents: smart tools sit alongside your files to speed up comprehension, surface insights, and spin up fresh material. The result is less time spent and more done across the document tasks you run every day.

### About generative AI in Acrobat apps

The term generative AI covers a set of artificial intelligence capabilities that read what's in a document and, from that content, produce new information that fits the context. Where conventional document tools only put information on screen, generative AI goes further — reading it, condensing it, fielding questions about it, and even drafting fresh content from what your documents contain.

## Generative AI technical requirements

Get to know the generative AI technical requirements so you stay within the file rules, usage limits, and sign-in conditions that apply to your Acrobat app.

### Supported file languages

- Acrobat apps on desktop and web: Files qualify for generative AI features only when they're in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, or Japanese.
- AI Assistant for webpages: Coverage extends only to English-language webpages.

### Supported languages for GenAI features

- Brazilian Portuguese only

### File requirements

- Keep the file under 100 MB in size.
- Stay within a ceiling of 600 pages per file.
- Steer clear of password protection or usage restrictions on the PDF.
- The file can't be a PDF Portfolio.

Note

Caps on PDF file size and page count come from the server settings, and they shift on their own whenever those server settings change.

### Usage limitations

- Keep prompts or questions under 500 characters.
- Hold text selections in AI Assistant under 8000 characters. Whatever you select has to sit on one page — it can't run across several.
- Images and intricate vector graphics fall outside what AI Assistant handles.
- For webpages, AI Assistant shows up only on English-language pages that carry well-structured, readable long-form content. Even on pages that look long, the AI Assistant button might stay hidden.

### Account and login requirements

- An active internet connection is required.
- Generative AI features have to be switched on.
- Sign-in through a supported Adobe ID is required.

### App settings

- Set the language of the Adobe Acrobat, Acrobat Reader, and Adobe Scan applications to one of the supported choices: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, or Japanese.
  - On Windows, select Menu > Enable new Acrobat.
  - On macOS, select View > Enable new Acrobat.

### Supported devices and browsers

Every GenAI feature works on these versions and up:

- iOS: Version 26.02.50 and later
- Android: Version 26.2.0.43379 and later
- Desktop: 25.001.21288 and later
- Acrobat on the web: Refer to the Technical requirements for Acrobat on the web

Note

After June 1, 2026, GenAI features will no longer be supported on older versions.

## Generative summary in Acrobat

See how AI-generated summaries in Acrobat make long or intricate PDFs easier to grasp.

A generative summary is an AI-driven capability: it studies a PDF and turns out a short, organized recap of the document's central points. By calling out the major themes and the sections that matter, it lets you get the gist of long or intricate files in a hurry. Lean on that recap to choose which parts deserve a closer read, to set documents side by side, or to gear up for meetings and research.

### Common use cases

Typical scenarios for generative summary include:

- Working through lengthy reports or case studies to pull out the key points.
- Trimming research papers down to gauge whether they're relevant.
- Skimming policy or reference material ahead of a thorough read.
- Building context before meetings, research, or reviews.

### Summary generation for long PDFs

For documents that run long or get complicated, generative summary works in stages. As each finished section becomes available, its summary shows up while processing carries on across the rest of the file.

### Advantages of real-time summaries

- Summary sections become available early, as soon as they're generated.
- The view refreshes in real time while analysis of the document keeps going.
- Waiting shrinks even for very large or highly detailed PDFs.
- Moving around the document stays easy while processing is still underway.

Open Adobe Acrobat and sign in to your account.

Note

### Review the status of your request

Want to know whether your access request went through? Open Acrobat and pick Refresh on the AI Assistant.

- When it's approved, Acrobat provisions your license on its own, and a confirmation email lands in your inbox.
- When it's denied, an email arrives spelling out why. Feel free to file a fresh request with a fuller reason.

A request may sit pending for as long as 60 days. Notifications reach you at the 30-day mark and once more at 60 days if it lapses. You can't file a pending request again until it expires.

### Deploying licenses for Acrobat Studio

Licensing requirements

Because AI Assistant leans on cloud-based services tied to a user's identity, it calls for a Named User license of Acrobat. To hand both out to users, the Acrobat license and the AI Assistant license have to show up within the same Admin Console. Organizations buying through the VIP buying program need both licenses tied to one and the same VIP account.

Deploy Acrobat Studio by way of the Admin Console. Ahead of deployment, confirm that Acrobat is up to date and set to one of the supported languages. Consult the basic configuration settings.

Bring users into the Admin Console through any supported method, the bulk upload method included. After the necessary product profiles exist, you can hand Acrobat Studio straight to users. For specifics, consult Packaging apps via the Admin Console.

Once Acrobat Studio has been assigned, users must restart Acrobat before the features open up.

### Managing access to Acrobat Studio

Through assignment rules in the Adobe Admin Console, IT admins can govern access to Acrobat Studio much as they would when handing out Acrobat Premium, Pro, or Standard licenses. Every license goes to one single user.

The Product Profiles for Acrobat Premium for enterprise, Acrobat Studio for enterprise, and Acrobat Reader Premium for enterprise are all open to admin customization. On the Acrobat Premium side, the services you can configure are: PDF Services, AI Assistant in Acrobat, and AI Assistant Chat History on Cloud for Acrobat Premium and Reader Premium.

When it comes to Acrobat Studio for enterprises, the services available to configure are PDF Services, AI Assistant in Acrobat, AI Assistant Chat History on Cloud, and Adobe Express. Read on about Product Assignments.

### Automatic provisioning

Through predefined rules, admins can have licenses assigned to eligible users automatically. Set it up by heading to Admin Console > Products > Products access automation > Automatic assignment rules.

### Managing user access and licensing using Product Profiles About Product Profiles

- Licenses get tied to individual users through Product Profiles, which open up Acrobat Studio features and cloud services.
- One user can sit in more than one Product Profile.
- Every addition of a user to a Product Profile uses up a license. Take a user placed in two profiles that each carry an Acrobat Pro license: that's two licenses consumed.

Make the most of your licenses by building Product Profiles around user roles and placing users only in the profiles they actually require.

### Setting identity types and authentication

To handle licensing and services, Adobe runs an identity management system that authenticates and authorizes users. Signing in happens through an email-based ID. These are the identity types it supports:

- Federated ID: Run by the organization. Tied to the enterprise directory through SAML2 Identity Provider (IdP) single Sign-On.
- Enterprise ID: Run by the organization and hosted by Adobe, where Adobe carries out the authentication.
- Business ID: Run by the organization. Sole authority to create and manage the user accounts stays with the organization.
- Adobe ID: Run by the end user, where Adobe carries out the authentication.

When accounts are run by the organization, getting to the AI Assistant calls for a federated ID or an enterprise ID. Read on about managing Adobe profiles.

### Data handling and security

Within Acrobat AI Assistant and Acrobat Studio, robust safeguards govern how data is handled and protected. Every capability — those that lean on third-party LLMs included — operates under Adobe's Responsible AI Ethics governance, which rests on the pillars of accountability, responsibility, and transparency. Further detail is available in our blog.

- Through the Adobe Privacy Policy, a request for early deletion can be submitted.
- Where in-app feedback is turned on, enterprises can rate AI responses by clicking like or dislike buttons.
- Any creation workflow that runs on Adobe Express writes its file to Adobe cloud storage.

While data is in transit, Adobe applies HTTPS encryption with TLS 1.2 or higher across all traffic. Data that has been stored is encrypted by the Acrobat generative AI service with SHA-256.

For the particulars of how the feature works — covering data handling, storage, processing, data flow, and encryption — consult our Security factsheet.
