## Create agreement templates

### See how to build agreement templates ready to be sent out for signatures.

Reusable agreement templates are simple to put together with Acrobat on the web, which smooths out your document workflow so e-signatures move faster and stay consistent. Where standardized contracts and agreements are a recurring need, agreement templates fit the bill.

Starting from the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose E-sign > Create e-sign template.

Note

Customers on enterprise plans get a prompt to be redirected to Acrobat Sign so they can reach the Create e-sign template feature. Choose Continue to keep going.

Should the RCreate e-sign template feature be out of reach for you, the likely cause is that your administrator turned it off. Contact your administrator to have access granted.

Once the Create a Library Template page appears, type a name for the template.

The Create a Library Template page is where you attach a file for a template and choose the template type you want.

The Create a Library Template page is where you attach a file for a template and choose the template type you want.

Choose Add Files, then pick the document you'll base the template on.

Beneath Template Type, choose one of the following options, or Both if that fits:

- Reusable document: Pick this to build a document carrying form fields that you find yourself sending often.
- Reusable form field layer: Holds form fields at set positions that can be laid over any agreement. When you preview an agreement, you can apply whichever form field layer template you want.

For Who can use this template, choose the option that fits — templates can be made just for you or for a group you're part of.

Choose Preview & Add Fields.

Form fields on the document get detected on their own, and a message like the one in the image below appears. Choose Place All Fields.

After the fields land, you're asked to confirm both the fields and where they sit.

When your form fields don't get detected automatically, simply carry on to the following steps and drag and drop the fields you need by hand.

When your form fields don't get detected automatically, simply carry on to the following steps and drag and drop the fields you need by hand.

Within the document preview pane, pull fields out of the tabs on the right pane and drop them wherever you want on the document.

Open one of the Field types drop-down menus, then drag the field onto the spot you want on the document.

Open one of the Field types drop-down menus, then drag the field onto the spot you want on the document.

Choose Save.

With that, the e-sign template exists and is held in Adobe cloud storage.

Tip

Every template you've made lately shows up in the Recent section of the Home page. The full set of your templates lives under Documents > Templates.

## Create PDFs using legal templates

### Reach for the Acrobat business templates library when you need to draft business agreements of many kinds — non-disclosure agreements, proposals, offer letters, and similar documents.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose E-Sign > Use a Legal template.

Note

Only Acrobat Pro Plus subscribers and a subset of Acrobat Pro customers get access to Acrobat business templates.

Look up the kind of template you want, or pick one straight from the Featured Templates list.

The search bar lets you track down whichever template fits your need.

Hit Get started on the template's preview page.

After a template is chosen, a sequence of questions guides you through building the legal document.

Answer the contract details that are required, then choose Continue.

Enter responses for every detail asked afterward, then choose Continue.

Should you want to revise an earlier answer, Back takes you there.

Note

Attempting to leave the template builder triggers a prompt asking whether to delete or save the draft. Either keep it in Adobe cloud storage or discard it. Whatever you save lands in the In Progress section, ready to be reopened and finished at any time.

After every query has an answer, choose Complete.

A PDF document is generated from the template at that point. The following actions are available on your legal document:

- Drop a company logo or image onto the top of the document.
- Revise the text, bring in images, or rearrange the document.
- Send it to the right recipients to request e-signatures on the document.

After the legal document exists, a company logo can be added and the document sent off for signature.

## Manage agreement templates

### Walk through how agreement templates are managed.

Acrobat on the web gives you the means to handle agreement templates well, keeping your document processes consistent and efficient.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Documents.

In the left pane, choose Acrobat Sign.

The Documents tab is where your files, shared documents, starred items, and agreements can all be organized, shared, and managed.

The Documents tab is where your files, shared documents, starred items, and agreements can all be organized, shared, and managed.

On the Acrobat Sign page, choose Manage > Templates.

When a template's form fields need changing, hover over it, choose Edit, then adjust the fields however you need.

To look at or change any template, find it in the templates listing and select it.

To look at or change any template, find it in the templates listing and select it.

Attaching a note to a template means hovering over it, choosing Add Notes, and jotting down a personal reminder for yourself. When you're finished, choose Save Note.

The Actions menu opens up these extra options:

- Remove the template entirely
- Grab the template PDF as a download
- Pull down the template's individual files

Beneath the template's name in the right pane, you can read its details — type, owner, creation date, and so on.

Beneath the template's name in the right pane, you can read its details — type, owner, creation date, and so on.

## Design documents using Express templates

### Find out how to design fresh pages and slot them into your PDFs in Acrobat on the web by tapping into the built-in Adobe Express app.

Right inside the Acrobat on the web interface, the richer design tools of Adobe Express let you build pages that look great.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Edit > Insert pages.

Pick Select files and browse your device to upload whichever file should receive the new pages.

When the next page appears, choose Design a new page.

Choosing the 'Design a new page' option prompts Acrobat to upload the file to cloud storage and launch it within Adobe Express on a fresh browser tab.

Choosing the 'Design a new page' option prompts Acrobat to upload the file to cloud storage and launch it within Adobe Express on a fresh browser tab.

Out of the template list shown in Adobe Express, choose whichever one you want.

A wide assortment of templates is available in Adobe Express, where your designs can be tailored to taste.

A wide assortment of templates is available in Adobe Express, where your designs can be tailored to taste.

When the dialog box appears, choose Add as pages.

After an Express template is designed, it can be folded into your existing file so a new file takes shape.

After an Express template is designed, it can be folded into your existing file so a new file takes shape.

Adjust the template however you like, and if you wish, set a Color theme for your PDF by choosing Theme from the main menu.

The page built in Adobe Express joins the document. That refreshed document saves itself automatically under Recent on the Acrobat on the web homepage. You'll also find the refreshed document inside Your stuff within Adobe Express.
