# Walk through handling your Adobe Acrobat PDF Pack account.

A package of Adobe's online services, Acrobat PDF Pack gives you the means to create, convert, combine, compress, and organize PDF files. Word and Excel files can be produced from PDFs, several documents can be merged, and PDFs can be shared and signed securely no matter the device. Reachable from web browsers and mobile apps, Acrobat PDF Pack runs across a range of operating systems while keeping your documents safeguarded by industry-standard security measures.

Sign in to Acrobat on the web using your Adobe ID along with your password.

Note

A social account — Google or Facebook — also works for signing in.

Click the profile icon in the top-right corner, then choose Adobe Account.

Choose Plans and payment > Plans.

Within the Plan information section, choose Manage Plan.

Adjust your subscription however you need to in order to reach extra features.

Whatever you change on your Acrobat PDF Pack subscription syncs to your account on its own.
