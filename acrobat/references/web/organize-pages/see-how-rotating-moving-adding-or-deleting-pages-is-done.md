# See how rotating, moving, adding, or deleting pages is done.

Straight from your browser, Acrobat on the web makes it simple to tidy up a PDF — shuffle, spin, or strip out pages with no fuss.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, pick Edit > Organize pages.

Pick Select files, then browse your device to bring in the file.

Tidy up your PDF by carrying out whichever of these you need:

- Reordering pages means grabbing a thumbnail and dragging it where you want it.
- Spinning a page is done by hovering on its thumbnail and choosing Rotate page left or Rotate page right .
- Removing a page happens when you hover on its thumbnail and pick Delete .
- Bringing in more pages starts with Insert pages , after which you point to the file supplying them.
- Building a fresh page for the PDF begins by picking Design a new page.

The top bar's Organize pages tools cover rotating, deleting, adding, or designing new pages.

Rotating, deleting, adding, or designing pages is all handled by the Organize pages tools sitting in the top bar.

Pick Save.

Tip

Reverse any edits by picking from the top bar.

The PDF pages settle into their new order, and the file saves itself to Adobe cloud storage automatically.
