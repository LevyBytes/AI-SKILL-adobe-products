# Learn how to hide an agreement.

Keep your document dashboard tidy by tucking away agreements that have gone inactive or that you no longer need, which leaves you with a cleaner, more focused workspace.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Documents.

Over in the left pane, choose All agreements.

Pick out the agreement you'd like to hide.

Over in the right pane under Actions, choose Hide.

A dialog box opens; in it, choose Hide.

Any document can be tucked out of your agreement list by way of its Actions menu.

With that, the agreement drops out of your view. Unhide the document whenever you want it back among the listed documents.
