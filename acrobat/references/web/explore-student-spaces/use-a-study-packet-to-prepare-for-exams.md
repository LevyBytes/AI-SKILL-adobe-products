# Use a Study packet to prepare for exams

See how a Study packet in Student Spaces takes your materials and shapes them into a goal-driven plan for learning.

Think of a Study packet as a tidy, intelligent learning plan aimed at one specific target — an exam, a midterm, or some deadline-bound assignment. Drawing on the sources you've uploaded, it pulls study guides, flashcards, and practice quizzes together and arranges them around your topics, the depth you want, and your schedule.

Rather than facing all the material in one sitting, a study packet splits dense content into focused sessions, so it's clear what to cover, when to cover it, and how to test your grasp along the way.

Head into Student Spaces, where a study packet can be built one of these two ways:

- From an existing Student Space: Pull up one where your sources are already loaded, then over on the Overview page hit Study packet in the Study tools menu.
- From new source material: From the Student Spaces homepage, hit Study packet, send in your documents, and fill in the details it asks for to kick things off.

When the Tell us a little more page appears, type in the topic you want to zero in on, pick a Learning level, lock in a Goal date, and then select Generate to build your study packet.

Spell out your focus, learning level, and deadline clearly, and the resulting study packet will line up more closely with how you learn and the time you've got.

Crack open the study packet you generated, and inside you'll find structured study sessions, each holding a study guide, flashcards, and a practice quiz.

Within each session sit a study guide, flashcards, and a quiz together, so it's clear what comes next and how to test your grasp.

From the study packet index, open a study session to go over the main topics and confirm the details against the linked source references.

Test how well it's sticking by going through the flashcards and sitting the practice quiz.

Keep tabs on where you stand through the Study Sessions list — each session displays its status and lets you pick back up with the guide, flashcards, or quiz.

The More actions menu is where you fine-tune and manage a Study packet — renaming it, saving it down for offline study, or sweeping away the stale ones.

Tip

The Like and Dislike options let you pass along feedback that shapes future study packets.
