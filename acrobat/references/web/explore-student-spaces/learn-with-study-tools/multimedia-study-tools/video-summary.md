# Video summary

Through visuals and narration, video summaries lay out your study materials plainly, so ideas land more easily and stick. After generation, video summaries surface as tiles in the Study Tools section, keeping them easy to spot and return to while you study. Open a Video summary tile and the video player starts up.

Playback and viewing options

- Drive the playback through play/pause, rewind, and fast‑forward
- Dial the speed anywhere from 0.5x to 2x
- Shift the volume whenever you need
- Blow the video up to full view, or pop it out so you can concentrate

Renaming or deleting video summaries is possible, as is downloading them for offline use and sharing feedback through the Like or Dislike options.

Grab a quick, visual rundown of your study materials to get the key topics down before going deeper into the sources.
