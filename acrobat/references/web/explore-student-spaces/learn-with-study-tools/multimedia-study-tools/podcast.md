# Podcast

What Podcasts do is convert your study materials into audio summaries, letting you go over key concepts with no reading required. A generated Podcast lands as a tile in the Study Tools section, so coming back to it anytime is easy. Open the Podcast tile and the audio player starts up.

Listening controls

- Start or halt the audio whenever you need
- Jump ahead or back while it plays
- Tune the playback speed to whatever suits your ear
- Set the volume to a level that's comfortable

The transcript can be shown or hidden; while the audio runs, the matching text lights up in sync.

The More actions menu is where you rename, delete, or download your podcast. Feedback, too, can go straight from the player by way of the Like or Dislike options.

An audio highlight paired with a transcript walks you through the main ideas in your materials, so you can stay in step with the playback or jump straight to a particular point.
