# Turn your notes into a Study guide

See how to build tight, per-subject study guides that map out what to cover, push the key topics to the front, and gauge whether you're ready.

When the clock is against you, the Study guide tool in Student Spaces takes your notes, slides, readings, and past papers and shapes them into one focused plan. Build a single guide for each subject so you can rank the topics, settle on how deeply each one needs studying, and recognize the moment you're ready to move past it. Whether you're prepping for an exam, cramming at the last minute, or making up ground after missing class, it spares you from rereading everything end to end.

Head into Student Spaces and pick a starting point:

- From your existing materials: Pull up a Student Space where your sources are already loaded — or spin up a fresh Student Space — and then hit Study guide.
- From a topic only: Over in the Tools section of the Student Spaces homepage, hit Study guide, then nail down the topic and how deep to go to kick things off.

Tip

Keep the study guide tight by sticking to one subject at a time and pulling in everything that bears on it — lecture PDFs, slides, notes, readings, links, or past papers.

Pull up the study guide you generated so you can look it over and map out your studying:

- Start with the Introduction, which lays out what the guide takes on and why that matters
- Turn to the Table of Contents to spot the topics worth prioritizing and figure out your starting point

Lean on the study guide to go over topics at whatever depth fits, dig into the cited sections, and run a quick quiz to confirm you've got it.

Work through the topic explanation to ground the concept in context, then click the cited links to confirm the source or go deeper into the original material.

Lean on the Key Takeaways and Study Tips to single out the concepts that matter most and learn the usual way they get tested.

Give the Suggested quick exercises a go to confirm your grasp and lock in what you've learned while it's still fresh in mind.

The More actions menu is where you tune and manage the study guide. From there you can:

- Give the guide a name that lines up with your course or chapter
- Pull it down for studying offline
- Clear out guides that have gone stale

Tip

The Like and Dislike options let you pass along feedback that shapes future guides.
