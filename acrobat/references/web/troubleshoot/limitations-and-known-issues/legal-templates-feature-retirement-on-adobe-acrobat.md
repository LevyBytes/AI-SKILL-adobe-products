# Legal Templates feature retirement on Adobe Acrobat

Find answers to common questions surrounding the end of the Legal Templates feature on Adobe Acrobat.

As of January 10, 2025, Adobe Acrobat will stop offering the Legal Templates feature. For anyone on Acrobat for desktop, be aware that this feature could become inaccessible as soon as December 5, 2024.

Should you need the Legal Templates feature in the window between December 5, 2024, and January 10, 2025, reach it straight from Acrobat on the web.

Every document you finish and save by way of Legal Templates on or before January 10, 2025, stays available as a PDF in your Adobe cloud storage. On Acrobat on desktop they live under Your files; on Acrobat on the web, check the Documents area.

Templates left in draft, not completed and saved within Acrobat, will be gone after January 10th. Keeping access to those in-progress drafts in Acrobat means finishing them and saving them as PDFs ahead of January 10.

Past January 10, unlimited tailored legal documents remain something you can create through Rocket Lawyer, our template partner.
