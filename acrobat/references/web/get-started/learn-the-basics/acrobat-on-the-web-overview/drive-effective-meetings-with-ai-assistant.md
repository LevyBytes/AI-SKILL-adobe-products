# Drive effective meetings with AI Assistant

Anyone running projects, heading a team, or handling admin duties can lean on Acrobat on the web to capture meeting notes cleanly, hand out action items, and keep tabs on the decisions that matter. Through Acrobat for Microsoft Teams, your notes go straight into a PDF as the meeting runs, and the AI Assistant ties itself to particular talking points while floating follow-up questions. There's also the Generative summary tool for drafting meeting outlines that call out the headline points and the choices made. That tidied-up document — action items and deadlines included — can then go out to everyone in the room. To lock in accountability, participants apply an e-signature confirming they got it and are on the hook for their action items.

Going about it this way sharpens how accurate and complete the meeting record is, smooths follow-up through AI-driven insight, trims time thanks to hands-off summaries and action item lists, and nails down accountability by way of e-signature confirmations.
