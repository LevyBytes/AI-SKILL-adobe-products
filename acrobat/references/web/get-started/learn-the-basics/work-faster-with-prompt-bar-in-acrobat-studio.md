# Work faster with prompt bar in Acrobat Studio

On the Acrobat Studio homepage, the prompt bar pulls the AI help, search, and creation power of Acrobat and Adobe Express together into a single tidy area. Forget hunting through menus — just state what you're after. Type a plain everyday request, and from there the tool points you toward the right actions, answers, or tools, so your intent becomes a result with fewer steps along the way.
