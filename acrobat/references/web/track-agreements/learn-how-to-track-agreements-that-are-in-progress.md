# Learn how to track agreements that are in progress.

Open Documents from the Acrobat on the web homepage.

In the left pane, choose Shared by you.

Tick the checkbox beside the agreement you'd like to follow within the document list.

Details about that agreement appear in the right pane, where the following items are surfaced:

- Agreement status
- Expiration date
- Date and time of creation
- Activity status of each recipient
- Activity report of the agreement

Lean on what the right pane shows to keep tabs on your agreements. Past activity is reachable through the Activity drop-down menu.
