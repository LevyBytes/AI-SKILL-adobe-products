# Customize the AI-generated audio summary

Rather than starting the audio over from scratch, polish it by reworking its transcript.

Within the Audio summary, choose Edit to change what recipients will hear.

Work right inside the transcript to rephrase, supply extra context, or strike items out.

To drop in a fresh item, choose Add content and then type your text.

The audio overview is yours to tailor and pass along, letting people skim the headline takeaways drawn from several documents at once.

For anything you'd rather leave out, choose Delete on that item.

Once your edits are ready, choose Update to push them through and refresh the audio summary.
