# Customize the theme and link preview

Decide both the visual style of your PDF Space and the way it shows up when you hand it off as a link.

From the left panel, choose Customize.

Swapping the theme means picking a color scheme or background that mirrors your brand or your material.

Drop in a color theme to give your PDF Space a personal touch.

Swapping out the link preview starts with choosing Replace image and uploading the picture you want — nudge its position, zoom, or rotation to taste, then choose Save.

Shut the Customize panel.

What you end up with is a PDF Space that reads like a designed story, letting recipients grasp, listen to, and dig into the things that count most.
