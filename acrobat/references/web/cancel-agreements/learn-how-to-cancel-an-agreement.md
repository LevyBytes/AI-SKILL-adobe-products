# Learn how to cancel an agreement.

Handling your document transactions is straightforward: cancel agreements right from within the Acrobat on the web tools. Doing so stops the signing process in its tracks and lets everyone involved know.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Documents.

Over in the left pane, choose All agreements.

Pick out the agreement you'd like to cancel.

Over in the right pane under Actions, choose Cancel.

A dialog box opens; in it, choose Cancel Agreement.

Want the recipients told that the agreement was canceled? Tick the 'Notify recipients' checkbox.

With that, the agreement is canceled and slips out of reach for the recipients. The canceled ones are listed under Documents > Agreements > Canceled.
