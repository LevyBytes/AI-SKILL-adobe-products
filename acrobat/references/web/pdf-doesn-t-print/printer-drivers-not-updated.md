# Printer drivers not updated

To keep pace with the newest software, including fresh releases of Acrobat Reader and Acrobat, manufacturers routinely refresh their drivers.

Pin down the model number of your printer.

Visit the manufacturer's site and look up drivers or printer drivers.

Track down and download the driver that matches your model number.

Stick to the manufacturer's directions to install the driver.

Reboot your system and have another go at printing the PDF.
