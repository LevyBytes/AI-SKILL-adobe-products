# Generate images from text

See how the Adobe Express tools build images for you to drop into your PDF documents.

From the Adobe Acrobat homepage, choose Create > Generate an image.

Within the Prompt section, type a thorough description of whatever image you have in mind.

Choose Generate.

Shape the appearance and arrangement of your image with the tools along the left panel:

- Choose Image size to settle on a format such as Square, Portrait, Landscape, or Widescreen.
- Choose Content type to land on a category such as Photograph, Digital Art, or Illustration.
- Reach for the Styles options to lay on an artistic style such as Realistic, Abstract, or Cartoon.

For the strongest results, make your prompt vivid and thorough, spelling out the elements, style, colors, and composition you want.

Look over the four AI-generated image options, then choose the one you like best.

Choose Download.

Back on the Acrobat homepage, choose Upload files, pick the image you just downloaded, and it opens in Acrobat. From there the left-panel tools let you go further — dropping the image into your PDF Space, turning it into a PDF, sharing it, or merging it with other files.

The left-panel tools open up further actions on the image you generated from text.
