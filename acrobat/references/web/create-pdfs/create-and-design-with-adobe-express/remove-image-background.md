# Remove image background

Find out how Adobe Express editing tools inside Acrobat strip backgrounds away from images.

From the Adobe Acrobat homepage, choose Create > Remove image background.

When the dialog box appears, pick the image you'd like to edit.

Within the Adobe Express image editor, take a look at the image now that its background is gone.

Once the background is gone, the blue handles let you resize, stretch, or rotate your image.

To polish how your image looks without its background:

- Resize or reangle it by dragging the edges or corner handles.
- Crop the image to zero in on a particular area.
- Lift its look by tuning brightness, contrast, and saturation.

In the top-right corner, choose Download (or Save) to store your edited image.

Bring the image into Acrobat and turn it into a PDF, which makes it simple to edit, reuse across documents, or pass along to others.
