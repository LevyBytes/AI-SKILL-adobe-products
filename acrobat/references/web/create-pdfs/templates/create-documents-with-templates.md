# Create documents with templates

Find out how Adobe Express templates in Acrobat on Android help you put together documents along with social posts.

Note

For now this feature runs only within Acrobat on Android; iOS does not support it.

Head to Acrobat on Android and pick Create from the bottom menu.

Under the Create from a template section, settle on a template type and pick a template.

Pick Add text and type your content into the text box.

Reach for the template editing tools to tailor your design and turn out professional documents or social media posts.

Lean on the formatting options to set the font, size, color, or alignment.

Pick Add Image, settle on an image from your device or library, and drop it onto the template. If you like, resize or reposition it with the handles.

To put a theme on your template, pick Themes, then settle on a theme style, such as Bold, Classic, or Pastel.

Your template's color scheme refreshes to match the chosen theme.

Pick a color theme to tailor your template's look.

Pick the checkmark to lock in your choice.

The top menu gives you options to download or share your document; you can also return to it afterward through the homepage's Recent list.
