# About presentations in Adobe Acrobat

Take a look at how the Generate presentation tool in Adobe Acrobat turns prompts, files, and whole PDF Spaces into polished, eye-catching presentations within minutes.

Pairing generative AI with Adobe Express design capabilities, the Generate presentation tool in Adobe Acrobat assembles structured, ready-to-edit presentations out of straightforward prompts or documents you already have. Be it a sales pitch, a business proposal, or a university presentation you're gearing up for, the tool helps you turn out presentations that read clearly and stay visually consistent.
