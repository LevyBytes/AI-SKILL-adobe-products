# Elements that shape your presentation

Your AI-generated presentation can be refined and personalized so it ends up polished, visually appealing, and a good fit for your audience.

Key elements

- Audience: Tailor the material to whoever is watching. Choose among options such as executives or technical specialists, or define an audience of your own.
- Length & detail: Dial in the number of slides and how deep the coverage goes, whether you want a fast overview or a thorough treatment.
- Template: Choose a visual style, which remains changeable afterward.

Enhancements

- Recreate slide: Revise the content and have AI rework the slide, matching layouts and visuals to whatever you changed.
- Edit page: Localize through translation, resize for social posts or infographics, and layer in animations, captions, or rephrased wording to sharpen the storytelling.
- Manual editing in Adobe Express: Refine the wording and layout, drop in charts, tables, and AI-generated images, and switch to fresh design templates for a new look.
