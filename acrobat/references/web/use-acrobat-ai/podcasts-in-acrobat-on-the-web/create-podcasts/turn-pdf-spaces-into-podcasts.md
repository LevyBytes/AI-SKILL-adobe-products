# Turn PDF Spaces into podcasts

Once the podcast is made, the transcript is yours to edit — revise the sections already there, tack on new ones, or polish the narration — so the podcast comes out exactly how you want it.

Open the PDF Space, pick Podcasts from the left menu, and then pick Create a podcast.

To shape your podcast, type in your topic, focus, or audience, settle on a format, and then pick Generate.

Before you generate it, you can steer the podcast with a prompt of your own and decide how the content gets summarized.

Once the podcast is made, pick Play to hear it.

Pick the podcast to bring up the transcript and play it with controls.

Use the Playback speed and Volume

controls to tune the settings as you like.

Hear the podcast in whichever format you prefer. To play it while you are out and about, scan the QR code sitting at the foot of the Podcasts pane.

To revise the transcript, hover over any section and pick Edit, change the text or insert a new section as needed, and then pick Update to lock in your changes.

Revising the transcript changes the podcast content without rebuilding it. You can swiftly polish wording, fold in context, or fine-tune the flow ahead of sharing.

Pick Hide transcript to stop showing the transcript.

Tip

Reach for Provide feedback to score the podcast and pass along comments that help sharpen future results.
