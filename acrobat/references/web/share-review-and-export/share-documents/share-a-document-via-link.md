# Share a document via link

A walkthrough for handing off a document by link from Acrobat on the web.

Passing along a document by link lets the people who receive it open it in a browser, no sign-in required. Should comment access be switched on, those recipients can log in so they can review and leave comments.

Within either Your documents or Recent files, move your pointer over the document you intend to share, then choose Get a link .

Move your pointer over the document you'd like a link for, then choose the link icon.

When the dialog box appears, flip on the Allow comments toggle if the PDF is going out for review.

Note

For any document that isn't a PDF, the Allow comments option can't be switched on.

When the handoff is for viewing alone, double-check that Allow comments stays switched off.

Choose Get link.

As soon as a notice tells you the link sits on the clipboard, paste it wherever you want to pass it along.

That document link reaches the recipients, who open it in a browser without needing to sign in. Where comments are switched on, recipients can log in to review and contribute their comments.
