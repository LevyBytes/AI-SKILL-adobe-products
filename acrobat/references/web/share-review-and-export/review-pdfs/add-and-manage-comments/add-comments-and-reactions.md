# Add comments and reactions

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Documents.

Note

Should the document's owner hand the PDF off through Adobe Document Cloud, an email invitation carrying a link arrives for you. Choose Open inside that email to reach the document and review it.

Pick the right category over in the left pane, then choose the document where your comments should go.

Highlight the text you want marked and commented on, then make your choice from the Context menu.

Recoloring a text highlight starts by selecting it, then choosing the Color icon out of the context menu. From there any shade in the color panel is yours to pick.

For a general comment that doesn't highlight any text, choose from the Quick Actions toolbar, then set it on whatever spot in the document should carry the comment.

Inserting text starts with choosing > Insert text out of the Quick Actions toolbar. Then bring the cursor to the spot you want on the document and begin typing.

Proposing a text insertion at any point on the document is what the 'Insert text' tool is for.

Over in the Comments pane, key in your comment.

Pairing a reaction with the comment means choosing Open emoji picker, then picking whatever reaction emoji you want off the panel.

Both the emoji reaction and your text land as a comment.

Choose Post.
