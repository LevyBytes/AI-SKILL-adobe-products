# Manage comments

Note

Comments left by other collaborators are off-limits to a contributor for editing, revising, or deleting. The PDF owner, on the other hand, is able to delete them.

Out of the right pane, choose Edit, delete, or reply to comments to bring up the Comments pane.

Pick whichever comment you mean to edit, delete, or answer.

Editing a comment means choosing > Edit.

Edit a comment and the change is visible to everyone. A deletion, by contrast, takes the comment off the document for good.

Removing the comment means choosing > Delete.

Answering a comment goes like this: pick the comment, key your feedback into the Add a reply text box, then choose Post.

Note

The instant a recipient answers a comment, an @mention goes automatically to the original author so they get notified. Want to switch off auto @mention? Choose your profile icon > Settings, then clear the Auto mention checkbox. Choose Save and reload the page for the change to take hold.

Reacting to a comment means hovering over it, choosing Add reactions, then picking a reaction off the panel.

Pick a reaction out of the emojis panel and it lands on the comment on its own.

Pick a reaction out of the emojis panel and it lands on the comment on its own. Note

Stripping off a reaction you placed is as easy as selecting it.

Whatever comments you contribute or revise on the PDF file get saved on their own.
