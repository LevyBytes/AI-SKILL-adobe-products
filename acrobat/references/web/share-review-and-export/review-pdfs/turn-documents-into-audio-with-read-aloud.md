# Turn documents into audio with Read aloud

A walkthrough for getting PDFs read out loud, boosting accessibility and freeing up your hands.

Built into Adobe Acrobat on the web is a Read aloud tool that voices the text inside documents. People with visual impairments, or anyone who would rather listen to what's in a document, find this handy.

Head to Acrobat on the web and open whichever document you want voiced.

Off the top bar, choose Read aloud

Reading kicks off at the beginning of the document, running the default voice and a 1X playback speed.

Read-aloud mode in Acrobat on the web. A - Playback speed control. B - Read previous paragraph. C - Read next paragraph. D - Voice options.

Halting the audio playback means choosing the Pause icon.

Choose the Play icon to pick it back up.

Lean on the |< and >| icons to jump to the paragraph before or after.

Swapping the audio voice means choosing Voice options and grabbing whichever option you want off the list.

Swapping the audio speed means choosing 1x off the top bar and grabbing your preferred playback speed.

Choose X to leave read-aloud mode.
