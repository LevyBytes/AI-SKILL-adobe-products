# Report content from an open document view

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Document > Shared by others.

Open whichever document you mean to flag.

Off the top bar, choose More options > Report abuse.

Complete the details on the form, then choose Report abuse.

Concerns can also go by email to abuse@adobe.com, and there's more to read on flagging a possible violation over at the Adobe Transparency Center.
