# AI-powered PDF review

Lean on the generative AI capabilities in Acrobat on the web to get through long, intricate PDF material in a hurry.

Comprehension climbs, insights come easier, and polished content gets produced fast thanks to the generative AI capabilities built into Acrobat and Acrobat Reader.

Editing what's in your PDF, rephrasing wording, and even running a quick pass over your PDF documents to make the edits you need — that's all within reach through the AI Assistant in Acrobat.

Note

For now, Acrobat's generative AI capabilities sit in beta and work in English only. Go over Adobe's Generative AI user guidelines so the governing terms are clear. Dig deeper into how Adobe handles content usage and data.
