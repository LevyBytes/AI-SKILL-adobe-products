# Send for signature in bulk

Walkthrough: leaning on a form template to dispatch hundreds of one-of-a-kind agreements to recipients for signing.

Note

Every agreement carries an audit report all its own, kept apart from the rest. None of the recipients can tell that any of the others exist.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose E-Sign > Send in bulk.

Note

Reaching the Send in bulk feature calls for an active subscription to either Adobe Acrobat Pro or Acrobat Pro for teams.

Should you be a Teams admin running on Acrobat Standard for teams, a prompt will ask you to move up to a higher plan. When that dialog shows up, click Manage Account to keep going. Any Teams members who want the feature have to ask their admins to turn it on.

Click Choose files and look through your device to pick the document you'd like to send out for signing.

Open Agreement settings and adjust things like the deadline for finishing the agreement, how often reminders go out, whether a password is needed, and what language recipients see. Once that's set, click Continue.

Tip

When the Send in Bulk window appears, confirm the Agreement name and reshape the Message however your situation calls for.

Down in the Add recipients section, type out the recipients' email addresses with commas between them. Hit Enter afterward to lay the addresses out as a list.

After the recipients show up as a list, you can fill in their names and, if you'd like, drop one of them by clicking the Delete icon.

Open the Email dropdown menu and set the recipients' Authentication.

Click Preview & add fields. Hand it several documents and they're merged together into one single PDF.

With your recipient chosen in the Recipients section, drag the signature and form fields out of the right pane and drop them wherever they belong on the document.

If it suits you, hold onto the agreement as a template by ticking the Save as template checkbox.

Click Send.

Off goes the agreement to every recipient for signing, and a confirmation note appears.
