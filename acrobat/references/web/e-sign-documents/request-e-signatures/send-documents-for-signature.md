# Send documents for signature

Walkthrough: collecting signatures from other people on your documents by way of the Request e-signatures feature.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, head to E-Sign > Request e-signatures.

Note

For enterprise accounts, a prompt offers to redirect you over to Acrobat Sign so you can reach the Request e-signatures feature. Click Continue to keep going.

Can't get to the Request e-signatures feature? Odds are your administrator has switched it off. Get in touch with that administrator to have access restored.

Click Choose files and look through your device to pick the document you'd like to send out for signing.

Tip

The Get documents signed page comes up next; here you can double-check the Agreement name and Message fields and tweak them if you'd like.

Open Agreement settings to adjust things like the deadline for finishing the agreement, how often reminders go out, whether a password is needed, and what language recipients see. Once that's set, click Continue.

Down in the Add recipients section, set the role for each person and fill in their email and name in the matching fields. There's also room to pick an authentication method or tack on a private message.

Want the document to reach more people? Click the icon and pick from the standard choices: Myself, Individual, Group, and CC.

Stack as many recipients as you need, and decide the sequence they sign in by ticking the "Recipients must sign in order" checkbox.

Click Preview & add fields to bring the document up in field authoring mode, where the form fields get spotted on their own. Hand it several documents and they're merged together into one single PDF.

Pick a recipient from the Add Form Fields For dropdown, then drag the signature and form fields out of the left pane and drop them wherever they belong on the document.

Note

So that building a form for several people stays manageable, every recipient's fields carry their own color. Click a recipient and their fields light up, along with every related field in the left rail, so you can confirm each one landed with the right person.

Click Send.

Want to adjust a field? Double-click it and change whatever you need over in the left pane.

Once the agreement goes out for signing, you'll see a confirmation note with a preview, and a copy lands in Adobe cloud storage. Every agreement you've sent lately is reachable from the Recent section on the homepage.

Alert

Running into a Request entity too large message or a Server Error while uploading? Wipe your browser cookies and give it another go.
