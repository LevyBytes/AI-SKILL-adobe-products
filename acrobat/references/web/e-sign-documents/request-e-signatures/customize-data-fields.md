# Customize data fields

Walkthrough: placing tailored data fields onto documents headed out for e-signature.

Before you begin

Kick off the signing workflow by working through steps 1 through 6 first.

With your recipient picked in the Add Form Fields For box, grab a field from the left pane (the walkthrough here uses a Text field) and let go of it wherever you want it placed on the document.

Give the form field you'd like to adjust a double-click.

Over in the left pane, the Customize field type tools let you dial in whatever field settings you need.

When it's a text field, choose its format, the type of characters allowed, how many characters fit at most, and the font. Want it to be unskippable? Tick the 'Required field' checkbox.

Clicking anywhere outside the field on the document commits the settings and locks them in.

Hit Send to fire the document off for signature right away, or Save it to hold it for later.

Your tailored fields now sit on the document, and a copy goes to Adobe cloud storage.
