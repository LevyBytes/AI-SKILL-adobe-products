## Create podcasts

Find out how to convert your documents into lively audio podcasts inside Adobe Acrobat on the web.

With the Generate podcast feature, Acrobat takes any document or PDF Space and spins it into a brief, lively audio rundown — ideal for quick learning, hands-free review, or accessible listening. Pick either a summary or a richer, conversational style, dial in the tone and expertise level, and receive a notification once your audio is finished. It is an easy route to working through reports, briefs, research, or legal material while you are on the move.

### Turn documents into podcasts

Head to Acrobat on the web and open the PDF you intend to turn into a podcast.

From the top menu, pick Listen to this document and then settle on a format:

- Highlights podcast: When you want a tight, focused summary
- Deep dive podcast: When you want a thorough, detailed walkthrough

As soon as the podcast finishes in your chosen format, an AI podcast control bar shows up along the bottom.

## Set light or dark mode

### Find out how to flip between light and dark mode and adjust how Acrobat on the web looks.

Starting from the Acrobat on the web homepage, click the profile icon at the top right, then choose Settings.

Open the Appearances drop-down menu and pick whichever option suits you:

- Use system settings – Matches whatever appearance your device is already set to.
- Light – Puts Acrobat into light mode.
- Dark – Puts Acrobat into dark mode.
