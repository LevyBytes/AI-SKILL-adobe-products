# Stylize PDFs using Adobe Express

See how reaching the Adobe Express design tools from within Acrobat lets you polish the look of your PDFs.

With the PDF open, click Edit on the global bar.

Beneath DESIGN WITH ADOBE EXPRESS, click Style this PDF.

A fresh browser tab opens with your PDF loaded in Adobe Express.

Reach for these shortcuts on the right pop-up menu to dress up the PDF:

- Add shapes and icons: Drop in geometric shapes or ready-made icons to strengthen your layout.
- Resize page: Reset the dimensions of your PDF pages so they suit different formats or needs.
- Apply color theme: Pick from preset color palettes to give your PDF a unified look.
- Add Adobe Stock images: Hunt through and place high-quality stock photos that illustrate your content.
- Change background color: Swap the background color of your PDF pages for stronger visual appeal.
- Get font recommendations: Pick up font suggestions that pair well with the text styles you already have.

Note

Whatever you change is saved on its own into the Adobe Express file, while your source PDF over in Acrobat stays as it was.
