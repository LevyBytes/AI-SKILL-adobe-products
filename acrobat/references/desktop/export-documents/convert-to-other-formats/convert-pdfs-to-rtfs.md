# Convert PDFs to RTFs

With Adobe Acrobat, here's how to turn a PDF into a Rich Text Format (RTF) file.

From the global bar, choose Convert.

In the left pane, pick Other Format, then choose RTF in the dropdown menu.

Under the EXPORT PDF TO section, open the Other format drop-down and pick RTF to carry out the conversion.

Choose Convert to RTF.

When the dialog box appears, pick where you want the file saved.

Rename the file if you like, then choose Save.
