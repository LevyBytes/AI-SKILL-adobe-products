# Convert PDFs to PostScript files

Use Adobe Acrobat to push a PDF out to a PostScript-format file for printing and prepress work.

The PostScript (PS) and Encapsulated PostScript (EPS) formats both serve printing and graphics. PS suits high-quality, multi-page document printing, particularly on PostScript printers. EPS is a slice of PostScript, normally reserved for single-page files. Its purpose is to drop images, illustrations, or logos into other documents, above all in design and publishing work.
