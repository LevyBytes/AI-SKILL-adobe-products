# Remove Bates numbering in Acrobat Pro

Strip Bates numbers out of PDFs when you need to tidy up or reuse legal documents.

Pull up the PDF that carries Bates numbering, then click Edit on the toolbar.

From the left pane, choose More > Bates numbering > Remove.

Reach for Remove on the Bates numbering menu to strip the Bates numbers off the pages in your document.

Confirm by choosing OK, and the Bates numbering comes off.

Write the document to disk so the change sticks.
