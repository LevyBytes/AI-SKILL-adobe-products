# Delete pages in PDFs

See how Acrobat on desktop makes it quick to pull pages you don't want out of a PDF.

Stripping out pages clears away surplus material yet preserves the file's framework along with interactive pieces such as bookmarks and links.

Before you begin

Confirm the PDF can actually be edited: head to File > Document Properties > Security and look over whatever restrictions are listed.

With the PDF open, choose All tools > Organize pages.

Thumbnails for each page show up in the document area.

For a single page, hover its thumbnail and pick Delete from the context menu.

When several pages are involved, work through the Pages Selected field. Two routes are open to you:

- Type out page numbers split by commas, like 2, 4, or give a span such as 6–8.
- Or settle on one of the dropdown's choices.

Note

At least one page has to stay behind — Acrobat won't let you wipe out every page.

From the left pane, choose Delete pages.

Confirm by choosing OK.

Save the PDF.
