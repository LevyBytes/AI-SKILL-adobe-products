# Flatten all layers

Select Layers from the right-side panel to open the Layers panel.

In the Layers panel, choose Options, then Flatten Layers.

Flattening strips the entire layer structure out of your PDF. Whatever was visible at the moment you flattened stays behind; any hidden layer content is gone for good.
