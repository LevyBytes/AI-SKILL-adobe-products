# Merge and flatten layers

See how merging and flattening layers can streamline the way a PDF is built.

Pull layer properties together, or fold visible content into one piece, to thin out a layered PDF. Merging draws the layers you pick into a single layer; flattening bakes every visible layer into one permanent page layer. Either route gets files ready for distribution or printing and trims away complexity.

Before you begin

Merging and flattening layers can't be undone. Stash a backup copy of your PDF before you combine anything.
