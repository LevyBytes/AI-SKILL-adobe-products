# Reorder PDF layers

Find out how to reshuffle the way layers stack within a PDF.

How overlapping layers stack comes down to their order, and rearranging that order is what decides which elements sit in front of which — a handy way to keep busy documents tidy.

With your PDF open in Acrobat, click Layers in the right-side panel to bring up the Layers panel.

Tip

If Layers isn’t visible, select Menu > View > Show/Hide > Side panels > Layers.

Click the layer you intend to relocate; once chosen, it shows up highlighted in the list.

Drag it up or down the stack until it lands where you want.

Let go of the layer once the blue indicator line shows up at the spot you want.

Note

Shifting a layer’s order affects nothing but the stacking sequence — its properties, visibility settings, and content all stay put.
