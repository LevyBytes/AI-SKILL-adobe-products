# Create marketing documents using Adobe Express

Spin up brochures and marketing pieces fast by tapping Adobe Express templates from within Adobe Acrobat.

Select Create from the global toolbar.

Choose Templates, then pick the kind of template that fits what you're making. Selecting All templates surfaces the full set. Adobe Express opens in a new browser window.

The left panel holds the controls you use to tailor the design:

- Search: locate particular design assets to drop in.
- Your stuff: pull in the fonts, colors, and logos that belong to your brand.
- Templates: look through and pick from a wider set of templates.
- Media: bring in photos, videos, or audio from the library, or upload files of your own.
- Text: place text and shape its formatting.
- Elements: drop in design assets, backgrounds, shapes, and icons.
- Add-ons: reach designs supplied by third-party apps.

Pick whichever format suits the marketing piece from Templates, Text, Media, and the rest of the options.

Building a marketing document comes down to selecting the format you want across Templates, Text, Media, and beyond.

Once your edits are done, select Download from the top bar, then pick PDF Standard from the File format dropdown menu.
