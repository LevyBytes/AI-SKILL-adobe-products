# Visual enhancement features in Acrobat

Bringing Adobe Express into Acrobat Pro unlocks a handful of features for sprucing up your PDF documents. With it you can:

- Design with Adobe Express: Jump straight from the Edit menu into the Adobe Express editor to reach its design tools.
- Use templates: Build PDFs such as flyers, reports, or proposals from customizable Adobe Express templates.
- Generate AI images: Convert text into one-of-a-kind visuals through Adobe Firefly-powered generative AI.
- Add pages: Drop fresh pages into your PDF by way of the Adobe Express editor.
- Apply color themes: Pick a preset theme to lend your PDF a refined, unified appearance.
- Add design elements: Dress up your PDF with icons, shapes, and Adobe Stock images.
- Get font suggestions: Sharpen your typography with font picks suited to your document.
