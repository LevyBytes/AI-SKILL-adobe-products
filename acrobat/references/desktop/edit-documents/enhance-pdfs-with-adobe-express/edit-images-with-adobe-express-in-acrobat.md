# Edit images with Adobe Express in Acrobat

See how reaching for Adobe Express tools straight from Adobe Acrobat lets you polish the images in your PDF documents.

Open the file holding the image you want to edit and click Edit on the global bar.

Pick the image you intend to edit.

From the left pane, click Edit image.

Adobe Express editor loads the image.

Reach for options such as Effects, Remove background, Erase, and Opacity to work the image however you need.

Working in Adobe Express lets you strip the background off a PDF image in one quick move.

A single step in Adobe Express clears the background from the image used in the PDF.

Click Apply.

Tip

Want to push your PDF further? Adobe Express features like Try Stylize this PDF, or Apply color theme, let you do exactly that. See the Adobe Express how-to for details.
