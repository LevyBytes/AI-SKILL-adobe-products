# Generate AI images from text

See how the Adobe Express editor inside Acrobat lets you produce AI images and drop them into your PDF documents.

With a document open, click Edit on the global bar.

Over in the left pane, click Image beneath ADD CONTENT.

Click Generate image. Should a prompt appear, click Agree to the Generative AI User Guidelines.

Into the text box, type a thorough description of the image you want produced.

Spell out a rich, detailed prompt to land the image you are after.

A precise, well-detailed prompt gives you the image you have in mind.

Choose the settings you need for:

- Image size
- Content type
- Styles

Click Generate.

Look over the four AI-generated image choices. Click Refresh to pull up more if you want them.

Pick the one you like and click Apply to drop it into your PDF document.
