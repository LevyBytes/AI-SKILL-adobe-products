# Design PDFs using Adobe Express

See how Adobe Express, reached straight from Acrobat, lets you enrich your PDFs with creative templates and design elements.

With your PDF open, click Edit on the global bar.

Beneath DESIGN WITH ADOBE EXPRESS, click any of these:

- Stylize this PDF
- Use design tools
- Apply color theme

Note

Acrobat sends your file up to Adobe cloud storage and loads it in Adobe Express on a fresh browser tab.

Reach for the Adobe Express tools to enrich your document:

- Click Templates to browse the designs on offer
- Click Add > Add new page to drop in another page
- Click Theme on the top bar to apply a color theme

Your edited file lives under the "Your stuff" section over in Adobe Express.

Look under "Your stuff" in Adobe Express to find the file you edited.

Once you have finished editing, click Download, then click PDF Standard (best for documents) to save the PDF.

Note

When you want to hand off the document, click Share, then pick something like Invite collaborators.
