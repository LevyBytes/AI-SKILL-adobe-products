# Apply timestamps to documents

Open whichever document needs a timestamp, then choose All tools > Use a certificate.

In the left pane, choose Timestamp.

Should you be prompted, save the document wherever you'd like and rename it if you need to.

As soon as the timestamp lands, a confirmation turns up in the Signature Panel up top.

Open the Signature Panel to see the timestamp details.

Through the Signature Panel you can confirm the document carries a digital signature and a timestamp. Signature details such as validity and expiration are there to review.
