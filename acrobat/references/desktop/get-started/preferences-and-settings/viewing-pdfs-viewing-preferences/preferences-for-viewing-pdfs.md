# Preferences for viewing PDFs

A default page layout, along with plenty of other customizations, gets defined through the Preferences dialog box. When it comes to viewing PDFs, look over the preference options under Documents, Full Screen, General, Page Display, and 3D & Multimedia.

Rather than belonging to any single PDF, the preferences govern how the application acts every time you run it. Open the preferences dialog by choosing the hamburger menu (Windows) > Preferences or Acrobat / Adobe Acrobat Reader > Preferences (macOS).

Note

Whenever third-party plug-ins are installed, configure their preferences through Third-Party Preferences.
