# Cyrillic, Central European, and Eastern European language PDFs

PDFs whose fonts are embedded can be opened, edited, and printed in Acrobat across these scripts:

- Cyrillic text, including Bulgarian and Russian
- Central European text
- Eastern European text, including Czech, Hungarian, and Polish

Searching works on these fonts whether or not they're embedded.

Note

Where a PDF relies on fonts for its form fields or text boxes that aren't present on your machine, Acrobat steps in to fetch those missing fonts for you the next time you check for updates.
