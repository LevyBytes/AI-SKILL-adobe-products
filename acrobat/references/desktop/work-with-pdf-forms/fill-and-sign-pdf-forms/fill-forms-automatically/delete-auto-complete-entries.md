# Delete Auto-Complete Entries

Go to Menu > Preferences (Windows) or Acrobat > Preferences (macOS).

Under Categories, pick Forms.

Under Auto-Complete, pick Edit Entry List.

In the Auto-Complete Entry List dialog box that opens, do one of these:

- Remove all entries: pick Remove All.
- Remove specific entries: highlight the ones you want gone, then pick Remove.

Pick Yes to confirm.

Pick Close.
