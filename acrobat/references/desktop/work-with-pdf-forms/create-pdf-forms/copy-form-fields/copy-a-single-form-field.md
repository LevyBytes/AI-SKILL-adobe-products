# Copy a single form field

Select All tools > Prepare a form.

Find the field you mean to duplicate, right-click it, and choose Copy.

Right-click somewhere on the page and choose Paste; the duplicate lands at the page's center.

Grab the duplicate and drag it wherever you want it.

Tip

While dragging, hold down Shift to lock the movement to either vertical or horizontal.

If you'd like the copied field to fill in automatically with whatever was entered in the original, leave the field name as it is.

When the form field is meant for unique information, rename the field.
