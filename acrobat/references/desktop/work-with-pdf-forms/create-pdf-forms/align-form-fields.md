# Align form fields

See how lining up form fields in Adobe Acrobat keeps your PDF forms easy to work with.

Select All tools > Prepare a form.

Using Shift + Click, pick out the two or more fields you intend to align.

Head to the Prepare a form pane and, beneath ALIGN, choose from these:

- Left align: lines the fields up against the left edge of the leftmost one you selected.
- Right align: lines the fields up against the right edge of the rightmost one you selected.
- Top align: lines the fields up against the top edge of the highest one you selected.
- Bottom align: lines the fields up against the bottom edge of the lowest one you selected.
- Align horizontal center: sets the fields on a shared horizontal center point.
- Align vertical center: sets the fields on a shared vertical center point.

To align the form fields, select the appropriate option from the ALIGN and CENTER sections.

For centering fields on the page, reach for any of these options under CENTER:

- Center horizontally: places the fields at the page's horizontal midpoint.
- Center vertically: places the fields at the page's vertical midpoint.
- Center both: sets the fields at the page's center on both axes at once.

To fine-tune the gaps between aligned fields, pick any of the following under DISTRIBUTE:

- Distribute Vertically: gives the fields equal spacing running top to bottom.
- Distribute Horizontally: gives the fields equal spacing running left to right.

Choose Preview to confirm the fields ended up properly aligned.
