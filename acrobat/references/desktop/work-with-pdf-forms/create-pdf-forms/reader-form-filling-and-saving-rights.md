# Enable filling and saving rights in PDF forms for Acrobat Reader users

Store a form as a Reader Extended PDF from Adobe Acrobat Pro so people on Acrobat Reader can complete and keep it.

Saving a form as a Reader Extended PDF from Acrobat Pro lets people in Acrobat Reader fill it in, save it, leave comments, and reach the measuring tools.

Go to Menu > Save As Other > Reader Extended PDF (Windows) or File > Save As Other > Reader Extended PDF (macOS).

Pick whichever of these you need:

- Enable Commenting & Measuring: lets people leave comments and reach the measurement tools.
- Enable More Tools (includes form fill-in & save): lets Reader users keep the data they've typed into interactive or fillable forms.

On interactive and fillable forms, leave comments, open the measuring tools, and retain the data.

Leave comments, open the measuring tools, and retain the data across interactive and fillable forms.

Confirm with OK.

When the Save as PDF dialog opens, give it a name, pick where it goes, then pick Save.
