# Create a personalized AI Assistant

From the Acrobat homepage, pick PDF Spaces, then open the PDF Space you want.

Within the chat panel, go to Choose an AI Assistant > Create your own.

When the dialog box appears, type in a name plus a short description for the AI Assistant.

Supply a name, description, and special instructions to build your personalized AI Assistant.

For extra fine-tuning of your AI Assistant, choose Generate instructions, look over what gets auto-generated, and revise it where needed.

Choose Save and apply to switch on your personalized AI Assistant.
