# Collaborating on PDF Spaces

See how PDF Spaces keeps teamwork running smoothly through shared files, well-defined permissions, and review-and-contribute tools baked right in.

Plenty of document work falls apart amid fragmented drafts, feedback strewn everywhere, and conversations that never connect. By pulling files, context, AI-powered insights, and activity into one shared workspace, PDF Spaces lets teams work with clarity and stay on the same page.
