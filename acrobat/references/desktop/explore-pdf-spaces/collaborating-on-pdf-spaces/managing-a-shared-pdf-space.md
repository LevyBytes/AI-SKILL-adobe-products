# Managing a shared PDF Space

Owning a PDF Space means access and participation stay firmly in your hands. Cutting off access on the spot is as easy as unsharing the PDF Space anytime, or you can wipe it out altogether once the project wraps—just grab any files you need beforehand. Should roles shift, the Share settings let you rework participant permissions or take people off entirely. Checking who currently holds access is available whenever the need arises.
