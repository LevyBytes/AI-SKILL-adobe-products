# Access and permission levels

Both who reaches a Space and what they're able to do once inside fall under your control in PDF Spaces.

Access levels determine the audience: Keep a PDF Space private by emailing invitations to particular people, which suits confidential or early‑stage work. To widen the circle, open it up to your organization so any colleague at your company can reach it through a link. And when learning resources or public-facing material need sharing, flip the PDF Space to public so the link works for anyone who has it.

Permission levels define participation: After access is granted, the level of engagement is yours to set. These permissions cover the whole PDF Space rather than any single file.

- Viewers can only read files. Use this role for stakeholders who need read‑only access.
- Reviewers can view files and add comments. Assign this role to anyone providing feedback.
- Contributors can add, update, or delete project content, including files added by owners and notes they create. It's ideal for teammates actively shaping the project. Contributor permissions are not supported for public access links.

Taken together, the access and permission settings let you settle who lays eyes on the project and just how far their collaboration reaches. Whatever permission you hand out fully shapes the recipient's experience.
