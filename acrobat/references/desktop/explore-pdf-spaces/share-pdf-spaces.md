# Share PDF Spaces

Find out how sharing a PDF Space in Adobe Acrobat lets the people you choose view, comment on, or edit the shared content.

Share a PDF Space and recipients can view, review, or contribute to its files, notes, and insights, all governed by the permissions you hand them. They can also work with the built-in AI Assistant or whatever custom AI Assistant the owner has picked.

The first time reviewers open a PDF Space, they're shown its name and description, a rundown of what's inside (files, notes, and insights), plus pointers that frame expectations and the steps ahead. Learn more about collaborating on PDF Spaces.

Note

Anyone receiving a shared PDF Space can only open it in the languages that are supported. Should the language you're on not be among them, change over to one that's currently supported.

On the Acrobat homepage, pick PDF Spaces from the left pane.

Pick the PDF Space you'd like to share, then choose Share over in the right panel.

Click the link beneath Control who has access, set the link access level along with the recipients’ permission level, and choose Done.

- Anyone with the link
- Anyone in <your‑organization> with the link (Default option for enterprise users)
- Invited people only

- Contributors: Can add comments, and add or remove files and notes
- Reviewers: Can add comments
- Viewers: Can view files and notes

Note

Once the link access level reads Anyone with the link, the only permissions you can grant users are Viewer or Reviewer. Contributor permissions are not supported for public access links.

Look over the access settings and tune them so you control the way collaborators engage with the Space.

Choose how to share:

- Invite people: Enter the recipient's name or email, review the message, and select Send.
- Direct link share: Select Create a link to share and share the copied link with recipients.
- Share via third-party apps: Select any third-party app, such as Outlook, Gmail, Teams, or WhatsApp, and follow the onscreen instructions.

Note

Each recipient's permissions remain yours to change at any point.

While sharing a PDF Space with invited individuals or across your organization, you can bring particular people in as Viewers, Reviewers, or Contributors.

Unsharing a PDF Space means picking it and then choosing Unshare PDF Space in the right panel. Removing it altogether is a matter of choosing Delete from the panel.
