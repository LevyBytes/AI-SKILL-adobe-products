# Bring text, images, or other clipboard content into a PDF file.

Content held on the clipboard, spanning one page or several, can be dropped from any application into a PDF that already exists.

Copy what you want onto your clipboard, then open the PDF where it should go.

Head to All tools > Organize pages.

From the left pane, choose Insert > From clipboard.

Bring copied content directly into your PDF by way of the Organize Pages tool's "From clipboard" option.

In the Insert Pages dialog box, say where the clipboard content should land:

- Before or after the first page
- Before or after the last page
- Before or after a designated page number

Select OK.

Acrobat then sets the clipboard content into your PDF at the spot you named.

Want the original PDF left whole as its own file? Choose File > Save As and give the merged PDF a new name.
