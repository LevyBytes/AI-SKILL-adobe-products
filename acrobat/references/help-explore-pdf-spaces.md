## Unlock AI-powered productivity with PDF Spaces

See what PDF Spaces in Adobe Acrobat can do to keep your documents tidy, surface insights, and make working alongside others easier.

Think of PDF Spaces as an AI-powered workspace inside Acrobat that gathers files, links, notes, and insights together in a single location. Rather than simply keeping documents in order, it pushes you toward genuinely understanding the material, reworking it, and passing it along with both clarity and context intact.

PDF Spaces also lets you hand off information as a tailored, guided walkthrough—pairing files, contextual summaries, audio overviews, and an AI Assistant so your point lands cleanly and recipients immediately grasp where to begin and which parts deserve attention.

Here is what PDF Spaces makes possible:

- Organize information: Bring together scattered files, links, and text into a single, organized knowledge hub.
- Get tailored insights: Use a prebuilt AI Assistant to extract key themes, summaries, and comparisons from your files, complete with citations. For tailored insights, create a personalized AI Assistant.
- Collaborate with others: Share your PDF Space so collaborators can view and modify the files and notes, and explore insights together using the selected AI Assistant.

## Create PDF Spaces

Walk through building PDF Spaces in the Acrobat desktop app to keep your content organized intelligently and let AI handle the analysis.

From the Acrobat homepage, pick PDF Spaces in the left panel.

Choose Create a PDF Space.

When the dialog box appears, pull in files through any of these routes:

- Choose files stored on Adobe cloud storage.
- Upload supported file types.
- Import from third-party storage. Select Add an account to connect to third-party storage accounts.
- Add text using the Paste copied text option.
- Add webpage URLs using the Add link option.

Note

Clearing the checkbox next to a listing takes that file or link back out of your PDF Space.

Pull in a mix of file types so the AI can surface insights right away.

Pull in a mix of file types so the AI can surface insights right away.

Once every file is in place, choose Add to PDF Space.

The following pieces appear when your PDF Space finishes loading:

- Overview
- Files listed in the left panel
- Auto-generated insights
- Auto-generated text and audio summaries
- Auto-generated podcasts from your PDF Space content
- A chat panel where you can ask questions or engage with a chosen AI-specialist

Look over the Overview page, and where it makes sense, shuffle or drop items, tweak the AI-generated text or audio summaries, or attach extras like notes, podcasts, or a logo before you share.

Renaming is simple: click the default name at the top, type a fresh name plus an optional description, and choose Save.

## Get smarter insights with personalized AI Assistant

Find out how working with a prebuilt or personalized AI Assistant inside PDF Spaces helps you surface insights, make sense of content, and build a fuller understanding.

Built into PDF Spaces are prebuilt AI Assistant tuned for particular roles, business needs, and document types, letting you rapidly surface meaningful insights, catch patterns, and even render information as charts and graphs. Pick whichever assistant lines up with your goal to work through content more quickly.

When you want sharper, more focused results, you can also create a personalized AI Assistant that produces deeper insights, comparisons, and visual summaries mapped tightly to your particular objectives.

### Get tailored insights

From the Acrobat homepage, pick PDF Spaces, then open the PDF Space you want.

- AI Assistant: General assistance for broad tasks
- Analyst: Best suited for analyzing data or summarizing research
- Entertainer: Great for creative storytelling or engaging content
- Instructor: Ideal for explaining concepts or teaching
- Create your own: Create a personalized AI Assistant for your unique needs

## Collaborating on PDF Spaces

See how PDF Spaces keeps teamwork running smoothly through shared files, well-defined permissions, and review-and-contribute tools baked right in.

Plenty of document work falls apart amid fragmented drafts, feedback strewn everywhere, and conversations that never connect. By pulling files, context, AI-powered insights, and activity into one shared workspace, PDF Spaces lets teams work with clarity and stay on the same page.

### Why collaborate on PDF Spaces

Meaningful collaboration and deeper knowledge exchange are exactly what PDF Spaces was made for. By gathering feedback, layering in insights, and growing ideas as a group, teams reach understanding faster, align better, and decide more clearly.

Because permission levels vary, you get exact say over how participants take part—whether they're offering feedback or directly shaping the project.

### How collaboration works

Within PDF Spaces, collaboration unfolds in one shared environment where everyone on the team handles the same files, context, and insights—no tool-hopping, no losing the thread of a conversation. The starting point is creating a PDF Space that pulls your project's files, notes, and links together to set up a single source of truth.

With the PDF Space in place, you can pass along the whole workspace or just links to particular files or notes, whichever suits what recipients actually need to see. From there, roles let you govern how participants take part, and you can tailor the landing view with guidance and context. On private or organization‑only links, fresh users can ask to get into the PDF Space through Request Access, which the creator then approves or turns down.

Being the owner, you can pull several collaborators into the PDF Space and hand each one a Viewer, Reviewer, or Contributor role to match what they need. Team members get to look over content, drop comments, and put the built‑in AI Assistant to work summarizing documents, fielding questions, or pulling out insights, so everybody finds their footing fast.

### Access and permission levels

Both who reaches a Space and what they're able to do once inside fall under your control in PDF Spaces.

Access levels determine the audience: Keep a PDF Space private by emailing invitations to particular people, which suits confidential or early‑stage work. To widen the circle, open it up to your organization so any colleague at your company can reach it through a link. And when learning resources or public-facing material need sharing, flip the PDF Space to public so the link works for anyone who has it.

Permission levels define participation: After access is granted, the level of engagement is yours to set. These permissions cover the whole PDF Space rather than any single file.

- Viewers can only read files. Use this role for stakeholders who need read‑only access.
- Reviewers can view files and add comments. Assign this role to anyone providing feedback.
- Contributors can add, update, or delete project content, including files added by owners and notes they create. It's ideal for teammates actively shaping the project. Contributor permissions are not supported for public access links.

Taken together, the access and permission settings let you settle who lays eyes on the project and just how far their collaboration reaches. Whatever permission you hand out fully shapes the recipient's experience.

### Staying in sync

Keeping up with changes is easy for collaborators:

- View and reply to comments. Files or notes with comments display a Comments icon that you can select to view and reply to comments.
- Use @mentions to notify teammates or bring new reviewers into the conversation.
- Get notifications for new comments and replies.

### Managing a shared PDF Space

Owning a PDF Space means access and participation stay firmly in your hands. Cutting off access on the spot is as easy as unsharing the PDF Space anytime, or you can wipe it out altogether once the project wraps—just grab any files you need beforehand. Should roles shift, the Share settings let you rework participant permissions or take people off entirely. Checking who currently holds access is available whenever the need arises.

## Supported file formats and limitations

Review which file types work, plus the constraints that currently apply, when using PDF Spaces inside Adobe Acrobat.

### Supported content types

A PDF Space can be assembled from many kinds of content:

- File formats: Files in PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, TXT, RTF, or VTT format.
- Text input: Pasted text.
- Cloud storage: Files imported from Google Drive and OneDrive on all platforms. Dropbox and Box imports are available only in Acrobat on desktop.
- Web content: Public web links.

### Document specifications

Keep these per-space details in mind, each of which governs an individual PDF Space:

- Number of files: Each PDF Space can support up to 100 files.
- Maximum file size: Each file can be up to 100MB.
- Maximum pages: Each file can contain up to 600 pages.
- Unsupported content: Password-protected files, videos, images with complex vector graphics, and handwritten notes are not supported.
- Citation limitation: Citations are unavailable in the Insights dashboard when the app locale differs from the asset language.
