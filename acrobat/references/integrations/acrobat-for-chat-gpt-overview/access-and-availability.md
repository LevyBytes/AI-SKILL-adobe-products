# Access and availability

Every user can reach Adobe Acrobat for ChatGPT, free-plan subscribers included. For now it ships in English across all regions that carry Adobe products and services. After you connect it, switch it on by opening your prompt with @Adobe Acrobat, and your PDF-related requests will be handled by Acrobat on their own.
