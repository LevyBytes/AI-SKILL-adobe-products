# Choose if the Bot notifies users when a transcript is ready (Microsoft Teams only)

It's up to you whether the bot pings your users once a transcript is ready. Leave the Disable meeting transcript notification toggle switched off and, when a scheduled recorded meeting wraps up, the meeting organizer gets a message from the Acrobat app. Picking any of the offered questions lets users spin up a meeting summary with little effort. For this to work, users need notifications turned on for the Acrobat for Microsoft Teams app. When GenAI touchpoints are off yet this feature stays on, only users who have GenAI access will receive the notification.
