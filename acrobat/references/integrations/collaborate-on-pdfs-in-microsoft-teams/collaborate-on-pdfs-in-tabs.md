# Collaborate on PDFs in tabs

Within the Teams channel, select Add a tab icon.

Pick Adobe Acrobat out of the list, or look it up by searching.

When a prompt appears, authorize the app and accept the permissions.

Pick a PDF from Teams, Channels, OneDrive, or send one up from your computer, then select Save.

Pulling a file from somewhere else means selecting the 'Select file from' dropdown menu and choosing the option you want.
