## Control PDFs opening from Gmail and Google Drive

Take charge of how PDFs open automatically from Gmail and Google Drive within Adobe Acrobat.

With the Adobe Acrobat extension for Chrome in place, a PDF you open out of Gmail or Google Drive might launch on its own in Adobe Acrobat. This happens whenever you view PDFs in Chrome. Should you rather rely on the default viewer in Gmail or Google Drive, you can switch this automatic opening off, and flip it back on down the line if you would like PDFs to default to Acrobat.

### Turn off PDFs from Gmail opening automatically in Acrobat

From Gmail, open a PDF and let it launch inside Adobe Acrobat.

In the Acrobat viewer, choose More options.

Switch off the Open PDFs from Gmail in Acrobat toggle.

Through the viewer's preferences, you decide whether Gmail PDFs launch on their own in Adobe Acrobat.

Through the viewer's preferences, you decide whether Gmail PDFs launch on their own in Adobe Acrobat.

### Turn on PDFs opening automatically in Acrobat again

Two routes are available for switching this back on.

### Turn on from Acrobat (recommended)

Inside Gmail, click a PDF link.

Pick Adobe Acrobat so the PDF loads in Acrobat.

In the Acrobat viewer, choose More options.

Flip the Open PDFs from Gmail in Acrobat toggle to on.

From here on, Gmail PDFs open by themselves in Acrobat.

### Turn on from Chrome settings

Reach for this route when you'd rather set the default outside Gmail, or extend it to Google Drive.

Within Chrome, open the three-dot menu

Head to Extensions > Manage extensions.

For Adobe Acrobat: PDF edit, convert, sign tools, choose Details.

Choose Extension options.

Switch on the default view options covering:

- Gmail
- Google Drive

## Edit and organize PDFs using Acrobat for WhatsApp

Discover how Adobe Acrobat lets you edit, arrange, and pass along PDFs straight from WhatsApp Web.

With Acrobat for WhatsApp, a PDF that arrived on WhatsApp Web opens for editing right inside Acrobat. Revise the contents, shuffle page order, and commit your edits without hopping between apps. When you've finished, push the revised PDF back to WhatsApp Web or tuck it safely into Adobe cloud storage. For details on what the feature does, how to reach it, and where it's available, consult the Acrobat for WhatsApp FAQs.

On a desktop or laptop browser, bring up WhatsApp Web, confirm the session is live, and then click a PDF that was shared in the chat to open it.

Note

Have the Chrome Adobe Acrobat extension, or its Edge counterpart, installed and WhatsApp Web PDFs jump straight into that extension. Absent the extension, the same PDFs land in the Adobe Acrobat custom app instead.

Take a look at the PDF inside the WhatsApp chat, then click the thumbnail to bring it up in full‑screen view.

- Format text: adjust typeface, size, styling, color, or letter spacing.
- Add content: drop in fresh text or imagery.
- Manage layout: shift objects around or move sections elsewhere.

## Acrobat for WhatsApp FAQs

Find answers to the questions people most often ask about Adobe Acrobat for WhatsApp.

Adobe Acrobat for WhatsApp opens the PDFs that land in your WhatsApp Web chats and lets you handle them right inside Acrobat. Editing, managing, and sharing happen fast with no app-switching. What it enables:

- Editing of the PDFs that come through WhatsApp Web
- Returning those PDFs to WhatsApp either as links or as copies
- One‑click handoff by way of Send to WhatsApp

Every supported language and every region can use this feature. It serves:

- People new to Acrobat who are on the free version
- Acrobat members already signed in, whether on a free or paid plan
- Anyone on WhatsApp Web with a standard or a business account

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge are the browsers where WhatsApp Web support applies.

For now it doesn't extend to WhatsApp Desktop, WhatsApp Mobile, or the WhatsApp Business API.

What the integration requires:

- A way into WhatsApp Web from a desktop or laptop browser (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge preferred)
- An Adobe Acrobat account
- Either the Adobe Acrobat extension (Chrome or Edge) or the Acrobat app set up on your device

Viewing PDFs and the basic tools cost nothing. The advanced editing capabilities call for an Adobe Acrobat subscription. Try to edit a PDF while signed out or without a subscription, and Acrobat asks you to sign in or upgrade.

## Edit and organize PDFs in Copilot

Find out how Acrobat for Copilot speeds up editing text, arranging pages, and adding annotations to your PDFs.

Straight from the Copilot chat, lean on Acrobat for Copilot to revise text, arrange pages, or drop annotations onto a PDF. Quick tweaks can happen in the preview, or you can move into the full Acrobat editor when text and image work gets more involved.

Before you begin

Confirm Adobe Acrobat has been connected to Microsoft Copilot. Launch Copilot, head to All agents, look up Adobe Acrobat, pick the agent, hit Add, and sign in if you're prompted.

Within the Copilot chat box, choose Add and manage sources > More > All agents > Adobe Acrobat.

Open your chat by spelling out what you're after. For instance:

- Make some edits to this PDF.
- Merge my resume, cover letter, and references into one job application PDF.
- Pull the main financial figures out of this scanned quarterly report.
- Shrink my file down for emailing.

Send up the PDF file that's needed.

Acrobat works through the file and brings it up in an interactive Acrobat editor.

Inside the Acrobat editor, highlight the text you'd like to look over, then settle on a choice from the context menu:

- Comment: drop in a note or remark and type out your feedback.
- Highlight, Underline, or Strikethrough: flag key passages or signal edits you're proposing.
- Annotation color: if you wish, switch the annotation's color before you apply it.
