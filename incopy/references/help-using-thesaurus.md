# Using Thesaurus

> Adobe InCopy reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

After you enter a word, the thesaurus returns synonym, related-word, and antonym suggestions. For instance, with "figment," the InCopy thesaurus may show "fiction" and "fable" as synonyms, "concoction" and "fantasy" as related terms, and "fact" as an antonym.

The first group of synonyms, related words, and antonyms does not have to end the search. Any suggested word can become the next lookup, showing its synonyms, related words, and antonyms. You can also return quickly to any of the 10 most recent lookup words.

InCopy uses these lookup categories:

Synonyms

Words that share the lookup word's meaning, or come very close to it.

Antonyms

Words whose meaning is opposite or nearly opposite to the lookup word.

See Also

Words conceptually tied to the lookup word, but not synonyms or antonyms.

Near Synonyms

Words with a meaning somewhat similar to the lookup word.

Near Antonyms

Words whose meaning approaches the opposite of the lookup word.

## Using the thesaurus — Look up words in the thesaurus

Use the thesaurus when you need synonyms, antonyms, or short definitions for words.

## Using the thesaurus — Look up a word visible in the document window

Open Window > Thesaurus to display the Thesaurus panel. With the Type tool , select the word to look up, then use one of these methods:

Copy the word and paste it into the Lookup text box.

Click the Load Word button .

Choose the language for the lookup. Click the Lookup Word button  or press Enter.

The Definition area of the Thesaurus panel shows a short definition. The Result list shows related words according to the choices made in the Meanings and Category menus.

Note

Drag the vertical divider between the Definition area and the Result list to change their relative widths.

From the Meanings menu, select the part of speech to look up. For example, choose noun for the noun meaning of the word, or verb for its verb meaning. Some meanings are unavailable for some words. From the Categories menu, select the category to search, such as Synonyms or Antonyms. Some categories are unavailable for some words. Note

To put a Result list word into the Lookup field, Alt-click (Windows) or Option-click (Mac OS) the word, then click the Lookup Word button. To put a Result list word into the Change To field, click a word in the Result list.

## Using the thesaurus — Look up a word not visible in the document window

Open Window > Thesaurus to display the Thesaurus panel. Enter the word in the Lookup text box, then press Enter.

## Using the thesaurus — Look up a word again

Open Window > Thesaurus to display the Thesaurus panel. In the Thesaurus panel, choose a word from the Lookup pop-up menu; it contains the last 10 lookup words. Click the Lookup Word button .

## Using the thesaurus — Insert a word from the Thesaurus panel

Use the Thesaurus panel to insert new text or replace selected text in a document. If you replace a document selection with a Thesaurus panel word, only that selection changes; other occurrences remain unchanged. Use Find/Change when every occurrence should be replaced.

Note

When the thesaurus has no matching entry for the text string in the Lookup text box, the remaining Thesaurus panel fields stay empty.

Use one of these choices:

For insertion, place the cursor in the document where the word should be added.

For replacement, select the word in the document.

In the Thesaurus panel, choose a word from the Result list. Click the Change Word button , or double-click a Result list word.
