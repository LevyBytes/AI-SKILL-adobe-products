# Workspace basics - Dock and undock panels

A dock groups panels or panel groups together, usually vertically. Move panels into a dock to dock them, and move them out to undock them.

To dock a panel, drag its tab into the dock at the top, bottom, or between existing panels.

For a panel group, drag the title bar (the solid blank strip above the tabs) into the dock.

A panel or panel group is removed by dragging it out of the dock with its tab or title bar. It can be placed in another dock or kept floating independently.
