# Workspace basics - (Photoshop) Restore a saved workspace arrangement

Photoshop automatically shows workspaces in their most recent arrangement, but the originally saved panel layout can still be restored.

To restore one workspace, choose Window > Workspace > Reset Workspace Name.

To restore every workspace installed with Photoshop, click Restore Default Workspaces in the Interface preferences.

Note

Drag workspaces in the application bar to change their order.
