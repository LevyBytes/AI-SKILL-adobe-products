# Workspace basics - Delete a custom workspace

Open Manage Workspaces from the workspace switcher in the Application bar, choose the workspace, then click Delete. (Fireworks does not offer this option.)

(Photoshop, InDesign, InCopy) In the workspace switcher, choose Delete Workspace.

(Illustrator) Choose Window > Workspace > Manage Workspaces, choose the workspace, then use the Delete icon for removal.

(Photoshop, InDesign) Choose Window > Workspace >Delete Workspace, choose the workspace, then click Delete.
