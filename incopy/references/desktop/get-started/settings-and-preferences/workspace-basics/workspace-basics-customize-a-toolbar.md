# Workspace basics — Customize a toolbar

Toolbar contents, toolbar direction, and toolbar grouping can all be adjusted: choose which tools show, change the orientation, and join or split toolbars.

Use whichever action you need:

To control the tools shown on a toolbar, open the triangle at the toolbar end, choose Customize, and then select the tools. That menu is tailored to the toolbar you opened.

To reposition a toolbar, drag its title bar.

To join toolbars, drag a toolbar by its gripper area onto another toolbar, or place it along the same application-window edge on Windows® or screen edge on Mac OS®.

To make a toolbar float, drag it by the gripper area away from the application-window edge on Windows or the screen edge on Mac OS.

To pull apart a grouped toolbar, drag the toolbar's gripper area away from that group. When a toolbar is dragged out of an existing group, InCopy creates another toolbar.
