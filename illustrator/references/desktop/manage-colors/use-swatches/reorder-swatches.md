# Reorder swatches

Reorder swatches in Illustrator to keep the color palette organized.

The Swatches panel lets you change the order of single Swatches and also manually rearrange Swatches within swatch groups.

Choose Window > Swatches.

In the Swatches panel, choose More options, then choose one of these sorting commands:

- Sort by Name: puts swatches in alphabetical order based on their names.
- Sort by Kind: groups swatches by type, such as Solid colors, Gradients, and patterns.

Note

Sorting commands affect only individual swatches in the Swatches panel; they do not sort swatches inside Swatch groups.

To reorder manually after sorting or at any other time, drag individual swatches to a new location in the Swatches panel.
