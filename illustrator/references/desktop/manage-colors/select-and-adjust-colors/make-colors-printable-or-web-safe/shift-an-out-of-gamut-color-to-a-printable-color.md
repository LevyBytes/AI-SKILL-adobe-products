# Shift an out-of-gamut color to a printable color

RGB and HSB can contain colors, including neon colors, that CMYK has no printable equivalent for. After you choose an out-of-gamut color, the Color panel or Color Picker shows an alert triangle.

Select Window > Color..

Pick individual artwork colors and verify whether any fall outside gamut.

Select Out of Gamut Warning to move the color to its nearest CMYK match, shown in the small box beside the triangle. Use Out of Gamut Warning icon to make the colors printable.

Use Out of Gamut Warning icon to make the colors printable.
