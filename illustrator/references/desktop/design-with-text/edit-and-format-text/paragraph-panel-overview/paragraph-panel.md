# Paragraph panel

Open Window > Type > Paragraph to show the Paragraph panel and edit paragraph and column formatting. Initially, the Paragraph panel displays only the options used most often. In the Paragraph panel, choose More options > Show Options to reveal the complete option set.

From the Paragraph panel, manage alignment, indents, spacing, and hyphenation for text.

A. Alignment and Justification B. Bullets and Numbered List C. Left indent D. First-line left indent E. Space before paragraph F. Automatic hyphenation G. Right indent H. Space after paragraph
