# Edges and faces of Live Paint groups

Within a Live Paint group, the paintable elements are edges and faces. An edge is the section of a path between intersection points with other paths. A face is a region enclosed by at least one edge. Edges can be stroked, and faces can be filled.

For instance, turning a circle crossed by a line into a Live Paint group makes that line an edge that splits the circle into two faces. With the Live Paint Bucket tool, you can fill each face separately and give each edge its own stroke color.
