# Modify faces and edges

Select the Live Paint Selection tool.

Move the tool over a face or edge in the Live Paint group. These cues indicate which item is targeted:

- For a focused face, its surrounding edges show a thick highlight color, and the pointer changes into a filled-bucket indicator.
- For a focused edge, the edge appears with a dull highlight color, and the pointer changes to the edge-painting indicator.

Select faces and edges as needed:

- Click a face or edge to select it.
- Drag a marquee around multiple faces and edges to select them. Faces or edges that the marquee only partly encloses are included too.
- Double-click one face to include every connected face unless a painted edge divides them.
- Triple-click a face or edge to capture matching-fill faces or matching-stroke edges. Another method is to click once, choose Select > Same, then choose Fill Color, Stroke Color, or Stroke Weight.
- Shift-click, or Shift-drag a marquee around items, to add them to or remove them from the current selection.

Apply or remove color according to the selected items:

- To color selected faces, choose a Fill color.
- To color selected edges, choose a Stroke color.
- To remove color from selected faces, press Delete.
- To remove selected edges, press Delete.
- To remove selected faces and edges, press Delete.

Tip

Store fill and stroke colors used by Live Paint groups in the Swatches panel. If an edit removes a color that should remain available, choose its swatch and use the Live Paint Bucket tool to apply that fill or stroke again.
