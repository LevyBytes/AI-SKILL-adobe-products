# When should I use Illustrator and Illustrator (Beta)?

Use the Illustrator desktop app when you need to create content for commercial purposes.

Use the Illustrator (Beta) app when you want to try new features and updates before public availability. After opening Illustrator (Beta), select the beaker icon in the Application bar to see What’s new in Illustrator (Beta).

Beta support and resources are also available through the community forum. Join the Illustrator beta community for help, bug or issue reporting, and feedback or idea sharing.

Note

Beta and pre-release app builds are still under development and may include bugs, incomplete features, or other workflow-impacting issues.
