# Technical requirements for version 29.x

Illustrator does not support case-sensitive file systems, removable storage devices, or network drives.2

Note

1. To activate and use this product, you need an Internet connection, an Adobe ID, and acceptance of the license agreement. This product may connect with certain online services hosted by Adobe or third parties, or provide access to them. Adobe services are limited to users age 13 and older, and use requires accepting additional terms of use plus the Adobe Privacy Policy. Applications and services may not be offered in every country or language, and they may change or end without notice. Extra fees or membership charges may apply.

2. Examples of unsupported virtualized or server-based environments include Citrix Metaframe/Citrix Presentation Server, NVIDIA Grid, Microsoft App-V, VMWare, Parallels, and Virtual PC. "Thin Clients" are also server-based environments. Illustrator has some technical support boundaries for virtualized or server-based environments.
