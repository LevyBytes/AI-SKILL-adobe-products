# Show or hide the homescreen

Learn how to choose whether Adobe Illustrator shows or skips the homescreen at launch.

When the homescreen is skipped and Illustrator is reopened, the workspace opens directly. If at least one document is open, Home in the upper-left corner of the Illustrator window still opens the homescreen.

Go to Illustrator > Settings > General (macOS) or Edit > Preferences > General (Windows).

Select or deselect Show The Home Screen When No Documents Are Open.
