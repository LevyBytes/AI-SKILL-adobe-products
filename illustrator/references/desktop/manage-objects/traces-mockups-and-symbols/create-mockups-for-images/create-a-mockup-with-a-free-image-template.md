# Create a mockup with a free image template

Open Window > Mockup .

Select the vector art with the Selection tool.

Select Preview Mockup in the Mockup panel. The panel shows your vector art previewed on the free image templates. For the art preview, select Preview Mockup.

To preview your art, select Preview Mockup.

Choose the template category for your intended application from the panel drop-down menu. Then drag the template onto the canvas to make a mockup.

Drag the vector art and put it where you want it on the image. The art automatically adapts to the object's shape.

To lock the art at its current position, click outside the image. To move it later, select the art, and then select Edit Content in the Properties panel.
