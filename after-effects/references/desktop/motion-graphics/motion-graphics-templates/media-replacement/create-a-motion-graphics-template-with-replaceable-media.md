# Create a Motion Graphics template with replaceable media

Step 1: Turning a layer into a replaceable one in After Effects means dragging it into the Essential Graphics panel.

Video and image layers qualify too, files brought over from Photoshop and Illustrator among them.

Step 2: Export the template as a Motion Graphics template.

Trimming your template's file size is possible — right-click its thumbnail in the Essential Graphics panel and swap in a thumbnail or a placeholder instead.

Note

MOGRTs that depend on Dynamic Link can't use the Media Replacement feature.
