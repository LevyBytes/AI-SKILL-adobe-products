# Edit your MOGRTs with replaceable media in Premiere

For kicking off eye-catching visual effects on your video without building everything from zero, media replacement MOGRTs are the fastest route. Within Premiere, you can extend templates that already exist — ones authored in After Effects carrying replaceable media — and keep your creative control intact. Working as an editor, you drag and drop fresh graphics, say a company logo or a new clip, straight into the template. Through MOGRTs, handling graphics and animations gets more efficient while visual consistency holds steady.
