# Expression basics

Search Last updated on May 11, 2026

Pick up the fundamentals of expressions in Adobe After Effects so you can automate animation, tie properties together, and drive dynamic motion inside your comps.

Faced with building and linking elaborate animation but reluctant to hand-place dozens or hundreds of keyframes? That's where expressions earn their keep. An expression is a short snippet of JavaScript that you attach to animated properties in your After Effects projects; for any one layer property at any given moment, it resolves to a single value.

A script directs the application to carry out a task; an expression, by contrast, directs a property to do so. Picture a ball traveling across the screen from left to right that you also want to wiggle. Instead of keyframing the Position property, you can hand it a wiggle expression.
