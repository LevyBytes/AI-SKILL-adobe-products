# Why use expressions?

Work faster and spin up animation in a hurry. Expressions let you put actions on autopilot — wiggle, jitter, or bounce, for instance. The payoff in time is large, since you skip cutting fresh keyframes for every action. A handful of starting points follows.

Link different properties.

Lean on expressions to bind one property to another, say Rotation and Position, across comps. That linking lets you spin up varied animation without authoring a separate expression for each.

Create motion infographics.

Expressions come in handy while you build Motion Graphics templates. Tweak assorted properties, tie animations together, and reshape the MOGRTs inside After Effects.

Control multiple layers to create complex animations.

With the pick whip you can steer many animations from just a few controls, producing results that other features would make far more laborious.

Create animated graphs and charts.

Whip up animated and motion infographics in short order — a living world map, say, or animated bar graphs charting the pollution index across countries.

Save and reuse expressions.

Stash expressions as templates and pull them into your other After Effects projects. There's no reason to reinvent the wheel!
