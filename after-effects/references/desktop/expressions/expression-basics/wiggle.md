# Wiggle

Wiggle randomly

Wiggle randomly

Among the After Effects expressions you'll reach for most often is wiggle. True to its name, it nudges a property around by a random amount — a handy trick for giving a scene a less rigid, more lifelike feel.

What sits inside the parentheses governs the motion: the leading value sets how many wiggles occur each second, and the trailing value sets the wiggle amount. Applied to a Position property, wiggle(2,30) therefore shakes the property twice every second across a span of up to 30 pixels.
