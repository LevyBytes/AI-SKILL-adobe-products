# Edit an expression manually

Click inside the expression field to start editing its text.

The moment you do, the whole expression highlights. Want to append rather than overwrite? Click within the expression first to drop your insertion point there — skip that step and whatever you type wipes out the entire expression.

Within the expression field, type and revise your text, drawing on the Expression Language menu if it helps.

Hit Enter on the numeric keypad.

Click somewhere outside the expression field.
