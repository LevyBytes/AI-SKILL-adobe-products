# Camera System and Camera Position controls

Camera System

Sets whether the cards' 3D rendering relies on the effect's Camera Position properties, its Corner Pins properties, or the composition's default camera and light placement.

X Rotation, Y Rotation, Z Rotation

Spin the camera about the matching axis. Reach for these to view the cards from above, the side, behind, or any angle you choose.

X, Y Position

The camera's placement along the x and y axes.

Z Position

The camera's placement along the z axis. Lower values bring the camera in toward the cards; higher values pull it back.

Focal Length

The zoom factor. Lower values zoom in.

Transform Order

The sequence in which the camera turns about its three axes, plus whether that rotation happens before or after the camera is placed via the other Camera Position controls.


Camera System

Picks the source for rendering 3D images: this effect's Camera Position properties, its Corner Pins properties, or else the composition's default camera and light positions.

X Rotation, Y Rotation, Z Rotation

Turn the camera about the matching axis. Reach for these to view the cards from above, the side, behind, or any other vantage.

X, Y Position

The spot in (x,y) space where the camera sits.

Z Position

The spot along the z axis where the camera sits. Lower numbers bring the camera nearer the cards; higher numbers pull it back from them.

Focal Length

The zoom amount. Lower numbers zoom in.

Transform Order

The sequence in which the camera turns about its three axes, plus whether that turning happens before or after the other Camera Position controls set its position.
