# Distort

With the Distortion effects you can remold layers through approaches like smearing, twisting, and stretching. Their controls hand you ways to recast how footage looks and arrive at one-of-a-kind visual outcomes.

- CC Bend It: Bows a layer between a pair of set points, echoing the way a rod or stick flexes.
- CC Bender: Arcs a layer around its vertical axis from one control point, producing a smooth bow.
- CC Blobbylize: Reworks a layer so it reads soft and fluid, as if it were jelly or liquid.
- CC Flo Motion: Warps a layer smoothly across a mesh driven by two clusters of control points.
- CC Griddler: Slices the layer into a grid-style pattern.
- CC Lens: Bends the layer outward from a center point to fake a fisheye lens.
- CC Page Turn: Plays back a believable page curl or flip, the way a sheet of paper turns.
- CC Power Pin: Pins a layer at all four corners so you can stretch it and reshape its perspective.
- CC Ripple Pulse: Sends concentric ripple rings out from the middle, the way a dropped object disturbs water.
- CC Slant: Tilts the layer on a diagonal toward whichever direction you pick for a leaning look.
- CC Smear: Pulls or drags a portion of the layer between two points for a smeared result.
- CC Split: Cuts the layer in half and sends the two pieces off in opposing directions.
- CC Split 2: A beefed-up CC Split that throws in extra knobs such as angle and edge softness.
- CC Tiler: Tiles the layer across the comp to lay down a repeating pattern.
