# Generate

The CC Generate effects come into play when you need to manufacture visual pieces from nothing — textures, gradients, blocks of color, and similar elements.

- CC Glue Gun: Imitates how hot glue behaves as it lays down and sets, leaning into looks like dripping, flowing, and trailing.
- CC Light Burst 2.5: Has elements throw out a burst of light from one point, akin to a flash or a lens flare.
- CC Light Rays: Sends shafts of light fanning out from a source the way sunlight or a spotlight would.
- CC Light Sweep: Slides a glinting streak of light over surfaces, well suited to drawing the eye to text or objects.
- CC Threads: Spins up twisting, flowing strands or fibers, a natural fit for abstract imagery and lively motion design.
