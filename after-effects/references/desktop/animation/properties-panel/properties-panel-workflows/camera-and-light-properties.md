# Use the Properties panel for layers with Camera and Light properties

Building animations with real visual punch leans heavily on Camera and Light layers. With Camera layers you add depth and perspective to a composition so it lines up with real-world camera settings — Focal Length, Angle of View, Zoom, and others. Light layers, meanwhile, let you assign light types that throw shadows, raise highlights, and bring depth into your scenes, pushing your compositions toward greater realism.

As you handle Camera and Light layers, the Properties panel doubles as a single control hub for swiftly tuning a layer's Transform properties and settings options, no trip to the timeline required.
