# Use the Properties panel for a Camera layer

Pick the Camera layer over in the Timeline panel.

Within the Properties panel, turn to the Layer Transform options to tune its attributes.

Point of Interest

Tune this to steer where the camera aims while its position stays put.

Position

Shift the camera through the composition by sliding it along the X, Y, and Z axes.

Orientation

Spin the camera across its X, Y, and Z axes to alter the angle it views from.

X Rotation

Turn the camera about the X-axis, the one running side to side from left to right.

Y Rotation

Turn the camera about the Y-axis, the one running up and down from bottom to top.

Z Rotation

Turn the camera about the Z-axis — either counterclockwise or clockwise.

Beneath Camera options, reach for the available options together with Camera Settings to govern how the camera acts and takes in the elements across your scene.
