# The Properties panel is designed to help you animate faster and easier

A handy panel

Sitting beside your composition window, the Properties panel lets you handle properties and transforms.

Animate faster

The Properties panel spares you from twirling open layered hierarchies or jumping between panels mid-animation.

Simplified workflow

Drop animation onto your text, shapes, and layers in a hurry, all without altering the timeline view.

Easy accessibility

Contextual, effortless reach to the key properties and controls tied to whichever layer or layers you've selected.
