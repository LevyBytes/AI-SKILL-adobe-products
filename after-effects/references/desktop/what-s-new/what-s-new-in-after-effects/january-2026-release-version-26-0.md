# January 2026 release (version 26.0)

Unmult effect for Keying transparency Strip solid black or white backgrounds out of footage while holding onto the foreground's colors and detail for compositing or other visual effects. Learn how to use the Unmult effect ›

Lossless Compressed Playback Preview longer stretches of your timeline using far less disk space, all without giving up any visual quality. Learn how Lossless Compressed Playback can help you ›

Crop composition to selected layer(s) bounds Fit your composition to the selected layers' bounds — shrink it down around smaller content or stretch it out to take in elements that run past the current frame. Learn how to crop composition to selected layer bounds ›

New built-in audio effects Three new audio effects — Distortion, Compressor, and Gate — let you shape and manage sound in your compositions, putting precise audio control right inside After Effects. Explore audio effects available in After Effects ›

Refreshed After Effects Preferences Preferences are now grouped and organized better, so you reach them faster and keep your workflow efficient. Explore the refreshed After Effects preferences ›

New expression and scripting enhancements Jump straight to the keyframe or marker before or after a given time, and read the current text value together with the full list of dropdown menu items. Learn about dropdown menu attributes ›

Windows ARM native support Running natively now on Windows ARM systems, After Effects delivers better performance and efficiency for ARM-based devices. Learn about support for ARM-based Windows devices ›

Improved UI language support Run After Effects in a language other than your OS display language, type Unicode characters into UI elements, blend several language scripts within one text field, and more. Learn about UI language support in After Effects ›
