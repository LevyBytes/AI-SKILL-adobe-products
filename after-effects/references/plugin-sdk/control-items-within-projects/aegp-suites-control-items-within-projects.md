# AEGP Suites — Control Items Within Projects

Items held inside a project or composition are reached and changed here.

Every entry sitting in the project bin counts as an `AEGP_Item`. Keep in mind that a camera, having no source, carries no `AEGP_ItemH`.

Stay at the most general level you can get away with, narrowing only when a particular function demands it; the bulk of calls hand `AEGP_Comps` back and forth as `AEGP_Items`.
