# PF_OutFlag_SEND_DO_DIALOG

`PF_OutFlag_SEND_DO_DIALOG`

For the effect to work it must show a dialog (added to stay compatible with Photoshop plug-ins). `PF_Cmd_DO_DIALOG` gets sent by After Effects after `PF_Cmd_SEQUENCE_SETUP`. Set during `PF_Cmd_SEQUENCE_RESETUP` rather than during `PF_Cmd_GLOBAL_SETUP`.
