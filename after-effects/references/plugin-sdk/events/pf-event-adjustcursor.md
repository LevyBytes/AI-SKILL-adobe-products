# PF_Event_ADJUST_CURSOR

`PF_Event_ADJUST_CURSOR`

The plug-in's UI has the mouse over it. Pick the cursor by adjusting the `PF_CursorType` within the `PF_AdjustCursorEventInfo`. For a custom cursor, make OS-specific calls and let After Effects know by setting `PF_CursorType` to `PF_Cursor_CUSTOM`. Lean on an After Effects cursor when you can, to keep the interface consistent.
