# Third-party Plug-in Hosts?

To keep working with hosts that only partly implement the API, some developers steer clear of the newest features in each After Effects release. Telling host applications apart is a matter of reading `PF_InData`>appl_id. The appl_id After Effects reports is 'FXTC'.

For Premiere Pro it's 'PrMr'. At the time of writing, SmartFX and our AEGP functions have no support from any third party host.

The Premiere Pro & Other Hosts section is also worth a look.
