# Reading the numbers

At large resolutions, PNG **`maximum`** compression can add much more encode time than **`default`**. For the biggest entries, **`maximum`** can hit `/v2/edit` job limits, which is why **N/A** appears. To improve completion reliability for very large exports within normal job timeouts, use **`default`** or a lower compression level. See [Technical Usage Notes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AdobeDocs/ffs-photoshop-API/6a5b7e759e09b595562aa4cb20a2e9b36437bb60/src/pages/getting-started/technical-usage-notes/index.md) for service limits, retries, and related behavior.
