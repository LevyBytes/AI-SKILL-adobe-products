# Sample and adjust colors

Place the Eyedropper tool over the image and choose to sample a color.

To add colors, choose Eyedropper or hold Shift while sampling.

To remove colors, choose Eyedropper or hold Alt (Windows) or Option (macOS).

Move the Fuzziness slider to broaden or narrow the color range.

When Localized Color Clusters is in use, move the Range slider to restrict the sampled-color area.
