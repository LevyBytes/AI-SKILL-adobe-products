# Draw straight-edged segments of a selection border

In Adobe Photoshop, the Polygonal Lasso tool creates precise selections made from straight-edged segments.

Choose the Polygonal Lasso tool from the toolbar.

Choose a selection mode from the Options bar.

Enter a Feather value and enable the Anti-alias checkbox to smooth selection edges.

On the canvas, choose the starting point for the selection.

- To draw a straight segment, choose again at the point where the segment should end.
- For a segment constrained to a 45° angle, hold Shift and choose the next point.
- For a freehand segment, drag while holding Alt (Windows) or Option (macOS).
- Remove the most recent segment with the Delete key.

To close the selection,

- When the circular icon appears over Polygonal Lasso icon, choose the starting point.
- Away from the starting point, close the selection with double-click or Ctrl+click (Windows) or Command+click (macOS).
