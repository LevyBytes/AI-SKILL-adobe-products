# Get new variations of generated content

Create another version of generated content with Generate Similar in Adobe Photoshop.

In Adobe Photoshop, Generate Similar produces fresh alternatives from an image that already exists. The feature is available in the Generative Fill, Generative Expand, Generate Background, and Generate Image workflows.

First, make content with one of the generative workflows, such as Generative Fill.

In the Variations tab of the Properties panel, open the ellipsis menu for the variation you want and select Generate Similar.

Photoshop refreshes the canvas with a related variation.
