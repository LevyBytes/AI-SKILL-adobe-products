# Common questions

Photoshop is Adobe's advanced digital imaging application for photographers, designers, web specialists, and video specialists. It includes strong tools for manipulating images, compositing, editing video, and analyzing imagery. Because it is part of Adobe Creative Cloud, updates and later releases become available through the membership.

Yes. Photoshop can be downloaded and installed on more than one computer, no matter which operating systems those computers use. Activation, however, is capped at two computers for each person tied to the membership. For more detail, see Adobe's terms of use or review how to deactivate a Creative Cloud license on a machine.

Yes. Every Photoshop plan includes Photoshop on the web and Photoshop on the mobile. Each version provides essential Photoshop features adapted for its platform. Purchasing details are available on the Creative Cloud plans page.

Platform determines the system requirements. For complete details, use the technical requirements for Photoshop on desktop. Those specifications help match performance expectations to the device.

Lightroom is also included with Creative Cloud and is built for importing, processing, organizing, and presenting large photo collections quickly. Its close Photoshop integration gives photographers access to pixel-level retouching and compositing as part of a professional photography workflow.

Photoshop cloud documents appear on the Homescreen when Photoshop starts. After signing in with your Adobe ID, you can reach them from any device where Photoshop is installed. Cloud documents sync across devices automatically, and downloaded documents remain available offline.

Adobe Camera Raw works with many digital camera models. Check the complete list of supported cameras for details. Adobe updates Camera Raw regularly to support additional camera models.

For help from Adobe product team members and experienced users, go to the Photoshop Community forum. Tutorials, help documents, and troubleshooting resources are also available from the Photoshop Learn & Support page.

Photoshop is available as an individual app subscription or through the Creative Cloud Photography plan, which also includes Lightroom. Businesses and teams can choose from additional license options. The Creative Cloud plans page has more purchasing information.
