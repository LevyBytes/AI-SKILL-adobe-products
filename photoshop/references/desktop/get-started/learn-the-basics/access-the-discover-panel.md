# Access the Discover panel

Use the Discover panel when you need contextual help, tutorials, or one-click fixes for common Adobe Photoshop workflows.

In Photoshop, the Discover panel brings together context-aware help, tutorials, and quick actions that can speed up workflows. It suggests items based on your skills and current work, helping make complex tasks easier.

Photoshop shows the Discover panel when an internet connection is active. From one place, it offers tools, in-app tutorials, quick actions, and help articles.

Open the Discover panel from Help > Photoshop Help. Another route is Cmd + F (macOS), Ctrl + F (Windows), or the Search for tools, help, and more icon near the Photoshop interface's upper-right edge.
