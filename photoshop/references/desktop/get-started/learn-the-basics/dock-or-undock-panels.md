# Dock or undock panels

Adjust docks in Adobe Photoshop by attaching, detaching, and regrouping panels in the workspace.

A dock holds panels together in a vertical group. Moving panels into a dock attaches them, while pulling them away undocks them.

Open Window and choose the panels to display, such as Brushes, Layers, or Swatches. Photoshop adds those panels to the workspace.

To attach a panel to a dock, drag the panel by its tab toward the edge of another panel or panel group.

After the blue highlight appears, release the tab.

Use docking, undocking, and panel organization in Photoshop to build a workspace around your workflow.

Arrange Photoshop panels with docks and floating groups so the workspace fits how you work.

Move a panel group into a dock by dragging the group from its title bar.

Pull a panel or group out of a dock by dragging its tab or title bar.

Note

Panels can also be moved into another dock or left as free-floating panels.

Raise the bottom edge of the dock until it no longer touches the workspace edge. This keeps the panels from taking over the full workspace.
