# Hide or show all panels

Use panel visibility controls in Adobe Photoshop when you need more canvas room or quick access to tools and controls.

Photoshop can hide all panels for a clearer canvas or show them again when controls are needed. Switching panel visibility helps keep the workspace efficient while you edit.

Use Tab to hide every panel, including the Tools panel and Options bar.

Use Tab a second time to display every panel again, including the Tools panel and Options bar.

Use Shift + Tab to hide the other panels while leaving the Tools panel and Options bar visible.

Use Shift + Tab once more to show the panels again while the Tools panel and Options bar remain present.

Note

When Auto-Show Hidden Panels is turned on in Preferences > Workspace, hidden panels can be shown temporarily by hovering near the application-window edge on Windows or the monitor edge on macOS.
