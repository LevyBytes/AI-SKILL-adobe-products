## Overview of Adobe Photoshop (beta) on desktop

See how Adobe Photoshop (beta) provides advance access to features before they ship broadly.

Photoshop (beta) installs as its own app and can run next to your standard Photoshop installation. It lets you try features that remain under development before they reach the regular product. Adobe uses this pre-release channel to collect direct user feedback ahead of release. With Photoshop (beta), upcoming features can be evaluated without interrupting production work. Both apps can stay installed, and you can move between them whenever needed.

### When to use Photoshop (beta) vs. regular Photoshop

Choosing the right Photoshop version helps match the app environment to the work you are doing:

### Choose regular Photoshop for:

- Stable behavior and features that have been fully polished
- Work intended for commercial use
- Deadline-sensitive critical projects
- Lower exposure to crashes or unexpected behavior

### Choose Photoshop (beta) for:

- Advance access to the newest features
- Chances to send feedback that can shape later releases
- Room to explore new creative possibilities
- YA testing environment for non-critical work where some instability is acceptable

### Generative AI features and credits

In Photoshop (beta), many generative AI capabilities subtract generative credits from the Adobe account you use. The available credit total stays synchronized in real time across Photoshop and Photoshop (beta). A few actions, including the Remove tool when it uses generative AI mode and the Remove button, do not spend credits.

Note

Commercial use is allowed for outputs made with generative AI beta features unless the product or another Adobe location says otherwise. During beta, those outputs do not qualify for indemnification.

## AI Assistant overview

Use AI Assistant in Adobe Photoshop (Beta) on desktop to change images from plain-language text prompts.

AI Assistant helps you try creative directions and move faster without detailed manual edits. To get AI Assistant in Photoshop, install and launch the Photoshop (beta) on desktop app by following the Adobe Creative Cloud Beta FAQs.

Open the panel by selecting the AI Assistant icon in the upper-right area of the window.

Use the AI Assistant icon at the top-right of the window to display the panel.

The panel opens from the AI Assistant icon in the window's top-right corner.

After the panel is open, simple prompts can:

- Apply fast changes, including object removal or element adjustment
- Create alternate versions of your image
- Target particular areas without hand-built selections

In the AI Assistant chatbox, describe the change in natural language, such as "remove the background", "make the sky more dramatic", or "remove the wires".

## Use technology previews

Turn on Technology Previews in Photoshop settings when you want to test experimental features.

Technology Previews expose tools that are still being built. They can make a workflow better, but they may lack polish or stability. Adobe uses the preview period to gather feedback and improve features before public launch.

Select Photoshop > Settings > Technology Previews (macOS) or Edit > Settings > Technology Previews (Windows).

For the available technology preview features, enable or disable the options that fit your workflow.

Select OK.

Restart Photoshop so the updated settings take effect.
