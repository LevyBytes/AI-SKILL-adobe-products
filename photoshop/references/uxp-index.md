## Overview

Welcome to Adobe Photoshop with UXP (**U**nified E**x**tensibility **P**latform). UXP runs on a modern JavaScript engine, supplies a curated UI component set, and gives developers a more direct workflow.

It is a strong moment to explore the future of extending and improving Photoshop. Connect to other services, automate workflows, customize processes, add new capabilities, and then share them with other Photoshop users.
