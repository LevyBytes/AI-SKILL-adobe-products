# Add Google Analytics tracking to your document

Open the InDesign document, then choose File > Publish Online.

In the Analytics tab, paste the Measurement ID generated in Google Analytics so it is attached to the published document. Adobe receives no access to, or data from, your Google Analytics account.

Choose Analytics and enter the Measurement ID so document-view data and other metrics appear in Google Analytics.

Choose Analytics and supply the Measurement ID again so Google Analytics can track views and related metrics.

Select Enable cookie banner on my document to show visitors a consent notice.

In Cookie Banner text, type the wording required for your cookie notice. The banner lets visitors accept or reject analytics collection for that document.

Select Publish, or select Close to collapse the dialog box while you continue editing other InDesign documents.

Open Google Analytics to review insights for the published documents.
