# Access Express templates from InDesign

Browse Adobe Express templates from inside Adobe InDesign and use them without leaving the InDesign workflow.

InDesign includes access to Adobe Express templates, so you can inspect and preview prepared designs. After choosing one, open it in Express and customize it while staying within the app experience.

Choose Templates on the InDesign homescreen.

Choose a template so it appears in the Adobe Express Templates dialog box.

Choose Edit in Adobe Express.

After the template opens in Adobe Express, adjust its text, images, and design elements.
