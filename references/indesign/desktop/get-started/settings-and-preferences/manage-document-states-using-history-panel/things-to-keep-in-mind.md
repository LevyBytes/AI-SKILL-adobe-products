# Things to keep in mind

- Changes that belong to the application, including panel and color settings, actions, and preferences, do not appear in the History panel because they are not edits to a specific document.
- The History panel stores as many as the previous 25,000 states. Once that limit is exceeded, older states are removed from the History panel automatically.
- Closing and reopening a document clears every state from the previous work session out of the panel.
- By default, if you select a state and then alter the design, every later state is removed.
- The Delete and Clear History actions cannot be reversed.
- For unsaved documents, the History panel still shows content and keeps the history after you first choose Save or Save As.
