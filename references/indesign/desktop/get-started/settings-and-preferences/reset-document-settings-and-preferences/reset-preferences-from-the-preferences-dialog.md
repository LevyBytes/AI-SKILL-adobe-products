# Reset preferences from the Preferences dialog

If InDesign can launch normally, use this procedure to schedule the preference reset for the next time the app closes.

Open InDesign.

Choose Edit > Preferences > General (Windows) or InDesign > Preferences > General (macOS).

Enable Reset Preferences On Quit.

In the Preferences dialog box, choose Reset Preferences on Quit to restore the default setting.

Exit InDesign and launch it again.
