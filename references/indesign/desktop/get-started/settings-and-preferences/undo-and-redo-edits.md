# Undo and redo edits

Keyboard shortcuts and menu commands in InDesign can reverse recent edits or apply them again.

During the current work session, InDesign can quickly undo mistakes or redo changes. These controls make it easier to try edits and fix errors without losing work.

Undo and Redo apply only within the open session. After a document is closed and opened again, changes from earlier sessions cannot be undone.
