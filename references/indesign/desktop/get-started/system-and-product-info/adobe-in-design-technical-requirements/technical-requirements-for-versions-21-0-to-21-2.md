# Technical requirements for versions 21.0 to 21.2

Applies to: InDesign 2026 versions 21.0, 21.1, and 21.2.

For hardware and operating-system requirements, use the [version-specific platform tables](https://helpx.adobe.com/indesign/desktop/get-started/system-and-product-info/adobe-indesign-technical-requirements.html). Versions 20.1, 20.2, and 20.3 have a separate table; their requirements are not the 21.0 to 21.2 table.

InDesign does not support case-sensitive file systems, removable storage devices, or network drives.2

NOTES:

1. To activate and use this product, you need an internet connection, an Adobe ID, and acceptance of the license agreement. This product can connect with, or provide access to, hosted online services from Adobe or third parties. Adobe services are limited to users who are at least 13 and require acceptance of additional terms of use and the Adobe Privacy Policy. Applications and services might not be offered in every country or language, and they can change or be discontinued without notice. Extra fees or membership charges may apply.

2. Unsupported virtualized or server-based environments include, without limitation, Citrix Metaframe/Citrix Presentation Server, deployments based on NVIDIA Grid, Microsoft App-V setups, VMWare configurations, Parallels environments, and Virtual PC. "Thin Clients" also count as server-based environments. InDesign has some technical support boundaries for virtualized or server-based environments.
