# Draw basic lines and shapes

Use this topic to create lines, rectangles, ellipses, and polygons for Adobe InDesign layouts with the drawing tools.

InDesign drawing tools let you make custom graphics, frames, and design elements that keep clean scaling. You can place filled shapes or placeholder frames directly in the layout with these tools.

From the toolbar, choose one drawing tool: Line tool, Ellipse tool, Rectangle tool, or Polygon tool.

In the document window, drag to draw the line or shape.

To keep a line at 45° angles, or to keep a path's width and height proportional, hold Shift while dragging.

To draw outward from the center, hold Alt (Windows) or Option (macOS) while dragging.

To move the shape before releasing the mouse button, hold Spacebar while dragging.

While dragging, use the Up and Down arrow keys to change the number of sides of a polygon, and use the Left and Right arrow keys to adjust how many polygons are created.

Tip

After drawing, press V or Esc to change to the Selection tool so you can immediately reposition or resize the new shape.
