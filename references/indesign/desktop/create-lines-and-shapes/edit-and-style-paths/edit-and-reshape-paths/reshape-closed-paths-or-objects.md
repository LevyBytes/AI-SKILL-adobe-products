# Reshape closed paths or objects

With the Direct Selection   tool, drag around the anchor points to include. You can also shift-click individual anchor points.

Move the pointer over the anchor point or segment that should act as the focal point, then select it.

Drag the highlighted anchor points to adjust the path.

Note

Distance from the focal point controls the movement. The focal point itself moves directly; selected points travel along with it; points outside the selection do not move.
