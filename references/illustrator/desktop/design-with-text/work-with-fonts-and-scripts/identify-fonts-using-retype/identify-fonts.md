# Identify fonts

Choose the image or outlined text, then open Window > Retype.

You can also open Retype panel from the Type menu, the right-click menu, or Quick Actions in the Properties panel.

In the Retype panel, select Find Matching Fonts. Retype auto-detects words and places a dotted line around them. Adjacent words that share a font also share one border. The text with the largest border is highlighted by Retype, surrounded with a marquee, and matched fonts appear in the panel.

By default, the panel lists 10 matching fonts in order of visual closeness to the highlighted text. At the bottom, select Find more font matches to show five additional candidates.

Use Retype to find matching fonts for rasterized or outlined text.

If necessary, choose Roman or Japanese from the Choose Script And Match Font menu to define the script.

Under Apply Filter, deselect From Adobe Fonts or From your device to remove suggestions from either source.

Use any of these options for a clearer preview of suggested fonts:

- From the panel menu, choose one of the three text sizes.
- Use the Type your text to preview on card field for a custom sample.

For another area that needs font suggestions, select that text in the image or outlined text. Retype highlights every word using that font, even when the words are separated. If the text you want is not enclosed by a dotted line, drag the marquee over it.

Note

- Use the marquee only when no dotted line surrounds the text.
- Marquee works best when one font is arranged horizontally.

To bring a matching font from Adobe Fonts into Illustrator, hover over it, and select Click to add. Added fonts become available in all Creative Cloud apps.

To favorite a font, hover over it, and select Add to favorites.

Press Exit to leave Retype scan mode. After you exit, the Retype panel automatically displays the fonts marked as favorites. To show every suggested font from the most recent scan, select Clear Filter.

Select Reset Retype panel to clear suggested fonts from the panel.
