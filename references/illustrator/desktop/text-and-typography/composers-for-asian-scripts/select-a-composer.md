# Select a composer

After East Asian or Indic language options are enabled, open the Paragraph panel flyout menu to access these composers. You can reach the panel from the Control panel or with Ctrl+T:

- Middle Eastern & South Asian Single-line Composer
- Middle Eastern & South Asian Every-line Composer

Composers available when you enable Indic or South East Asian language options

Composers available when you enable Indic or South East Asian language options Note

In Preferences > Type, only one of the Indic and East Asian options can be selected at a time. You can leave both options off, or choose one of the two.
