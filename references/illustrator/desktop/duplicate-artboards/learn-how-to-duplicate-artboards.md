# Learn how to duplicate artboards.

Choose Artboards in the tool panel. If the Artboards icon is not visible, choose Window > Artboards.

Choose the desired artboard.

In the Artboards panel, open More options and choose Duplicate Artboards.

In the Artboards panel > More options menu, choose Duplicate artboards to duplicate your selected artboard.

Press Esc to leave artboard-editing mode.
