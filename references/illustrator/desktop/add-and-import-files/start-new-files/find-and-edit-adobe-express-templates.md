# Find and edit Adobe Express templates

Find Adobe Express templates from the Adobe Illustrator homescreen and open them in Adobe Express for additional editing.

From the Illustrator homescreen's left pane, select Templates.

Use the Adobe Express Templates picker to search by template name, or browse categories until you find the needed template.

Choose the template you want, then select Edit in Adobe Express. Adobe Express creates a new document from that template.

Continue refining the artwork as needed.
