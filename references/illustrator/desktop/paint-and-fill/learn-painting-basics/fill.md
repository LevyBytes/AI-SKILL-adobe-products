# Fill

Inside an object, the Fill supplies color, a gradient, or a pattern. It gives vector shapes visual weight, depth, and texture. Apply a Fill to open paths, closed paths, and faces in the Live Paint group.

Fill can be one of three types:

- Solid: lays one consistent color across the shape.
- Gradient: blends gradually between two or more colors.
- Pattern: covers the shape with a repeating design or texture.
