# Paint faces and edges

With the Live Paint Bucket tool, the current fill and stroke colors can be applied to Live Paint group faces and edges.

Select the Live Paint Bucket tool from the toolbar, or press K.

Choose the Fill or Stroke color to apply. When you pick a color in the Control panel, the Appearance section of the Properties panel, or the Swatches panel, the pointer displays three colored squares. The active color appears in the center, with adjacent colors on the left and right. Press the left or right arrow key to use one of those adjacent colors.

Move the mouse pointer over a face or edge:

- Over a face, Illustrator shows a thick colored highlight around that face, and the pointer becomes a filled paint bucket.
- Over an edge, Illustrator shows a thin colored highlight on that edge, and the pointer becomes a brush.

To paint faces, use one of these actions:

- Click a face to paint it.
- Drag across several faces to paint them together.
- Double-click a face to flood paint through unstroked edges into neighboring faces.
- Triple-click the focused face to paint all faces matching its color.

Tip

For Eyedropper sampling, use Option-click (macOS) on the desired fill or stroke; use Alt-click (Windows) for the same action.

For edge painting, double-click the Live Paint Bucket tool in the toolbar, then select Paint Strokes. As a temporary alternative, hold Shift while hovering over the Live Paint group to toggle Paint Strokes.

Then use any of these actions:

- Click an edge to stroke it.
- Drag across several edges to stroke them at the same time.
- Double-click an edge to flood paint from that edge to all connected edges with the same color.
- Triple-click an edge to paint every edge with that same color.

Note

Press Shift to quickly switch between painting only strokes and painting only fills. The Live Paint Bucket Options dialog box also lets you set these behaviors. If both Paint Fills and Paint Strokes are currently selected, pressing Shift changes the mode to Paint Fills only. This is useful when filling a small face surrounded by stroked edges.
