# Change the caps or joins of a line

Change stroke cap and join styles from the Stroke panel.

A Cap is the endpoint of an open line; a join is the point where a straight line changes direction or turns a corner. To alter a line's caps and corners, adjust the stroke attributes on the object.

Open the Stroke panel with Window > Stroke.

Select the object in the artwork.

In the Stroke panel, choose Show Options from the Stroke panel menu .

To change caps, choose from these options:

- Butt Cap: makes stroked lines end squarely.
- Round Cap: gives stroked lines semicircular ends.
- Projecting Cap: creates squared line ends that extend by half the line width beyond the line endpoint. With this option, the line weight extends evenly in every direction around the line.

To change joins, choose from these options:

- Miter Join: creates pointed corners on stroked lines. Enter a Limit between 1 and 500.
- Round Join: creates rounded corners on stroked lines.
- Bevel Join: creates squared corners on stroked lines.

Modify corner styles in the Stroke panel.
