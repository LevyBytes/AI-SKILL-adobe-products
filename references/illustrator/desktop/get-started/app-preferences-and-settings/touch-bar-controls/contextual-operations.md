# Contextual operations

Available context-specific operations change based on what you are working on in Adobe Illustrator.

- In the Start workspace, the Touch Bar offers document creation and opening controls.
- With nothing selected in the Illustrator workspace, the Touch Bar offers controls for creating and opening a document, placing a file, showing or hiding rulers, grids, and guides, and working with artboards.
- With a path object selected, the Touch Bar offers fill color, stroke color, opacity, alignment, and arrangement controls.
- With multiple path objects selected, the Touch Bar offers fill color, stroke color, opacity, alignment, and arrangement controls. Pathfinder effects and grouping for selected objects are also available.
- With a text object selected, the Touch Bar offers fill color, stroke color, opacity, alignment, arrangement, paragraph alignment, and font size controls.
- With multiple text objects selected, the Touch Bar offers fill color, stroke color, opacity, alignment, arrangement, and paragraph alignment controls. The selected text objects can also be grouped from the Touch Bar.
- When the selection includes a text object and a path object, the Touch Bar offers fill color, stroke color, opacity, alignment, and arrangement controls. The selected objects can also be grouped with the Touch Bar.
- With an image selected, the Touch Bar offers opacity, alignment, and arrangement controls. It also provides an option to crop or mask the image.
- With a grouped object selected, the Touch Bar offers controls for group opacity, alignment, and arrangement. The objects can also be ungrouped from the Touch Bar.
- With any other object selected, the Touch Bar offers opacity, alignment, and arrangement controls.
- With multiple other objects selected, the Touch Bar offers opacity, alignment, and arrangement controls. The objects can also be grouped from the Touch Bar.
