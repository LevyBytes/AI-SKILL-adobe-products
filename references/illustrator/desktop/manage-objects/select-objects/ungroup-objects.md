# Ungroup objects

Ungrouping returns items to independent editing and movement. Grouped artwork is stacked behind the frontmost object and can move onto the top selected layer. In the Layers panel, groups are shown as &lt;Group&gt; entries, and items can be moved into or out of those groups there.

With the Selection tool, choose the grouped object.

To separate the objects, right-click and choose Ungroup; to clear every grouping, including nested groups, choose Ungroup All.
