# Align and distribute artboards.

From the Tools panel, choose the Artboard tool.

Choose one or more artboards for alignment or distribution.

Choose Window > Align.

In the Align panel, choose the settings you need:

- Align Objects: aligns selected artboards in relation to one another.
- Distribute Objects: distributes selected artboards evenly using their centers.
- Distribute Spacing: spaces selected artboards evenly by a specified distance.
- Align To: sets the reference point for aligning or distributing selected artboards.

Use the Align panel for alignment choices such as Align to Artboard, Align to Selection, or other options.

Apply options such as Align to Artboard and Align to Selection from the Align panel.
