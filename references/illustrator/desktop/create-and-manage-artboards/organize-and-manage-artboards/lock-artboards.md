# Lock artboards

Lock objects on Illustrator artboards when accidental selection or edits need to be prevented.

In files with many artboards and layered content, locking an artboard protects its objects from unintended movement or editing while you work elsewhere in the design.

Press Shift + O or choose the Artboard tool from the toolbar.

Choose the artboard to lock.

After the target artboard is chosen, right-click or control-click (macOS), open Lock Artboards, then choose one of these options:

- Lock Selected
- Lock All Except Selected
- Lock All

Another method is to open the Artboards panel, select an artboard, and select the lock control to lock the artboard.

Locking artboards helps keep layouts intact while you edit other designs.

Keep layouts intact during edits to other designs by locking artboards.

To unlock an artboard, go to the Artboards panel and deselect the lock control.

Tip

The Artboards panel indicates whether an artboard is locked: an icon appears next to the artboard name when locked, and the space remains blank when editable.
