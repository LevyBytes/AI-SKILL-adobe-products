# Queue panel

Place encoding inputs in the Queue panel. Sources can include video or audio files, Adobe Premiere sequences, and Adobe After Effects compositions. Add them by dragging files into the queue, or use Add Source and choose the media to encode.

Jobs can also be sent straight into the queue from other Adobe applications, letting work continue while AME renders in the background. Export to AME is supported by these Adobe applications:

- Premiere
- After Effects
- Audition
- Character Animator

Once the queue is started, Adobe Media Encoder encodes the items it contains. AME can begin automatically after an item is added, or it can wait until you start the queue manually. A preference can also trigger encoding after a chosen delay following the addition of a new item to the encoding queue.

Add, delete, or reorder entries directly in the Queue panel. For details, see Add and manage items in the encoding queue.

Once video and audio sources are queued, additional presets can be applied from the Preset Browser; output details can also be changed in the Export Settings or Ingest Settings dialogs. Related guidance is in Encode using presets or custom settings.
