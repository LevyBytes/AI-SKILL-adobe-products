## The work behind the references

Adobe Media Encoder references for managing encoding jobs and automating delivery workflows. Desktop controls, scripting objects and automation examples are grouped separately to connect queue operations with their configuration. Behavior depends on the installed Media Encoder version and media formats, and examples come with their queue and event prerequisites.
