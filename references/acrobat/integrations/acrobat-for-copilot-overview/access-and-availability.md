# Access and availability

Free-plan users included, every user can reach Acrobat for Copilot. At the moment it ships in English across all regions that carry Adobe products and services. After you connect it, kick off any prompt with @Adobe Acrobat, and Acrobat handles your PDF-related requests on its own.

Follow [Edit and organize PDFs](../edit-and-organize-pdfs-in-copilot.md) for connection and output steps.
