# Acrobat for Copilot overview

See the ways Adobe Acrobat inside Copilot can streamline and speed up your PDF work.

Acrobat for Copilot reshapes the whole way you handle PDFs. Creating, editing, arranging, and pulling information out of documents becomes a matter of plain-language commands, done in a friendly, straightforward manner. The chat itself puts strong PDF tools at your fingertips, with no need to jump between applications.

Be it polished documents you're putting together, intricate workflows you're steering, or research material you're sorting, Acrobat for Copilot lets you land accurate, high-quality outcomes with little effort.
