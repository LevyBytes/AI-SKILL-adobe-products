# Efficiency in meetings with AI Assistant

Getting more out of a meeting with AI Assistant starts before the meeting itself: line up the participants and turn on recording/transcription. Once it wraps, a notification lands along with suggested questions that give you a starting point. From there, the AI Assistant prompt lets you:

- Create meeting notes: Choosing Create meeting notes on the bot notification launches the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Viewer and builds a meeting summary. Pass that summary along to everyone by choosing Send Summary.
- Get useful insights: Ask the AI assistant to draft meeting notes, lay out the concerns that came up, clarify what the meeting set out to achieve, and pin down the next steps.

Whatever action you want to take, the suggested options in the AI bot notification will get you there.

For Acrobat to alert organizers once their meeting has been recorded, the meeting transcripts notifications settings have to be switched on in the Admin Config Portal. If your admin already enabled that setting and the notification still never arrives, confirm that notifications from the Acrobat for Microsoft Teams app are turned on. To handle that, head to the Chat in Adobe Acrobat app inside Microsoft Teams, type "Notifications on," and select Send.
