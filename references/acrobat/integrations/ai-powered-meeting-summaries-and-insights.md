# AI-powered meeting summaries and insights

See how meeting summaries and insights get produced with the Acrobat AI Assistant and Generative Summary tools.

Ask a question about what a PDF contains and the Acrobat AI Assistant tool returns thorough answers in short order. It ties its replies back to the text and data tables sitting inside the document, and it proposes follow-up questions worth asking.

Document outlines come from the Generative Summary tool, which lays out headings alongside summaries that call out the main points. To turn around summaries faster, the feature works through PDF content in pieces while surfacing the key takeaways.

For specifics on how to use it, along with the guidelines and limitations, see Adobe Acrobat's Generative AI guide.

Note

Should the AI Assistant be unavailable to you, reach out to your admin and request access.
