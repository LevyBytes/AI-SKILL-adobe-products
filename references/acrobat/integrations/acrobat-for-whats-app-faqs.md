# Acrobat for WhatsApp FAQs

## Which platforms work?

Use WhatsApp Web in Chrome or Edge, or WhatsApp Desktop on Windows. macOS desktop, mobile and Business API access are unsupported. Standard and business accounts are eligible worldwide in supported languages.

## What can I do?

Open PDFs, annotate them, use Acrobat editing tools and share through Send to WhatsApp.

## Do I need a subscription?

Viewing and basic tools are free. Editing requires a paid Acrobat plan and sign-in or upgrade when prompted.

## Why is the feature missing?

Availability is phased. Confirm the platform is supported, then refresh the browser or restart the Windows app.

## Can I send PDF copies?

Use Send to WhatsApp for a PDF originating in WhatsApp; direct copy sharing is unsupported.

## What happens to protected PDFs?

Session and conversation association remains, and password restrictions still apply.

## What if opening or sharing fails?

Check browser pop-up blocking and that your WhatsApp session is active. Retry after refresh or restart. For opening problems, follow [Why can't I open a PDF](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/kb/cant-open-pdf.html).
