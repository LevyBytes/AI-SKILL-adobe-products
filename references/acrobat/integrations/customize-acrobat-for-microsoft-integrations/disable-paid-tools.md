# Disable paid tools

Switching on the Disable paid tools setting greys out the paid tools in the Acrobat viewer for Microsoft Teams, SharePoint, or OneDrive whenever a user is free or not signed in. The paid tools drop off the Acrobat Home page too.

Leave the setting off and every user sees the paid tools no matter their sign-in status or account type. People who aren't signed in get a prompt to log in, while those on free accounts are told to request paid tool access from the organization admin.
