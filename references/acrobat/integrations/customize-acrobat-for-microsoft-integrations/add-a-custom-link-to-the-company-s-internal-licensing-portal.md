# Add a custom link to the company’s internal licensing portal

Direct your free users toward the established process your organization uses for requesting a paid Acrobat license. Inside Acrobat, the custom link to your internal licensing portal can be surfaced in these spots:

- In the All tools pane in the Acrobat PDF viewer
- In the Acrobat Home tab

On Acrobat for SharePoint and OneDrive, the Request license link lets users apply for a paid Acrobat license by way of your organization.

On Acrobat for SharePoint and OneDrive, the Request license link lets users apply for a paid Acrobat license by way of your organization. Note

Should a free user pick a paid tool, a message explains that premium tools aren't covered by their plan, along with a link to Request a license.
