Use `storage: "DROPBOX"` with a provider-issued temporary URL that permits the required download or upload. Obtaining that URL requires the application's Dropbox authorization. The temporary URL carries delegated access for its consumer; the absence of a separate access token on that transfer does not remove the issuer's authorization requirements.

For input, [get_temporary_link](https://docs.dropboxapi.com/dropbox-api/api-reference/user-endpoints/files/get-temporary-link) requires `files.content.read` and returns a file-content link valid for four hours. For output, [get_temporary_upload_link](https://docs.dropboxapi.com/dropbox-api/api-reference/user-endpoints/files/get-temporary-upload-link) requires `files.content.write` and returns a one-use upload link. That upload link is consumed with `POST` and `application/octet-stream`; it expires after at most four hours. Do not treat it as a reusable generic PUT URL.

Check the [Dropbox host patterns](supported-url-prefixes.md#dropbox) and [Adobe transport constraints](overview-limitation.md). Use the operation's [external-storage request](../overview-external-storage-for-adobe-pdf-services-apis.md) with the correct provider and transfer direction.
