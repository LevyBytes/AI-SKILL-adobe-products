# Linearize Pdf

> Adobe Acrobat reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

Tune your PDFs so they open snappily on the web, with mobile readers especially in mind. Through linearization, the people you share with can pull up a hefty PDF a piece at a time, which means pages surface much sooner even when bandwidth is tight.

## Linearize PDF

Running linearization on a PDF yields a web-optimized file that permits piecemeal access whenever it travels across a network.

For guidance on calling our APIs, consult the [API usage guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AdobeDocs/pdfservices-api-documentation/main/src/pages/overview/pdf-services-api/howtos/api-usage.md).
