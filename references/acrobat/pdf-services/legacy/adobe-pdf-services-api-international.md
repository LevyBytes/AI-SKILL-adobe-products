# Adobe PDF Services API (International)

We appreciate your interest in subscribing to the Adobe PDF Services API on Amazon Web Services. Purchases of the international subscription are restricted to billing addresses located in these allowed countries:

India, Indonesia, Malaysia, South Africa, Japan, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Canada, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Isle of Man, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, and UK.

For customers based in countries left off this list, additional ways to buy the Adobe PDF Services API may exist. Check our [pricing page](https://developer.adobe.com/document-services/pricing/main#adobe-pdf-services-api) to see what purchase options are open to you, or [contact us](https://www.adobe.io/apis/documentcloud/dcsdk/form.html) if you would like more details.
