# Image usage rights

- Usage of the Generate presentation feature in Adobe Acrobat is capped in certain ways; a small number of free generations may be granted so users can sample the service, and continuing past that requires buying a plan that entitles Acrobat AI Assistant features. Adobe keeps the right to revise usage limits, or to throttle or suspend activity beyond those limits, at any moment.
- The Adobe Stock image library and the usage rights available to Acrobat Studio subscribers match those of Adobe Express Premium users, although the library does not cover every Adobe Stock image.
- Right now there is no extra charge for AI-generated visuals produced with third-party models. That could change later through usage limits or subscription requirements.
