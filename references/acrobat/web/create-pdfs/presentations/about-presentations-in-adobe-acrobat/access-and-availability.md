# Access and availability

- Access points: Both the web and desktop apps surface the presentation tool, yet launching it on the desktop hands you off to the web interface, since generation happens in the cloud. Entry is possible from the homepage, the Create tab, PDF Spaces, and while viewing a document.
- Languages & regions: Offered in the supported languages across the countries that carry Adobe Acrobat.
- Availability: The Generate presentation feature is reachable by Individual, Teams, and Enterprise subscribers whose plan covers Acrobat Studio, AI Assistant, or Acrobat AI capabilities. We keep the option open to charge generative credits for the Generate presentation feature down the road and may revise usage limits whenever we choose.

Support for additional languages and regions is being rolled out by Adobe in stages.
