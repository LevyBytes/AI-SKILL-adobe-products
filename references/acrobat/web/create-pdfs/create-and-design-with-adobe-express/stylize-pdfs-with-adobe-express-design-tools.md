# Stylize PDFs with Adobe Express design tools

Discover how Adobe Express design tools let you stylize your PDFs in a hurry, lifting the layout, colors, and overall visual appeal.

From the Adobe Acrobat homepage, choose Create > Stylize a PDF.

When the dialog box appears, pick the PDF you'd like to enhance.

Your document opens within Adobe Express on a new tab in Acrobat.

Lean on the ready-made options under the Stylize PDF shortcuts to spruce up your PDF fast.

For a unified look spanning the whole document:

- Choose Apply document style and pick from the styles offered in the Document panel.
- Take it further with the color theme options found in the Color panel.

For font styling that fits your design:

- Choose Get font recommendations and pick from the suggested typefaces that match your present text style.
- Look over similar or featured fonts and choose them to apply to your text on the spot.

To reset the dimensions for various formats or platforms:

- Choose Resize page; within the Resize pane, type custom dimensions using the width, height, and unit selectors.
- Toggle aspect ratio locked or unlocked, which either pins proportions or frees them up.
- Pick from recommended sizes such as Flyer, Resume, or Poster.
- Skim preset sizes spanning social media, video, photo, document, and web formats.

To drop in visual elements:

- Choose Add shapes and icons and pick from the basic shapes or themed icons.
- Drag the element and set it wherever you want on the page.
- Resize or recolor them so they suit your design.

To swap the background color:

- Choose Change background color and pick a color from the palette or punch in your brand's HEX code.
- Spread it across all pages or limit it to selected ones.

To bring in Adobe Stock images:

- Choose Add Adobe Stock image, search a keyword, pick an image, and drop it into your document.
- Resize and reposition it however you need.

Head to the Recent list on the Acrobat homepage to reach your updated PDF. From there it's easy to open it for more editing or to share it whenever you like.
