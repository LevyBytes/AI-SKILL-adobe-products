# Create professional documents with templates

Learn the quick path to spinning up marketing documents and social posts from Adobe Express templates, all begun in Acrobat.

From the Adobe Acrobat homepage, choose Create.

Within the Create from a template section, settle on a template type for your document, then pick one of the templates on offer to begin.

Acrobat hands you off to the Adobe Express app to customize it.

A template type can be picked from the choices on offer. For a wider selection of template types, choose View all to bring up the full template library in Adobe Express.

Build out your content with the tools along the left panel:

- Hunt for and drop in design assets
- Lay on saved brand fonts, colors, and logos
- Rely on brand kits to keep design consistent from one project to the next
- Bring in photos, videos, or audio from the media library or your own collection
- Build and revise charts or layout grids that turn data into visuals
- Spin up custom text effects or images with AI

The top-bar tools let you tweak size, lay on themes, add animations, swap background color, and set margins.

Fine-tune your document in Adobe Express, using the top-bar tools to dial in design elements and layout.

Choose Add page to tack on more pages drawn from the template.

To give your template a new name, choose the default file name in the top bar and type the one you want.

Head to the Recent list on the Acrobat homepage to reach your saved template. From there it's easy to open it for editing or to share it whenever you like.
