# Join a Student Space shared with you

Click the Student Space link that landed in your message or email.

The shared content opens straight away for you.

Whether you arrived as a guest or as an invited user, poke around the Student Space. What's available to you depends on your access:

- View uploaded sources
- Work with study tools that other people built
- Take a shot at building study tools of your own, as far as your access permits

Go over and cite the material:

- Follow the attribution links back to wherever the source material sits
- Dig into Curated Student Spaces to round out your understanding

Pitch in by building study tools, posting notes or insights, and dropping back into the Student Space to keep the collaboration going and watch your progress.
