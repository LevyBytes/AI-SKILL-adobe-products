# Why use multimedia study tools

Multimedia study tools earn their place when your aim is to:

- Go over content quicker, or while you're out and about
- Take things in by way of audio or visuals
- Team up with peers to jointly create and polish study materials
- Present as a group by spinning shared study content into slides, with no need to redo the work

Tip

Begin with text-based study tools to get the concepts down, then move over to multimedia tools for review; the sources you started with carry through.
