# Presentation

With the Presentation tool, whatever you've gathered gets shaped into an organized slide deck, so your thinking lands clearly and you can present it with assurance.

What shapes a presentation

- A few starting choices drive what gets built: who you're presenting to, how many slides you want, and which template you pick.
- Every deck arrives with an outline that summarizes what each slide covers, so the overall flow is clear to you before you ever present or hand it off.

How to fine-tune the deck

- Edit slide content and summaries
- Delete or duplicate slides
- Reorder slides by dragging them
- Add new slides as needed

Branding and extras

- Change themes and apply branding
- Insert media such as photos, videos, or music
- Use AI-powered tools to further refine content and design

Getting the deck out to others

- Present directly from Student Spaces
- Download the deck in formats such as PPT, PDF, JPEG, PNG, or individual PDF files
