# Upload logos

See how to put your logo on the emails and the experiences your recipients see.

Note

A logo image runs 60px tall by 200px wide, and PNG is the format we recommend for it.

Starting from the Acrobat-on-the-web homepage, head to E-Sign > Add e-sign branding.

Note

Getting at the Add e-sign branding feature calls for an active subscription to either Adobe Acrobat Pro or Acrobat Pro for teams.

A Teams admin running on an Acrobat Standard for teams subscription gets nudged to move up a plan. When the dialog shows up, select Manage Account to keep going. As for Teams users who want the feature, the route is to ask their admins for it.

Once you're on the Account Settings > Brand Settings page, hit Upload logo.

Pick out the image file you'd like to serve as your logo, then hit Open.

Select Save.

The Account Setup page of Acrobat Sign is another spot where you can upload a logo image.

From here on, every one of your users will see your logo.
