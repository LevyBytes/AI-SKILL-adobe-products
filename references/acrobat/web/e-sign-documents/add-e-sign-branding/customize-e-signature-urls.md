# Customize e-signature URLs

See how to put your own domain on the signing URL so signers can tell the agreement came from you.

Reshaping your e-signature request links in Acrobat on the web is straightforward, and it hands recipients a signing experience that feels more polished and personal.

Starting from the Acrobat-on-the-web homepage, head to E-Sign > Add e-sign branding.

Note

Getting at the Add e-sign branding feature calls for an active subscription to either Adobe Acrobat Pro or Acrobat Pro for teams.

A Teams admin running on an Acrobat Standard for teams subscription gets nudged to move up a plan. When the dialog shows up, select Manage Account to keep going. As for Teams users who want the feature, the route is to ask their admins for it.

Once you're on the Account Settings > Brand Settings page, track down the Customize agreement URL option.

Whatever custom brand value you'd like folded into your Acrobat Sign URLs goes into the URL address field.

Select Save.

From here on, your brand rides along in the signing URLs.
