# See how to roll the Company name out to every user.

Folding your company name into your e-signature workflows is one way to keep branding uniform and your document transactions clear.

Note

Living in each user's profile is a Company name property, which certain agreements may call for as part of signing.

Starting from the Acrobat-on-the-web homepage, head to E-Sign > Add e-sign branding.

Note

Getting at the Add e-sign branding feature calls for an active subscription to either Adobe Acrobat Pro or Acrobat Pro for teams.

A Teams admin running on an Acrobat Standard for teams subscription gets nudged to move up a plan. When the dialog shows up, select Manage Account to keep going. As for Teams users who want the feature, the route is to ask their admins for it.

Once you're on the Account Settings > Brand Settings page, work your way down to the Add company name section.

Drop your company's name into the Company name field.

Want every user on the account to carry the company name? Tick the 'Set company name for all users in account' checkbox.

Want every user on the account to carry the company name? Tick the 'Set company name for all users in account' checkbox. Note

Ticking the Set company name for all users in account checkbox and saving pushes that company name out to every user. That said, individual users are still free to swap the company name on their own profiles.

Select Save.

Note

The Company name field value is off-limits to admins. Changing it at the account level means reaching out to Acrobat Sign support.
