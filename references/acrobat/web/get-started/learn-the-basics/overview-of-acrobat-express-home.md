# Overview of Acrobat Express home

Get acquainted with Acrobat Express home, the single platform where document insights, sharing of information, and content creation all come together.

Powered by AI, Acrobat Express home folds in Acrobat AI Assistant alongside Adobe Express to speed up how you grasp documents with AI, lift collaboration, and shape ideas into eye-catching designs, images, and videos. Only users entitled to Acrobat Express get access to the Acrobat Express home experience.
