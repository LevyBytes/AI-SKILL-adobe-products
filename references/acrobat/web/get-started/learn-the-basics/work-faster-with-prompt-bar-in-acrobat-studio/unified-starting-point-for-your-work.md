# Unified starting point for your work

Built to act as a smart command surface, the prompt bar gathers your routine tasks in one spot so your attention stays on the outcome you're chasing. Be it reviewing a document, producing content, or hunting through your files, the prompt bar strips out the friction and gets you to a result sooner.

Out of the prompt bar you can reach for a hand-picked group of high‑value actions, among them:

- Posing questions about your documents through Doc Q&A and pulling back instant answers rather than digging through the content by hand.
- Producing images with Adobe Express straight off the homepage.
- Putting together polished presentations within minutes by way of natural‑language commands.
- Looking across PDFs, PDF Spaces, and Adobe Express files, every bit of it from a single spot.
- Reaching Acrobat tools on the spot while you work on desktop.
- Browsing ready‑to‑use Adobe Express templates for flyers, invoices, and further projects.

Together these abilities hand you quicker entry to the tools already in your routine while opening up fresh ways to work and create.

Lean on the prompt bar to kick off tasks in a hurry - examine documents, build visuals, assemble presentations, or page through templates.
