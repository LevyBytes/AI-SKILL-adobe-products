# Get started with Acrobat Express home

Think of Acrobat Express home as the doorway to the Acrobat and Express tools that ship with your plan. It's shaped around the daily rhythms of how business professionals get work done.

Acrobat Express home experience

Acrobat Express home experience

Within Acrobat Express home, the prompt bar doubles as one shared doorway into all of the following:

- Template discovery: Browse ready‑to‑use Adobe Express templates that turn the content you start with into polished, high-impact results — think flyers, invoices, and other projects.
- Document analysis: Lean on Doc Q&A to drop in files, pull together the headline findings via Acrobat AI Assistant, lift out action items, or weigh insights across documents with citations and sources tied back to the source content. Questions can also go straight into your PDF Spaces, where those insights become real collaboration with your team.
- File search: Look across PDFs, PDF Spaces, and Adobe Express files, every bit of it from a single spot.

Backed by Adobe Acrobat and Adobe Express, Acrobat Express home also carries action cards that keep everyday content jobs straightforward and intuitive:

Upload a file: Open the file browser to send up documents, images, or videos. From there Acrobat Express steers each file to its proper experience on its own—documents head into document workflows, while media goes toward content creation.

Start a new design: Out of a modal, pick a format such as presentations, flyers, or social posts, and dial in custom sizes.

Edit photos: Run quick actions like clearing the background, resizing, cropping, and conversion.

Create PDFs and presentations: Reach for the core PDF tools to build, convert, export, protect, and compress files, or spin up presentations straight out of your documents.

Create a PDF Space: Pull several files into one AI workspace, surface insights, and produce content such as presentations and podcasts. Hand it off with a prebuilt AI Assistant, or roll your own to steer how it responds.

Acrobat Express home additionally lets you flip between the Recent and Templates folders so professionally designed flyers, presentations, social posts, and more are right at hand.
