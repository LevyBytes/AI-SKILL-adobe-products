# Unshare PDFs

A walkthrough for stripping collaborators of their access to files you've shared.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Documents > Your documents.

Pick a file you've shared with others, then choose Unshare .

Files you've shared can also be picked out of the 'Shared by you' section beneath the Documents tab.

When the Unshare file dialog box appears, choose Unshare.

Note

Pulling sharing on a file sends no alert to the recipients, yet their access ends all the same.

Sharing on the file ends, and the people who originally received it lose their access.
