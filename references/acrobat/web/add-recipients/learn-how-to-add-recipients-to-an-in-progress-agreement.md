# Learn how to add recipients to an in-progress agreement.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, choose Documents.

Over in the left pane, choose Shared by you.

Scan the document list and tick the checkbox beside the agreement you want to bring more recipients into.

In the right pane, click the # people drop-down menu to unfold the recipient list.

Choose Add people.

Tack on additional recipients by choosing Add people alongside the people already listed.

Type the email of whoever you'd like to bring in into the Enter email address field.

That email then shows up beneath the field. Keep going to enter every recipient's email address.

Choose Send invitation.

The fresh recipients join the agreement and get a notification about it.
