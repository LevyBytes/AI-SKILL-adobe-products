# Extract pages

Find out how to pull pages out of a PDF and keep them as their own separate PDF.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, pick Edit > Extract pages.

Pick Select files, then browse your device to bring in the PDF whose pages you want to pull out.

Mark the pages you intend to pull out.

Tapping a page thumbnail lets you spin or remove any of the ones you've marked.

Through the thumbnails you can rotate or delete whichever selected pages you choose.

Pick Extract.

The pulled pages land in Adobe cloud storage on their own as a brand-new PDF, saved for you.
