# Crop pages

See how trimming the visible region of PDF pages works in Acrobat on the web.

From the Acrobat on the web homepage, pick Edit > Crop pages.

Pick Select files, then browse your device to bring in the PDF you mean to edit.

Resize the crop region by dragging the handles on its border.

Want every page to share these crop margins? Choose All under the Page Range section.

Inside the blue bounding box sits the cropped page. Adjusting the Page Range settings lets one crop carry across all pages or just a chosen set.

That blue bounding box frames the cropped page; tweak the Page Range settings and the same crop spreads to every page or a subset you pick.

Pick Crop.

Cropping finishes on the PDF pages, and the file tucks itself into Adobe cloud storage automatically.
