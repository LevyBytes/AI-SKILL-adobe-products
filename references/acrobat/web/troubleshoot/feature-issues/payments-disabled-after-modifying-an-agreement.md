# Payments disabled after modifying an agreement

How to fix payment that gets switched off once an agreement has been edited.

When the original first recipient hands off their authority to sign, or the sender edits the agreement after that hand-off, payment can end up skipped, so the agreement reaches completion without any payment being secured.

Head to Acrobat Sign and sign in with your admin credentials.

Select Account Settings > Send Settings.

Within the Modify Agreements section, tick the Allow senders to modify agreements checkbox.

Granting senders permission to modify agreements lets them edit an agreement even once delegation has occurred, all without breaking payment processing.

Select Save.
