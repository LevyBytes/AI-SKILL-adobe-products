# Share PDF Spaces

Share a PDF Space as a guided experience, so recipients grasp what the content covers, where to begin, and how to engage with it.

Being the PDF Space owner, you get to shape how your content is introduced and taken in before sharing. That covers polishing AI‑generated summaries, laying out items on the Overview page, and folding in elements such as notes, podcasts, or branding.

The first time recipients open your shared PDF Space, they arrive at a structured Overview that quickly conveys what matters, how the content is arranged, and what to do next.

Head to PDF Spaces and then open the PDF Space you intend to share.

Shape the Overview experience if you need to:

- Shuffle the order of items, or take some out
- Revise the text or audio summaries that AI generated
- Bring in supporting pieces: notes, text blocks, podcasts, or a logo for branding

Note

By shaping the Overview, you steer recipients and set their expectations ahead of opening any individual file. Find out how to customize the PDF Spaces experience.

From the top menu, pick Share.

Within the Share dialog box, pick Change beneath Link settings, then decide on both a permission level and how the link grants access.

Access settings:

- Anyone with the link
- Anyone in &lt;your-organization&gt; with the link
- Invited people only

Permission levels:

- Add comments, and add or remove files and notes
- Add comments
- View files and notes

Note

Each recipient's permissions remain yours to change whenever you like.

Pick Done.

Decide how to share:

- Invite people: Type the recipient's name or email, look over the message, and pick Send.
- Direct link share: Pick Create a link to share and pass the copied link along to recipients.
- Share via third-party apps: Choose one of the third-party apps — Outlook, Gmail, Teams, or WhatsApp, say — then do as the onscreen instructions direct.

Before sharing, settle on a permission level to govern how others reach your PDF Space, so recipients view and work with the content the way you meant.

Find out more about interactive experience sharing with PDF Spaces so you understand the way recipients work with shared spaces and which engagement insights you can track.

Note

To stop sharing a PDF Space, hover over it and then pick Options > Unshare PDF Space. To remove it, pick Delete from the Options menu.
