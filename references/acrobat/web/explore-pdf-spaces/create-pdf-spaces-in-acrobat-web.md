# Create PDF Spaces in Acrobat web

Walk through setting up PDF Spaces in Acrobat on the web, where content stays organized and both analysis and content creation are AI-powered.

Several files and links can be brought together so you get AI-powered insights and keep your research within one conversational knowledge hub. Drawing on the files and links you've added, it builds summaries, fields questions, brings key insights to the surface, and turns out podcasts and presentations.
