# Customizable Overview (entry point)

Editable content items are the building blocks of your PDF Space experience. Drag items into a new order or take them out so the flow follows your intent instead of the default file sequence. Pick a background color theme to set the look of your PDF Space, and pass it along with a rich link preview that previews the contents for recipients before they ever click in.

Out of the box, a PDF Space spins up a headline, files, an audio summary, and a text summary. Should the underlying content shift, those pieces refresh on their own — unless you've already edited them by hand.
