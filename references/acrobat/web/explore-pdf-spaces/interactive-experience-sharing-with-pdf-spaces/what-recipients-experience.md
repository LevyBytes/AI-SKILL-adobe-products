# What recipients experience

A shared link is the way in for recipients, dropping them onto a guided Overview page that gets them oriented right away.

From that page they can grasp the point through headlines and summaries, scan the material visually with file thumbnails, play the audio summaries, or drill into the individual files. Where it's turned on, the AI Assistant is also there for exploring insights or posing questions.

Notes, images, podcasts, and other extra formats open up fuller ways to interact with the material. And to keep things safe and trustworthy, the Overview page lets recipients flag abuse without leaving it.
