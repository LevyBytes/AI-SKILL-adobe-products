# Pinning down which track and version your Acrobat product runs on.

Open Acrobat.

Your Acrobat version shows up once you choose Help > About Acrobat (Windows) or Acrobat > About Acrobat (macOS).

Note

Depending on which subscription you hold, the name shown for the product can differ.

What appears in that pop-up is a version string: a run of digits and letters encoding the release year, build ID, and track ID for your copy of Acrobat.

Identify your Adobe Acrobat Reader product versioning.

A. Release year B. Track ID

Your track is revealed by the eighth digit of that version string:

- 2: Denotes Continuous track
- 3: Denotes Classic track.

Note

To suit different customer needs, Acrobat ships on two tracks, Continuous and Classic. For the full picture, consult Acrobat product tracks.
