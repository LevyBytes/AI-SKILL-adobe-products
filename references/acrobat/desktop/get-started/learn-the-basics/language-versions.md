# Language versions

These are the languages the Acrobat 2020 desktop app ships in:


- English
- Danish
- Turkish
- French
- Finnish
- Hungarian
- German
- Norwegian
- Ukrainian
- Japanese
- Simplified Chinese
- Slovak*
- Italian
- Traditional Chinese
- Slovenian*
- Spanish
- Korean
- North African French #
- Dutch
- Czech
- Middle Eastern Hebrew §
- Brazilian Portuguese
- Polish
- Middle Eastern Arabic §
- Swedish
- Russian

Source: [Adobe 2020 requirements](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/acrobat/desktop/get-started/learn-the-basics/tech-requirements-acrobat-2020.html).
- Available on Windows only.

The North African French (Français) version is a French application user interface with Arabic/Hebrew right-to-left language support enabled by default.

§ With Arabic and Hebrew, English drives the application user interface, and right-to-left support for Arabic and Hebrew comes switched on out of the box.

Which languages you can use shifts depending on the Acrobat app and service features in play. The Acrobat language tiers documentation has the details.

Note

Anyone using Adobe online services must be 13 or older and has to accept extra terms together with the Adobe Privacy Policy. Coverage of the online services does not span every country or language, registration may be required, and Adobe can change or withdraw any or all of them without notice. Extra fees or subscription costs can come into play.
