# Navigate through pages - Open and navigate an article thread

Think of an article as a guided route through a PDF: it carries the reader along one storyline, leaping past any pages or page regions that fall outside that thread. The experience mirrors how you'd flip through a printed newspaper or magazine, staying with a single piece and passing over everything else. As you follow it along, the view scales up or down so whatever portion of the article you're on takes up the whole screen. Here's how to follow an article thread inside a PDF:

From the Side Panels, open Articles.

A double-click on the Article icon kicks off your reading.

Stepping along the thread is done with Enter, Shift-click, Shift + Enter, or Ctrl-click.

Want out before the thread finishes? With the Hand tool active, press Shift + Ctrl-click, which drops you back to the page view you had before.
