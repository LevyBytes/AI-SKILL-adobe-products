# View PDFs in Full Screen mode

Full Screen mode shows nothing but the document — the menu bar, toolbars, task panes, and window controls all disappear. Either the PDF's creator configures it to launch this way or you switch the view on yourself. Presentations frequently rely on Full Screen mode, occasionally pairing it with automatic page advancement and transitions.

Because the pointer stays live in Full Screen mode, you can still click links and open notes. Moving through a PDF this way can be done two ways: keyboard shortcuts handle navigation and magnification commands, and a Full Screen preference can put navigation buttons on screen that you click to flip pages or leave Full Screen mode.

Review PDFs can also be opened in Full Screen mode so attention stays on the content and the comments sent your way. The shortcut Ctrl + L flips you into Full Screen mode.

1. Within the Preferences dialog box, under Categories, pick Full Screen.
   Turn on Show Navigation Bar, then click OK.

From the hamburger menu (Windows) > View, or the View menu (macOS), pick Full Screen Mode.

Previous Page , Next Page , and Close Full Screen View buttons make up the Full Screen navigation bar. They sit in the lower-left corner of the work area.
