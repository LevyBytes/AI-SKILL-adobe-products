# Display PDFs in Line Weights view

Lines render at the weights set in the PDF when Line Weights view is on. With it off, every line gets a fixed one-pixel stroke regardless of zoom. Printing the document, though, reproduces the stroke at its real width.

Choose (Windows) > View or View (macOS), then pick > Show/Hide > Rulers & Grids > Line Weights. Switch the Line Weights view off by choosing > View (Windows), View (macOS) > Show/Hide > Rulers & Grids > Line Weights once more.

Note

Line Weights view will not turn off while you view PDFs inside a web browser.

As you view a PDF, Menu > View > Show/Hide (Windows) or View > Show/Hide (macOS) brings these Acrobat elements into view:

- Menu Bar: (macOS) Show or hide the menu bar through View > Show/Hide > Menu Bar. The shortcut Shift + Command + M does it as well.

  - (Windows) Open the hamburger menu at the upper left, then go to View > Show/Hide > Customize toolbar.
  - (macOS) Choose View > Show/Hide > Customize toolbar.

  - (Windows) Open the hamburger menu at the upper left, then go to View > Show/Hide > Side panels.
  - (macOS) Choose View > Show/Hide > Side panels.

  - (Windows) Open the hamburger menu at the upper left, then go to View > Show/Hide > Rulers & Grids > Grid.
  - (macOS) Choose View > Show/Hide > Rulers & Grids > Grid.

When the grid is on display, a check mark sits beside the command name. There is more under view PDFs of Grids, guides, and measurements.

- (Windows) Open the hamburger menu at the upper left. Go to View > Show/Hide > Cursor Coordinates.
- (macOS) Choose View > Show/Hide > Cursor Coordinates.
