# Modify e-signatures

See how to revise your e-signature in Adobe Acrobat so it reads more accurately and feels more personal.

While you fill in and sign PDFs, Acrobat lets you refresh or swap out your e-signature. Switching the signature style, nudging where it sits, or removing one and dropping in a replacement are all straightforward.

Open a PDF document, then pick E-Sign on the global bar.

Within the current signature box in the left pane, choose Remove your signature X.

Choose Add signature.

When the dialog box appears, type, draw, or drop in an image of whichever signature you want to add.

Reshape how the signature looks by opening the Change style dropdown menu and picking the style you prefer.

Choose Apply, then click the spot in the document where the signature should go.

Need to move the signature? Hover until the plus icon shows up, then drag.

For resizing, pull the blue corner handle.

Note

Once you've signed a PDF form, your e-signature is locked in securely, which blocks casual edits or deletion. That keeps the document intact.
