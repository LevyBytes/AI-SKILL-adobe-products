# Windows

Open Menu > Preferences.

Within the Preferences dialog box, pick Signatures from the menu on the left, then choose More inside the Creation & Appearance section.

Inside the Appearances section, choose New.

When the dialog box appears, give the signature a title.

Under Configure Graphic:

- Pick No graphic if you want text on its own.
- Pick Imported graphic when you'd like to bring in an image (for instance, a scan of your handwritten signature). Choose File > Browse to upload the image.
- Pick Name to show nothing but the default icon alongside the name pulled from your digital ID.

Beneath Configure Text, decide which details to show, for example your name and organization. Ticking Distinguished name surfaces the particulars stored in your digital ID.

Beneath Text Properties, set the writing direction together with the style of digits.

Graphics, text details, and text direction for the signature are all adjustable from the Configure Signature Appearance dialog.

Choose OK.

Tip

Want your handwritten signature included? Scan it and store it as an image. You'll get the cleanest result by dropping it into a document, saving that as a PDF, and importing from there.
