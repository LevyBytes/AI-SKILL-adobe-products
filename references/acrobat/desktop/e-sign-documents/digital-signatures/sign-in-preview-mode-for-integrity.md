# Sign in preview mode for integrity

Find out how to sign while Preview document mode is on so you can confirm a document's integrity in Adobe Acrobat.

For workflows where a signature demands that the document stay trustworthy, turn on View documents in Preview Mode before you sign. The mode scans for anything that could shift how the document looks and holds it back, letting you read and sign a frozen, secure rendering.

Signing under preview mode reveals whether the document carries:

- Dynamic content or dependencies that reach outside the file.
- Items like form fields, multimedia, or JavaScript that could change how it appears.

Once you've gone over the report, you can reach out to whoever authored the document about anything it flagged.
