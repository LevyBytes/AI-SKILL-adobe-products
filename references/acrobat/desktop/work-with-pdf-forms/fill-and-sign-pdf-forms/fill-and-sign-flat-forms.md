# Fill and sign flat forms

Type details into flat forms in Acrobat, sign them, and then share or safely keep them.

A flat form is a scanned paper form or a PDF with no interactive fields to fill in or sign. Acrobat still lets you work with flat forms by laying text or signatures on top.

Open All tools > Fill & Sign.

Click the field that should hold your text. Up comes a text field together with a text box menu.

Either type into the field or pull a signature or initials over from the E-sign panel.

Click the text box and revise it however you need.

If repositioning is needed, hover at the text box's border until the cursor shifts, then drag it into place.

Recolor the typed text by picking Choose a color for type text on the Quick action toolbar.

Switch the text spacing from normal to combed by opening the Options menu on the text box menu and then picking Character spacing.

Once the information is in, save the form.

Note

Acrobat Pro's Prepare a form tool can turn a flat form into a fillable one. Acrobat Reader doesn't offer this.
