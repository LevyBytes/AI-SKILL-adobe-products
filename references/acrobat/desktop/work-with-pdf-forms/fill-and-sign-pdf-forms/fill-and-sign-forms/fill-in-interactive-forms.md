# Fill in interactive forms

On interactive forms you'll find highlighted spots — text fields, checkboxes, or radio buttons — where you enter or pick your information.

Click a highlighted field in the form. The pointer turns into an I-beam or a hand-sign.

Key your details into the text field, or tick checkboxes and choose radio buttons to complete the fields.

Tab moves you ahead through the fields, while Shift + Tab steps you back.

Once it's complete, save the form or pick Send to submit it.
