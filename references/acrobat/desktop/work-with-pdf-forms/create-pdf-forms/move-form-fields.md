# Move form fields

Reposition fields on a PDF form in Adobe Acrobat and reshape its layout with ease.

Start by opening a fillable PDF form.

Open All tools > Prepare a form.

Pick the field — or several fields — that you intend to relocate.

Use whichever of these fits the move you need:

- Reposition fields by dragging your selection wherever you want it to sit.
- Hold Shift as you drag if the motion should stay purely horizontal or vertical.
- For an exact spot, right-click the field, pick Properties, open the Position tab, and key in the Position values.
- For fine horizontal or vertical adjustments, tap the arrow keys to inch the selected field along.
- To recenter a field on the page, right-click it and pick Cut, go to where it belongs, then pick Paste.
