# Insert a blank page into a PDF you already have, using Adobe Acrobat.

Open the PDF that needs a blank page, then head to All tools > Organize pages.

From the left pane, choose Insert > Blank page.

Note

Another route: right-click a page thumbnail and choose Insert Pages > Blank Page.

Add a blank page to your PDF via the Organize Pages tool, choosing Insert > Blank Page over in the left panel.

In the Insert Pages dialog box, say where the blank page belongs:

- Before or after the first page
- Before or after the last page
- Before or after a specific page number

Select OK.

Acrobat then drops the blank page in at the spot you named.
