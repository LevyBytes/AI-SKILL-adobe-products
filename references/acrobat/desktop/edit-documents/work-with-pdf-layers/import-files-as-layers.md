# Import files as layers

Learn how to manage layered content from external files in your PDF.

Pulling in layers transfers layered material out of a second PDF or a supported image and into the PDF you have open. Keeping those visual pieces apart is what lets you govern how they are arranged, edited, and shown. The choices open to you include spinning up fresh layers, dropping content into layer groups that already exist, or holding onto the layer structure exactly as the source document had it.

Before you begin

Confirm first that your source files arrive in one of the supported formats: PDF, BMP, GIF, JPEG, JPEG 2000, PCX, PNG, or TIFF.
