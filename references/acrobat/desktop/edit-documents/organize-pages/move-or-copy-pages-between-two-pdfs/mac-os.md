# macOS

With both PDFs open in Acrobat

Choose Window > Tile > Vertically so the two sit beside each other.

Within one PDF, choose All tools > Organize pages.

Thumbnails for each page show up in the document area.

Pick out the page or pages you mean to move or copy:

- Click a single thumbnail for one page.
- Hold Command and click to gather several pages.

Now move or copy what you picked:

- To move: drag the chosen thumbnails over to where you want them in the second PDF.
- To copy: right-click the chosen thumbnails, pick Copy, then right-click the target spot in the other PDF and choose Paste.

For both PDFs, choose File > Save.
