# Move or copy pages within PDFs

See how Adobe Acrobat handles moving and duplicating pages inside a single PDF.

Acrobat's visual thumbnail layout makes reshuffling pages or cloning material inside one PDF straightforward. Pages slide around or copy over to a different spot whether you drag them or work from the context menu.

With the PDF open in Acrobat, choose All tools > Organize pages.

Thumbnails for the pages turn up in the document area.

Click the thumbnail of whichever page you intend to move or copy.

For more than one page at a time, hold Ctrl (Windows) or Command (macOS) as you click the thumbnails.

Relocating pages is as simple as dragging the chosen thumbnails to a fresh spot.

Tip

There's also the option to move or copy pages by choosing Page thumbnails from the right pane.

Cutting and pasting goes like this: hover a thumbnail, choose More > Cut. Next, click in the gap between thumbnails where it belongs — a blue line flags the position — and choose Paste from the context menu.

Copying and pasting works much the same: hover a thumbnail, choose More > Copy. Next, click in the gap between thumbnails where it belongs — a blue line flags the position — and choose Paste from the context menu.

Drag thumbnails or lean on the context menu to reorder, cut, or copy the pages of a PDF.
