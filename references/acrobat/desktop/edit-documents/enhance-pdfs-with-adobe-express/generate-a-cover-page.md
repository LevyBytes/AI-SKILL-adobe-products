# Generate a cover page

Find out how to turn out clean, professional cover pages for your PDFs inside Adobe Acrobat.

Round off the look of your documents by building a polished, AI-designed cover right inside Acrobat with the Generate cover page tool. To check access, availability, and what the feature requires, see About cover pages in Adobe Acrobat.

Open the PDF in the desktop version of Acrobat.

Select Edit from the global bar.

Select Generate cover page from the left panel.

Browse the generated designs, choose your preferred option, and select Add cover.

Look over several cover designs before you commit to one, pick the layout that suits your document type best, and then tweak the text or styling once it's in place.

Run through the design options first, settle on the layout that fits the kind of document you have, and adjust wording or styling after the cover is inserted.

Update the text on the cover page, if needed.

If you like, select Do more with Adobe Express to send your document into Adobe Express for richer editing, for instance:

- Changing colors
- Resizing pages
- Adding icons or visual elements

When you're done, export the document.

Following customization, your refreshed file shows up in both Acrobat and Adobe Express.

Note

Drawing on the document's content, branding colors, and dimensions, Adobe Acrobat shapes the cover page designs so the whole file holds together visually.
