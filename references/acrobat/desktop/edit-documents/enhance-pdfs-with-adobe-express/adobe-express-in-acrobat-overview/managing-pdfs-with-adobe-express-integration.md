# Managing PDFs with Adobe Express integration

Choosing the Stylize this PDF option in Acrobat duplicates the open PDF and turns the copy into a brand-new Adobe Express file, which opens on a fresh browser tab. Meanwhile your source PDF stays open and within reach in Acrobat. Edits done in Express do not flow back into Acrobat on their own. Getting the revised file back means downloading it out of Adobe Express and uploading it into Acrobat. With the Edit or Generate image option, whatever you change in the PDF surfaces straight away in Acrobat.
