# Feature access and availability

Now everyone has access to the Adobe Acrobat and Adobe Express link-up, image editing features such as Edit, Generate, and Stylize included.

- Reachable through Adobe Acrobat Studio, Acrobat Pro, or Acrobat Standard subscriptions.
- On offer for Individuals, Teams, and Enterprise customers.
- Backed across ETLA and VIP plans.

Anyone lacking an Adobe Express subscription is granted 25 generative credits per month.
