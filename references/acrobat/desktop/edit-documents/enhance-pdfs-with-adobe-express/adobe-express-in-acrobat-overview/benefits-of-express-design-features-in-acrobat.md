# Benefits of Express design features in Acrobat

Here is what the Adobe Express features in Acrobat get you:

- Improved visuals: Lean on design tools so your PDFs come across as more professional and engaging.
- Efficient workflow: Style and revise PDFs right inside Acrobat, with no app-switching involved.
- Consistent design: Lean on templates and design elements to hold branding steady from one document to the next.
- Cloud access: Pick up Express-enhanced PDFs again and keep editing them in Adobe Express.
- Availability: Anyone on a qualifying subscription gets the image editing features.
