# About cover pages in Acrobat

Reach for the Generate cover page tool when you want a polished, AI-designed cover added to a PDF in moments.

Powered by generative AI from Adobe Express, the Generate cover page feature builds a cover that lines up with the document's content, brand colors, and page size on its own. The result lifts reports, proposals, project briefs, and academic work without much effort on your part.

Several AI-generated designs are offered, so you can drop a cover into the PDF quickly. Want finer control? Open the file in Adobe Express to adjust colors, layout, icons, and the rest of the visual elements, then keep editing the updated document across both Acrobat and Express.
