# Learn how to e-sign agreements using Adobe Acrobat.

Get an email asking you to sign a document, and Acrobat makes adding your signature simple. Your signature lives safely in Adobe cloud storage, which keeps it on hand across every device and app you use. Each time you open a document to sign, Acrobat pulls up your saved signature on its own, so the next round goes faster.

Tip

On mobile, Acrobat lets you photograph your signature and apply it when signing across desktop, web, and mobile, thanks to the signature sync feature.

Head to Home, and beneath Agreements pick Waiting for you.

Double-click whichever agreement you mean to sign.

Click the signature field in the agreement and fill in the information.

Signing for the first time? Build your signature by going with one of these:

- Type: Type your name in the field. You can choose from a selection of signature styles.
- Draw: Draw your signature in the field.
- Image: Browse and select an image of your signature.
- Mobile: Select this option to create your signature on a mobile device. Enter your mobile number and select Send. A link is sent to your mobile. Selecting the link opens the web browser on your mobile device, where you can draw or select an image of your signature.

Typing, drawing, or bringing in a signature image are all on the table. Whatever signatures and initials you add get saved for next time.

Select Apply.

Fill every required field and signature, then select Click to Sign.
