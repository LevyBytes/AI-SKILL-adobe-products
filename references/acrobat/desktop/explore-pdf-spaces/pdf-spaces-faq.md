# PDF Spaces FAQ

Find responses to the questions people ask most often about PDF Spaces, the AI-driven workspace recently added to Acrobat.

Built into Adobe Acrobat, PDF Spaces is an AI‑powered workspace that reshapes the way you handle and distribute your documents. By uniting files, context, audio overviews, and an AI Assistant in one place, it lets you grasp information more quickly and then pass it along as a tailored, guided experience that gets your point across.

Think of each PDF Space as a conversational knowledge hub: everything tied to a single task or topic sits together and can be arranged to lead others through it. With it you can:

- Keep as many as 100 files organized and viewable inside one space
- Lean on an AI Assistant—prebuilt or your own—to brainstorm, field questions, and surface what comes next
- Back up AI‑generated answers with citations that are quick to inspect
- Jot notes that hold onto insights, or stash an AI reply worth keeping
- Pass the whole workspace along as a guided experience that pairs files, summaries, audio, and an AI Assistant, pointing recipients to a starting place and the parts that count

For details on which files work and what the limits are, see the supported file formats and limitations in PDF Spaces.

The places where Acrobat PDF Spaces shows up are as follows:

- Open to anyone buying Acrobat Studio, or any plan that bundles in Acrobat AI features
- Open to anyone buying the AI Assistant add-on, which comes with access to Adobe online services plus Adobe cloud storage
- Reachable from Acrobat on desktop, web, and mobile
- Runs on Windows and macOS alike
- On offer in the supported languages across the globe
- Within reach of Individual, Teams, and Enterprise users whose subscription is Acrobat Studio, AI Assistant for Acrobat, or another plan carrying Acrobat AI features

Note

The option to charge generative credits for the Presentations feature may be added down the road, and usage limits are subject to change at any point.

Worldwide, PDF Spaces is presently offered in the supported languages, within the countries where Adobe sells its products and services. Look for additional languages and broader regional coverage in the near future.

The point of curated PDF Spaces is to let you see how PDF Spaces function while you dig into relevant, hand-selected material. Assembled by Adobe from licensed content supplied by trusted partners such as TED and Forrester, these ready-made PDF Spaces offer an easy path to fresh ideas and insights without starting a project of your own.

Reach for curated PDF Spaces when you want to:

- Read through skillfully prepared material
- Pass along thought-provoking ideas to your team
- Pull down documents you can refer back to easily
- Pick up worthwhile insights, get your creativity going, and deepen your learning

On Acrobat desktop, web, iOS, and Android, curated PDF Spaces are available. From time to time, fresh curated PDF Spaces or other updates may show up.

Once you build PDF Spaces, you can pass them on as a tailored, guided experience—pulling together files, contextual summaries, audio overviews, and an AI Assistant so your message lands clearly. A set of ready-made access levels and permissions governs how you share:

- Private: the space stays reachable only by the collaborators you invite
- Organization: everyone within your organization can get to it
- Public: anyone holding the link gets in with no sign‑in needed (steer clear of public links when the content is sensitive)

Out of the box, recipients reach all shared content—files and notes alike—and can dig into summaries, audio, or the AI Assistant to work through the material. Re‑sharing the PDF Space is also open to signed‑in users.

For more, see the steps to tailor the PDF Spaces experience and to follow how recipients engage with the PDF Spaces you've shared.

Indeed: by shaping both the material and the way it reaches recipients, you can pass information along as a tailored, guided experience.

Here are the ways to make the experience your own:

- Shifting around, revising, or inserting items on the Overview page so attention lands where you want
- Reworking the audio summary transcript so the message fits your aim
- Dropping in a logo to give it a branded feel
- Giving files clearer names and arranging them sensibly
- Tuning the AI Assistant so its replies to recipient questions follow your lead

Engagement is something you can follow as well—right down to total views and the moment recipients last opened the PDF Space—so you get a sense of how people are taking in your content.

The generative AI models behind Acrobat's features aren't trained on customer content by Adobe. See how Adobe safeguards your documents and content by means of responsible content usage and handling practices.

It is against the rules to create, upload, or share content that infringes someone else's copyright. Should you have any concern that your content might infringe a third party's copyrights, consult a lawyer.

From the foot of any AI-generated response, Acrobat Individual subscribers can weigh in directly by choosing Upvote or Downvote. You're then free to give one or more reasons behind your pick and tack on extra comments where appropriate.

Should something inappropriate turn up, choosing Report abuse at the asset level lets you flag whatever specific content strikes you as out of place or unsuitable.
