# Collaborating on PDF Spaces

See how PDF Spaces keeps teamwork running smoothly through shared files, well-defined permissions, and review-and-contribute tools baked right in.

Plenty of document work falls apart amid fragmented drafts, feedback strewn everywhere, and conversations that never connect. By pulling files, context, AI-powered insights, and activity into one shared workspace, PDF Spaces lets teams work with clarity and stay on the same page.

- Start with [How collaboration works](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/how-collaboration-works.md) and [Why collaborate](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/why-collaborate-on-pdf-spaces.md).
- Choose audience and participation through [Access and permission levels](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/access-and-permission-levels.md).
- Add people through [Inviting collaborators](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/inviting-collaborators.md).
- Follow comments and notifications in [Staying in sync](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/staying-in-sync.md).
- Revoke access or update roles through [Managing a shared PDF Space](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/managing-a-shared-pdf-space.md).
