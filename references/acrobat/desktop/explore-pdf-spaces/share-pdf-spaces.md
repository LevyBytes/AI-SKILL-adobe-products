# Share PDF Spaces

Share a workspace overview or an individual file. Recipients use the assigned access and permission settings; [collaboration roles](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/access-and-permission-levels.md) apply across the Space. Check [language availability](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/explore-pdf-spaces/supported-formats-limitations.html) before sharing.

Recipients can use the selected AI Assistant to explore the shared material.

## Share the overview

1. Open the PDF Space and customize its Overview if needed, including summaries, item order, notes, podcasts or branding.
2. Select Share from the top menu.
3. Choose the starting view: Overview, The complete file list or Spotlight a file. Select Done.
4. Set Who can access this PDF Space to Anyone with the link, your organization with the link, or Invited people only.
5. Under What can they do, allow content changes, comments only, or viewing files and notes.
6. Invite people by name or email and select Send; use Copy link for direct sharing, or select a third-party app and follow its prompts.

Public links support Viewer or Reviewer access; Contributor access requires restricted sharing. An enterprise Knowledge Base collection can be shared only within the organization or with invited people.

## Share an individual file

Open the PDF Space, select Files, open the file's options and choose Copy link to file. Send that link to the recipient.

To change roles, remove people, revoke sharing or delete the Space, follow [Managing a shared PDF Space](collaborating-on-pdf-spaces/managing-a-shared-pdf-space.md). Download needed files before deletion.

For browser sharing, follow [Share PDF Spaces on the web](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/web/explore-pdf-spaces/share-pdf-spaces.html). Recipients can [inspect citations](view-citations-in-responses.md) or use a [personalized AI Assistant](get-smarter-insights-with-personalized-ai-assistant.md) to explore the material.
