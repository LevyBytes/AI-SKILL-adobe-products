# View citations in responses

Get the hang of locating, interpreting, and confirming the citations that show up in AI responses.

The citations attached to AI responses link back to the source files behind the generated content. Inside PDF Spaces, the AI-generated responses carry clickable citations so you can trace and confirm what you are reading. That lets you double-check the main points and pass along references that are both clear and credible. Citations can help you:

- Locate insights in your added files
- Jump to the most relevant section in files
- Strengthen your work with trusted references

Open your PDF Space, then generate a summary or pose a question.

Look over the AI-generated response, which carries interactive citations beside each section.

A given result section might carry a single citation or several. Every citation comes with metadata—the document title, section, and page number among it—which keeps tracing straightforward.

Chatting with the AI Assistant surfaces the insights you're after, each backed by clickable citations. A - Section with one citation. B - Section with multiple citations.

Click a citation to bring up the source file.

Look over the file details in the citation references panel once it opens.

A multi-citation link brings up a list of references and drops you right at the cited section, with the file name and page called out.
