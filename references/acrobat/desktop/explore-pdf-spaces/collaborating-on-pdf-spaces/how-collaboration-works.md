# How collaboration works

Within PDF Spaces, collaboration unfolds in one shared environment where everyone on the team handles the same files, context, and insights—no tool-hopping, no losing the thread of a conversation. The starting point is creating a PDF Space that pulls your project's files, notes, and links together to set up a single source of truth.

With the PDF Space in place, you can pass along the whole workspace or just links to particular files or notes, whichever suits what recipients actually need to see. From there, roles let you govern how participants take part, and you can tailor the landing view with guidance and context. On private or organization‑only links, fresh users can ask to get into the PDF Space through Request Access, which the creator then approves or turns down.

Being the owner, you can pull several collaborators into the PDF Space and hand each one a Viewer, Reviewer, or Contributor role to match what they need. Team members get to look over content, drop comments, and put the built‑in AI Assistant to work summarizing documents, fielding questions, or pulling out insights, so everybody finds their footing fast.
