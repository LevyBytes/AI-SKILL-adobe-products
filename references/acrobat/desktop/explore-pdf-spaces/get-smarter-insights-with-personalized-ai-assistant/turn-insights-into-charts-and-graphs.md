# Turn insights into charts and graphs

Take what lives in your documents and let AI Assistant in PDF Spaces render it as clean visual charts and graphs. No more combing through tables or lengthy reports for what's changing or how things stack up against each other. Here is what's possible:

- Create charts and graphs from data mentioned across one or more documents
- Compare values, categories, or trends visually
- Summarize complex information into easy‑to‑understand visuals you can reference or share

Try these prompts

Type everyday language into the AI Assistant chat, for instance:

- “Create a bar chart comparing quarterly revenue mentioned in these documents.”
- “Generate a pie chart showing issue categories across the uploaded reports.”
- “Visualize year‑over‑year trends from this data.”
- “Create a line chart highlighting changes over time.”

Tip

When the output misses the mark, refine the chart through a chat follow-up. You might, for instance, request a different chart type or narrow it to a particular data range.
