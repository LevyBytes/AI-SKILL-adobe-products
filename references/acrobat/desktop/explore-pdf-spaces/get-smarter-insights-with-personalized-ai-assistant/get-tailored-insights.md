# Get tailored insights

From the Acrobat homepage, pick PDF Spaces, then open the PDF Space you want.

In the chat panel, choose Choose an AI Assistant and settle on one of these:

- AI Assistant: General assistance for broad tasks
- Analyst: Best suited for analyzing data or summarizing research
- Entertainer: Great for creative storytelling or engaging content
- Instructor: Ideal for explaining concepts or teaching
- Create your own: [Create a personalized AI Assistant](create-a-personalized-ai-assistant.md) for your unique needs

Have the AI Assistant produce insights or outputs built around your PDF Space. Phrase your questions in terms of your goals, problems, or tasks to pull back the most relevant and actionable results, such as:

- "Create a study guide with an answer key and suggested study schedule."
- "Draft an email summarizing the top 5 key takeaways and suggested next steps."
- "Create a table comparing sales results over the past six quarters."

Tip

Once the AI Assistant replies, sharpen your queries with follow-ups to probe further, surface anything missing, or redirect the focus toward more tailored insights.
