# Unlock AI-powered productivity with PDF Spaces

See what PDF Spaces in Adobe Acrobat can do to keep your documents tidy, surface insights, and make working alongside others easier.

Think of PDF Spaces as an AI-powered workspace inside Acrobat that gathers files, links, notes, and insights together in a single location. Rather than simply keeping documents in order, it pushes you toward genuinely understanding the material, reworking it, and passing it along with both clarity and context intact.

PDF Spaces also lets you hand off information as a tailored, guided walkthrough—pairing files, contextual summaries, audio overviews, and an AI Assistant so your point lands cleanly and recipients immediately grasp where to begin and which parts deserve attention.

Here is what PDF Spaces makes possible:

- Organize information: Bring together scattered files, links, and text into a single, organized knowledge hub.
- Get tailored insights: Use a prebuilt AI Assistant to extract key themes, summaries, and comparisons from your files, complete with citations. For tailored insights, create a personalized AI Assistant.
- Collaborate with others: Share your PDF Space so collaborators can view and modify the files and notes, and explore insights together using the selected AI Assistant.
