# Recipient experience

Bring up the email carrying several attachments. Up comes a prompt that nudges you to try out PDF Spaces and what it offers.

Select Try now. A short walkthrough of PDF Spaces greets you on a first visit; for anyone who has already used it, the attached documents become a PDF Space and the Overview page opens with its AI summaries and insights in view.

Look over the summary and insights, then contribute your feedback.
