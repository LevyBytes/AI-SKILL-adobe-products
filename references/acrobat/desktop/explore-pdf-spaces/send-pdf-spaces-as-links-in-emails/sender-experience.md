# Sender experience

In Outlook, select New mail to open a fresh message.

Attach a couple of documents or more. Up comes a prompt offering to send those attachments as a PDF Space.

Select Try now. From every attachment, Acrobat assembles a PDF Space, using the files as its source documents, and drops the link into the body of your email.

Note

Whatever subject the sender enters before selecting Try now becomes the name of the PDF Space.

Tack on any further notes or details you'd like the recipients to see.

Select Send. Off goes an email to the recipient carrying the PDF Space link.
