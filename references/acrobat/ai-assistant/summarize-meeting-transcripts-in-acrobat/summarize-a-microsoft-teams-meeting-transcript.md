# Summarize a Microsoft Teams meeting transcript

From the left panel on the Acrobat homepage, under Third-party apps, select Add meeting transcripts.

Under Microsoft Teams, select Sign in to Teams and enter the credentials for your account.

From the list of available transcripts, pick a meeting to open it in Acrobat.

For each meeting, the transcript list carries the name, organizer, date, time, and duration, and you can filter it by date range.

In the navigation panel, select Ask AI Assistant or Generative summary.

Go over the AI-generated overview, then select Copy to drop it into emails, notes, or documents.
