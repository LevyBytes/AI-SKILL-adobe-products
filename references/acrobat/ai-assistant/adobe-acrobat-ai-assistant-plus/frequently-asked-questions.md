# Frequently asked questions

## What does AI Assistant Plus include?

It combines Acrobat AI Assistant with Adobe Express Premium to turn document insights into finished content. See the [included capabilities and use cases](../adobe-acrobat-ai-assistant-plus.md).

## Is customer content used to train AI models?

Adobe does not use customer documents or data to train AI models. Adobe's responsible AI principles and enterprise security standards apply.

## Which platforms and languages are supported?

AI Assistant Plus works on web, desktop and mobile and is currently offered in English worldwide.
