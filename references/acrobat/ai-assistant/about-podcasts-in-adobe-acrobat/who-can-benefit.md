# Who can benefit

Document podcasts support hands-free review and accessible listening:

| Audience | Example use |
| --- | --- |
| Professionals | Review business reports, briefs, or manuals while commuting or doing other work. |
| Higher education students and researchers | Listen to condensed notes, research papers, and project documents. |
| Sales teams | Catch up on product changes, customer briefs, and sales information during travel. |
| Marketing teams | Review customer feedback, market research, and competitor information in audio form. |
| People needing accessible formats | Listen to books, guides, and learning materials. |
| Legal teams | Review audio summaries of case documents, laws, and regulatory developments. |

Check [access and availability](access-and-availability.md) before generating a podcast. See the [feature overview](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/use-acrobat-ai/podcasts/about-podcasts.html) for the complete product description.
