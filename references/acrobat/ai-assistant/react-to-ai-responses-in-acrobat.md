# React to AI responses in Acrobat

See how to respond to AI-generated replies in Acrobat so you can signal whether one is useful or needs another look.

While you're working with the AI Assistant or Generative Summary features in Acrobat, you can signal whether a reply lands. Depending on how you engage, you can like the ones that are accurate, flag the ones that aren't, or report material that needs moderation.

Reach for Like or Dislike to signal whether a reply helps or holds up. Reach for Report to flag anything that might be inappropriate or that breaks content guidelines.

- For accuracy and usefulness feedback, follow [Like or dislike a response](react-to-ai-responses-in-acrobat/like-or-dislike-a-response.md).
- To report inappropriate output, follow [Report inappropriate content](react-to-ai-responses-in-acrobat/report-inappropriate-content.md).
- For the overall feedback prompt, read [Rate your experience](react-to-ai-responses-in-acrobat/rate-your-experience.md).
