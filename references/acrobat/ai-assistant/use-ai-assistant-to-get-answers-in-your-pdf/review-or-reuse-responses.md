# Review or reuse responses

Click a source number to move to the part of the document it refers to.

Select Copy to put an answer on the clipboard.

Open the Options menu, then select Copy full summary to grab the whole summary.

Tip

To help make AI Assistant better, you can mark a response with a like or dislike, flag inappropriate content, and leave feedback. Follow [React to AI responses](../react-to-ai-responses-in-acrobat.md) for feedback steps.
