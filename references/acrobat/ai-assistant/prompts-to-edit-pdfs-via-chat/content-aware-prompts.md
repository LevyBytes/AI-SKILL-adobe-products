# Content-aware prompts

With a content-aware prompt, AI Assistant studies your document's content and structure and works out on its own which pages or elements to act on for the task.

For example, ask "Move the pages describing delivery milestones to the end of the PDF." Review the identified pages before accepting the change. If Acrobat selects the wrong content, retry with exact page numbers. Content-aware requests require an individual Pro or Studio plan; see [supported tools](../edit-pdfs-via-chat-overview/supported-tools.md).
