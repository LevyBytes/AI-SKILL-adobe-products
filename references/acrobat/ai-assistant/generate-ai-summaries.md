# Generate AI summaries

See how to pull an AI‑powered summary of a PDF in Acrobat.

Lean on AI‑powered summaries when you need to grasp a document's purpose, layout, and main points fast. A summary may carry section overviews, called-out insights, and links back to the source so review goes quicker.

Open the PDF you want to summarize.

From the right panel, select Generate Summary. This produces a document overview built from section headings and their summaries.

Within the summary, click any cross-reference to jump straight to that part of the PDF.

Select Share to share the summary.

Select Copy to copy the summary.

Select Ask AI Assistant to raise follow-up questions. Type your question and receive fast, AI‑powered answers.
