# Use AI Assistant to get answers in your PDF

See how Acrobat AI Assistant can produce answers from inside your PDF document.

Pose questions about what's in the PDF and get back replies that cite their sources. AI Assistant points to the spot in the document where it located the answer and offers questions you might ask next.

- For one document, follow [Ask questions about a PDF](use-ai-assistant-to-get-answers-in-your-pdf/ask-questions-about-a-pdf.md).
- To query several sources, follow [Ask questions across multiple PDFs](use-ai-assistant-to-get-answers-in-your-pdf/ask-questions-across-multiple-pdfs.md).
- To inspect citations or copy answers, follow [Review or reuse responses](use-ai-assistant-to-get-answers-in-your-pdf/review-or-reuse-responses.md).
