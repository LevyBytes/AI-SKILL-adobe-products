# Usage limitations

- Keep prompts or questions under 500 characters.
- Hold text selections in AI Assistant under 8000 characters. Select text within a single page; selections cannot span pages.
- Images and intricate vector graphics fall outside what AI Assistant handles.
- For webpages, AI Assistant shows up only on English-language pages that carry well-structured, readable long-form content. Even on pages that look long, the AI Assistant button might stay hidden.
