# Adobe Acrobat AI Assistant Plus

AI Assistant Plus combines document insights, shared workspaces and content creation.

| Included component | Capabilities |
| --- | --- |
| Acrobat AI Assistant | Ask across files; summarize; generate presentations or podcasts; cite source locations; work with PDFs, slides and spreadsheets. |
| PDF Spaces | Share source files and AI insights; add branding, summaries and audio; customize the assistant; centralize up to 100 files, links and notes. |
| Adobe Express Premium | Create presentations, social posts, flyers and videos; use brand kits, Firefly generation, Stock assets and translation. |

| Role | Example workflows |
| --- | --- |
| Marketing and communications | Campaign collateral, approved assets, localization and research presentations. |
| Human resources | Policy summaries, onboarding, internal communications and recruiting content. |
| Sales | Pitch decks, RFP summaries and shared proposals with supporting documents. |

| Security topic | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| Data privacy | Customer documents and data are not used to train AI models. |
| Firefly generation | Image/video generation uses licensed and public-domain training content. |
| Responsible AI | Adobe's responsible AI principles apply. |
