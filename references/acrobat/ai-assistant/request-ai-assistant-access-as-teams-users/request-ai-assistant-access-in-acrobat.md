# Request AI Assistant access in Acrobat

Open Adobe Acrobat and sign in to your account.

Should your Adobe account carry more than one profile, pick the right profile to sign in with before you send the request. Whichever profile you choose determines the organization and admins your request reaches.

Within the AI Assistant for Acrobat section, pick Get access.

Note

No option to request access to AI Assistant for Acrobat in view? Your admin may not have turned it on yet. Reach out to your admin to find out more.

Inside the Request access dialog box, give a business reason for wanting access to the AI Assistant for Acrobat.

Pick Send request.

Now it's a matter of waiting while your administrator looks over the request and approves it. A notification reaches you the moment access is granted.
