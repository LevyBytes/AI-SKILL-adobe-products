# Assigning AI Assistant via Creative Cloud

- AI Assistant is within reach for users who have Acrobat via Creative Cloud.
- For 0GB or China-specific SKUs, AI Assistant isn't offered.

For Studio license assignment in the Admin Console, follow [Assigning AI Assistant to Acrobat Studio](assigning-ai-assistant-to-acrobat-studio.md).

After signing in with an enterprise or federated ID, you have to open Acrobat Reader twice before the 7-day Free Trial of Acrobat prompt stops appearing.
