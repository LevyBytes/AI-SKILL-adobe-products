# Assigning AI Assistant to Acrobat and Acrobat Reader

- Credential sign-in for Acrobat Reader for enterprise is something admins can configure.
- Users sign in with their enterprise ID to access the assigned AI Assistant license.
- Add users to the Admin Console to enable sign-in capabilities.
- Holding an enterprise or federated ID, administrators can enable [automatic account creation](https://helpx.adobe.com/business/enterprise/directories-domains-access/directories-and-domains/automatic-account-creation-overview.html) from an existing directory.
- Following license assignment, an email reaches users carrying a link to open Acrobat Reader on the web and sign in with their enterprise, federated, or business ID.
- Signing in to the desktop build of Acrobat Reader is an option too. Follow [Reader installation](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/get-started/access-the-app/install-acrobat-reader.html).
- For users who aren't signed in, switch on the AI Assistant by updating the registry key where required.
