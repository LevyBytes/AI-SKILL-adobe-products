# Edit PDFs via chat overview

Find out more about editing PDFs through chat with Acrobat AI Assistant, including which tools and actions it covers, and where it's available.

Inside Acrobat, AI Assistant lets you handle PDF tasks just by describing, in everyday language, what you'd like done. It works out what you mean, picks whichever tool or tools fit, and runs them, sparing you from hunting through menus or tracking down a feature on your own. Among its main capabilities:

- Multi-tool workflows: one request alone can set several actions going, such as "Compress this file and share it with my team," or "Add a watermark on pages with pricing information."
- Content and structure awareness: because AI Assistant reads how your document is organized and what it says, it can act on it directly. Point it at a particular section, topic, or detail and you won't have to supply exact page numbers, for example, "move the pricing section before the case studies."
- Text editing: describe a replacement, rewrite, restructure, redaction, formatting or highlighting change. Review the matching text and preview before confirming.

Should an action be one that can't be undone, require extra detail, or simply call for a check that the right content or pages are in scope, Acrobat stops and checks with you first. Ask it to "Move the pages with the pricing information," for example, and it will pinpoint those pages and request your confirmation before touching anything. After a supported document change, use Undo on the top bar to reverse that change.

Note

For the moment, editing PDFs by chat neither draws on generative credits nor counts toward your capped number of AI Assistant requests.
