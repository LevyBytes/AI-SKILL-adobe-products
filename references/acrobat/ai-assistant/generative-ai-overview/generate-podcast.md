# Generate podcast

Take dense, motionless documents and turn them into lively podcast‑style audio rundowns. This lets you:

- Render documents as natural‑sounding audio that surfaces the main insights and themes.
- Make heavy content simpler to take in while you multitask, a fit for professionals with full schedules.
- Serve up chaptered, structured audio that tracks the document's flow and holds onto its meaning.
- Pass audio summaries to your team through PDF Spaces so they can listen wherever they are.

Sitting beside AI‑powered insights, the Generate podcast feature is woven into both Acrobat and PDF Spaces.

To create a podcast, follow the [desktop workflow](../create-podcasts-in-adobe-acrobat.md), [web workflow](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/web/use-acrobat-ai/podcasts/create-podcast.html) or [mobile workflow](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/mobile/acrobat-ai/create-podcasts.html).
