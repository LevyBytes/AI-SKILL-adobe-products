# Generate presentation

Turn documents or insights into refined, on‑brand decks in short order. This lets you:

- Spin reference documents or insights straight into a draft that's ready to present.
- Stay on brand in a single click by applying logos, fonts, and colors through brand kits.
- Sharpen and tailor slides with Adobe Stock assets, rewrite tools, animations, charts, and visuals.
- Work together as it happens through sharing, live co‑editing, contextual commenting, and presenter mode.
- Send output to PPT or PDF and speed things along with Acrobat AI Assistant in PDF Spaces driving the content analysis.

To build and refine a deck, follow [Generate presentations in Acrobat Studio](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/web/create-pdfs/create-presentations/generate-presentations.html).
