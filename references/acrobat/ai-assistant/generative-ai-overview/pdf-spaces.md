# PDF Spaces

Build shared knowledge hubs that pull files, links, AI-powered insights, and collaboration into one secure place. PDF Spaces lets you:

- Convert sprawling sets of documents into clear answers, each linked back to its source and driven by AI.
- Pose questions and surface insights spanning as many as 100 PDFs, Office files, notes, meeting transcripts, and web links, all in one workspace.
- Hand out secure, collaborative PDF Spaces and govern who gets in with view, comment, and add/remove permissions.

PDF Spaces rests on the trusted Adobe Acrobat platform, which brings along Responsible AI plus security at enterprise grade.

To create a PDF Space, follow the [desktop workflow](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/desktop/explore-pdf-spaces/create-pdf-spaces.html), [web workflow](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/web/explore-pdf-spaces/create-pdf-spaces.html) or [mobile workflow](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/mobile/pdf-spaces/create-pdf-spaces.html).
