# Key benefits of generative AI in Acrobat

Working with documents in Acrobat gets easier through AI, which deepens understanding and smooths out your workflows. Among the main payoffs:

- Better comprehension: AI supplies summaries and contextual insights that take the difficulty out of dense PDFs and scanned documents.
- Increased productivity: Move quickly across many documents and formats, including PDFs, Word files, and transcripts from Teams and Zoom, and shave time off the repetitive work.
- Stronger communication: Draw on AI-generated suggestions when you draft clear, relevant material such as meeting notes, emails, presentations, and other professional documents.
