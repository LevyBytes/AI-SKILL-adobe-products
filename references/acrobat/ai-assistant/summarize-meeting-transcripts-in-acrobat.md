# Summarize meeting transcripts in Acrobat

See how AI Assistant turns Teams and Zoom meeting transcripts into summaries and action items.

Put Acrobat's generative AI to work condensing meeting transcripts, querying what was discussed, and drafting content from the meeting details. Transcripts can come from Microsoft Teams or Zoom, or you can upload a transcript file straight into Acrobat.

Before you begin

Microsoft Teams: your admin must [configure Acrobat as the Teams PDF viewer and approve the integration endpoints](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/web/use-acrobat-extensions/acrobat-for-microsoft-teams/install-acrobat-teams.html). Transcript API access must also be enabled in Teams meeting settings; see [Enable Transcript API access](https://helpx.adobe.com/acrobat/web/use-acrobat-extensions/acrobat-for-microsoft-teams/ai-meetings-summaries.html). After access is granted, sign in to Teams from Acrobat.

Zoom: Log in to the Zoom web portal, grab the meeting transcript, and open it in Acrobat to tap the AI features.

- For account-connected transcripts, follow [Summarize a Microsoft Teams meeting transcript](summarize-meeting-transcripts-in-acrobat/summarize-a-microsoft-teams-meeting-transcript.md).
- For a downloaded transcript, follow [Summarize a Zoom meeting transcript](summarize-meeting-transcripts-in-acrobat/summarize-a-zoom-meeting-transcript.md).
