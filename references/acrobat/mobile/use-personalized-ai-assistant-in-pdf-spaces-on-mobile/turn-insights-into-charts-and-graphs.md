# Turn insights into charts and graphs

Take the information sitting in your documents and turn it into clear visual charts and graphs with AI Assistant in PDF Spaces. Rather than poring over tables or lengthy reports, you can rapidly picture trends, comparisons, and patterns. What you can do:

- Build charts and graphs out of the data cited across a single document or several
- Stack values, categories, or trends side by side in visual form
- Boil down complex information into clear visuals that are easy to grasp, reference, or share

Try these prompts

Speak plainly in the AI Assistant chat, such as:

- “Put together a bar chart that sets the quarterly revenue cited in these documents against each other.”
- “Build a pie chart that breaks out the issue categories found across the reports I uploaded.”
- “Picture the year‑over‑year trends drawn from this data.”
- “Draw a line chart that calls out how things shift over time.”

Tip

When the result misses the mark, follow up in chat to refine the chart. You might, for instance, ask the assistant to switch the chart type or zero in on a particular data range.
