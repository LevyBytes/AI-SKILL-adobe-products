# Workspace basics - Manipulate panel groups

Use the highlighted drop zone inside the group to add the panel by its tab.

Adding a panel to a panel group

Adding a panel to a panel group

To reorder panels within a group, drag a panel's tab to another position in the group.

To make a panel float independently from the group, drag it by the tab outside the group.

To move an entire group, drag the title bar, the area above the tabs.
