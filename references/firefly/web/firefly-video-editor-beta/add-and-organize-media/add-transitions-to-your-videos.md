# Add transitions to your videos

Learn how to apply transitions such as Dissolve and Fade to visual timeline items for smoother scene changes.

Transitions blend scenes or add dramatic pauses, helping videos look smoother and more professional. Firefly video editor (beta) provides simple transitions that can be applied directly to clips already on the timeline.

These effects work best after media clips are arranged in the timeline and you are ready to improve how scenes connect. Prerequisites for applying transitions:

- Adobe Firefly account that can use Firefly video editor (beta)
- Open project in Firefly video editor (beta)
- Video clips already placed on your timeline
