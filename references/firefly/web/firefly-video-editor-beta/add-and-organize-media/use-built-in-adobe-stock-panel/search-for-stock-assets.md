# Search for Stock assets

Open the Adobe Stock panel from one of these places:

- On the Get started page, select Import media > Adobe Stock.
- Use the hamburger menu > Import > Adobe Stock.
- From the Your media or Timeline panel, choose Select files > Adobe Stock or Add > Adobe Stock.

Enter a keyword for the asset in the search bar, then pick Videos, Images, or Audio to set the kind of asset to return.

After the panel has been used for a search, a Recent Searches tab appears beside the avatar icon.

The panel also adds a Recent Searches tab next to the avatar icon after you run a search.

For additional technical filtering, choose the Filters icon.

- Exclude GenAI content: turns off AI-generated assets in the results.
- Only free content: removes paid assets from the result set.
- Resolution: filters video quality with All or Available in 4K.
- Duration: sets the shortest and longest clip lengths with the slider.
- Orientation: limits matches by project shape with Horizontal, Vertical, Panoramic, or Square.
- Frame Rate: narrows results to a frame rate such as 24, 30, or 60 fps that fits the timeline properties.

- Exclude GenAI content: removes AI-generated images from the image results.
- Only free content: hides paid items from the result set.
- Subcategory: filters by Photos, Illustrations, or Vectors for the asset category.
- People: uses All, Include people, or Exclude people according to whether people should appear.
- Orientation: limits the image shape to Horizontal, Vertical, Panoramic, or Square.
- Local artists only: refines contributor location with All or Local artists only.
- Background: helps compositing by returning Transparent or Isolated assets.
- Color: matches the visual style by choosing a dominant color.

- Exclude GenAI content: omits AI-generated audio from the results.
- Type: filters audio with All, Music, or Sound effects.
- Duration: defines the audio track length with the slider.
- Tempo: sets a BPM range that fits the pacing of the edit.
- Style: uses moods or genres to narrow results by tone and category.

For audio filters, multiple Moods and Genres can be selected together for tighter matching.

Selecting more than one Mood and Genre at the same time gives audio searches more precise results.

Choose Search to apply the filters, then review the matching assets and select the one you want. Results appear under the search bar as preview thumbnails. To view more information or preview an item, hover over a result.

Tip

- When downloading vector assets to the video editor, choose PNG or JPEG.
- Let video previews load completely before expecting the watermark to disappear.

In the results or collections, find the asset you want and choose the action that matches your Stock plan.

- Users with Stock plans: for licensed assets, choose the download icon; for unlicensed assets, choose the Download button. Enterprise accounts download immediately, while Individual plans show the credits that will be used.
- Users without Stock plans: use the Download button for free assets and Free trial for paid assets. Paid assets are marked with a crown icon; selecting one asks you to sign up for a plan.

Run another search when you need to find and download several assets in one session.

To collect more than one asset at the same time, search again and download the additional results.

On a free plan, choosing the Download button for a paid asset starts Stock plan signup.

Note

Available Stock credits can be checked from the account menu inside the Stock panel.

Downloaded assets are placed in the timeline or in the Your media panel based on the location used to open the Stock panel:

- Get started page: opens a new project and places the assets directly on the timeline.
- Timeline: places the assets on the timeline and also in the Your media panel.
- Your media panel: downloads the assets into the Your media panel so they can be added to the timeline later.
- Hamburger menu: places the assets directly on the timeline and in the Your media panel.

The Stock panel also supports browsing curated collections, so discovery does not require a search. Select a collection or category to inspect its assets, then use filters to tighten the result set.
