# Use built-in Adobe Stock panel

Learn how to search, filter, and download stock videos, images, and audio from the built-in Adobe Stock panel.

In Firefly video editor, the Adobe Stock panel speeds up the workflow by giving direct access to millions of stock assets without leaving the editing environment.

The Adobe Stock panel can supply videos, images, or audio tracks for projects. Search filters narrow results by technical details such as resolution, duration, and format. Downloaded assets appear automatically in the Your media panel, or on the timeline, based on where and how the Stock panel was opened.
