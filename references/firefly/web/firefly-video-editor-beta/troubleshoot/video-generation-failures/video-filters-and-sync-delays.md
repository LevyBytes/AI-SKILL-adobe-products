# Generated videos are hidden due to filters or delayed synchronization

When expected media is missing from the Generation history panel, use this sequence:

Go to the Generation history panel.

Change the filter to All or Video.

Confirm that Favorites is not selected.

Sort by Newest first, then scroll to the top.

If a placeholder thumbnail that says "Still working..." or "Generating..." remains in the Generation history panel after several seconds, refresh the page.

Check the Generation history page to verify that the video exists.

Go back to the editor and refresh so results sync.
