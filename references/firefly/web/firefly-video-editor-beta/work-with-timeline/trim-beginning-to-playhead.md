# Trim beginning to playhead

Cut everything from the start of a clip through the playhead position on the timeline.

In Firefly video editor, Trim beginning to playhead deletes the portion from the start of the selected clip up to the playhead position on the timeline. After the trim, the clip begins exactly at the playhead, which makes unwanted footage at the beginning easy to remove without manual in-point adjustment.

Preparation:

Create a new project, then put media files on the timeline.

Select the video, image, or text layer in the timeline that you want to edit.

Scrub through the timeline clip, find the exact frame where the video should begin, and place the playhead there.

From the top-left menu, select Clip, then choose Trim beginning to playhead.

Trim beginning to playhead removes frames from the start of the selected layer through the playhead position on the timeline.

Use Trim beginning to playhead to remove the selected layer's starting frames up to the playhead position on the timeline. Tip

Press Q to instantly trim the selected clip from its beginning to the playhead position on the timeline.

All content from the start of the selected layer through the playhead is removed, causing the layer to begin exactly at the playhead position.
