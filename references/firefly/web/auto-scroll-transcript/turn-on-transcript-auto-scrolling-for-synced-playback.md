# Turn on transcript auto-scrolling for synced playback

With Text-based editing, Auto-scrolling keeps the transcript moving with the spoken words while the video previews. That lets you follow the matching line without manual scrolling and makes the current spoken passage easier to find, replay, or review.

Before you begin:

Place a video file with dialogue on the timeline in a new project.

In the right panel, select the Text-based editing icon.

The Text-based editing panel shows the transcript that was generated automatically for the video.

Use the icon and enable the Auto-scrolling option.

Auto-scrolling keeps the transcript aligned with the spoken words during playback.

Auto-scrolling lets the transcript follow the words being spoken in the video.

After it is enabled, playback or playhead movement causes the transcript to scroll to the matching text.
