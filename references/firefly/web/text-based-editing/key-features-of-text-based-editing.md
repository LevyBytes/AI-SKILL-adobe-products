# Key features of Text-based editing

- Auto-transcribe your footage: Generate a transcript automatically and align spoken dialogue to timecodes, so transcript text can be searched, navigated, and selected as clip ranges.

- Edit Using Text: Build rough cuts by choosing transcript text and moving it into the desired order. Matching video sections are trimmed automatically and added to the timeline as the text changes.

- Fine-tune with precision: After the rough cut lands in the timeline, continue refining it by trimming clips or changing their order against the spoken words.

- Clean up dialogue easily: Find filler words such as "uh" and "umm" quickly and remove them in bulk. You can also filter by speaker and delete that speaker's dialogue.
