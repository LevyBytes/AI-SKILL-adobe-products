# Firefly AI Assistant FAQ

Find answers to common questions about using Firefly AI Assistant (beta).

Firefly AI Assistant changes the creation process: many AI tools only respond, while Firefly AI Assistant coordinates and carries out work.

- Firefly AI Assistant understands what you want to create, selects appropriate tools from Adobe creative apps, and runs complex multi-step workflows for you inside one unified conversational interface.
- Firefly AI Assistant lets you express the desired outcome in your own words while it runs complex, multi-step workflows. It introduces a creation model where you begin with the vision, explain what you want, and shape the work as the assistant helps build it.
- The assistant can begin with image generation, continue into video editing, and prepare social-channel assets without requiring app switching or step-by-step direction. You keep attention on the creative vision while the agent performs the work in the background.

Firefly AI Assistant uses Adobe creative suite capabilities from Photoshop, Lightroom, Illustrator, Premiere, Firefly, and more.

Additional tools and capabilities are planned.

Availability includes:

- People subscribed to Creative Cloud Pro, Firefly Pro, Firefly Pro Plus, or Firefly Premium.
- Adults who are 18 or older.
- Inputs written in English.

Optimization is limited for:

- Non-English usage.
- Enterprise All apps subscribers (CCT/E).
- EDU higher-ed use.

Unavailable for:

- Adobe desktop apps, Firefly mobile web, and the mobile app.
- Certain legacy enterprise plans, including CCT Standard AA (no SA), plus CCE v3 AA/SA, CCE Core AA/SA, and CCE Pro AA/SA.
- K-12 (EDU).

Firefly AI Assistant relies on tools and AI models from across the creative ecosystem, including the Firefly family of models and partner models, to produce the best creative result it can.

Unless you name a model explicitly, AI Assistant automatically chooses between Firefly and partner models according to the request. Outputs made with Firefly models in AI Assistant are treated as safe for commercial use. For outputs generated from partner models in AI Assistant, you are responsible for deciding whether the partner model fits your project. Details about partner models are available on the partner models page.

Firefly AI Assistant stores only the preferences you explicitly tell it to remember. Within a session, those saved preferences may be used to make responses relevant or to complete tasks, but the assistant does not keep extra information beyond what you chose to save. At any time, you can ask the assistant to remove every saved preference. For details, see creating, managing, and viewing your preferences.

Chat history appears in the left panel. To delete or rename a chat, go to the left panel and select the three-dots icon for that chat.

A Beta Version is not the final Services and Software, and bugs in it may cause system problems, other failures, or data loss. Adobe may decide not to make a commercial version of the Beta Version available. For beta details, review the terms of use for Beta Versions.

Planned Firefly AI Assistant creative skills include "Portrait Retouch," "Prepare Product Photos," "Create Social Variations," and more.

In addition to creative skills that behave like prompt templates, the assistant will be able to use powerful and precise creative suite tools such as Blur background (portrait), the Blur tool, Auto tone, Auto straighten, Vectorize, Generate image, Transcode video, and Generative expand, among others.

Firefly AI Assistant is designed for conversation, so course correction works like giving direction during collaboration. When the output is not quite right, you can explain what feels off, what should change, or where you want the work to go next, and the agent adjusts. Because it understands creative intent and professional standards, it can refine the work while staying aligned with your vision and brand.

You do not need to restart or move to another tool. Continue the conversation until the work reaches the exact result you want. You can also use thumbs-up or thumbs-down, together with the feedback option, to tell us what worked or did not work.

Regardless of which generative AI models may be offered in our creative apps, user content is not used, and will not be used, to train generative AI models.
