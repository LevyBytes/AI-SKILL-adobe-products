# Access Adobe Firefly

Firefly supports professional Creative AI work from concepting through production. Open the mobile or browser experience to produce images plus video and audio with advanced creative controls.

Prerequisites

Create an Adobe account.

Use Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari to access Firefly.

Sign in using your email address or credentials from your Google, Facebook, Apple, Microsoft, or Line account.
