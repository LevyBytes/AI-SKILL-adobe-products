# Using Firefly

For features that do not carry the beta label, Firefly-generated outputs may be used in commercial projects. For beta features, Firefly-generated outputs may also be used commercially unless the product explicitly says otherwise.

Results vary with the prompt, because prompts strongly guide the AI's behavior and affect response quality and relevance. Write descriptive prompts to produce striking, vivid images and video; if the output is not what you want, revise the prompt to move closer to your goal.

When your plan includes Firefly entitlement, it cannot be disabled, but you can simply avoid Firefly-powered workflows in your apps or choose not to sign in to the Firefly website.

Submitting work to the Firefly community means you allow Adobe to use the generated image and prompt in marketing materials and on inspiration and gallery pages; attribution may include your Adobe profile photo and name. This also lets others create from the same prompt. Adobe's design team reviews and curates contributions. Do not submit community content from Business profiles, including end users of Enterprise, Teams, or Education accounts.
