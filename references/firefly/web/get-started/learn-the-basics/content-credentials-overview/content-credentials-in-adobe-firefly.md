# Content Credentials in Adobe Firefly

Content Credentials are tamper-evident, industry-standard metadata that supplies context about a file's creation or edits and the parties involved. The metadata can carry many kinds of details; in Adobe Firefly, its role is to make generative AI use more transparent.

To support transparency for generative AI, Adobe automatically adds Content Credentials to assets whose pixels are 100% generated with Adobe Firefly, including Text to Image.
