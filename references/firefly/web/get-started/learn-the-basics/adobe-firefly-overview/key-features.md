Because AI content generation consumes substantial compute, plans now include a monthly generative credits allowance.

- Your Creative Cloud, Adobe Express, Adobe Firefly, and Adobe Stock paid plan provides a plan-specific monthly allotment of generative credits for generative AI features on the Firefly website and inside eligible Adobe apps. Review the credit amount assigned to each plan.
- Free Creative Cloud users also get a fixed monthly quantity of generative AI creations. After that monthly limit is used, you can buy a paid plan to keep making Firefly-powered assets or wait for the next monthly reset.

Note

Generative credits work only in apps included in your entitlement.
