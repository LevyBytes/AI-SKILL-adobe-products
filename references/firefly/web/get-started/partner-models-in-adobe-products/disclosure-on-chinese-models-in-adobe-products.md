# Disclosure on Chinese models in Adobe products

Firefly is being expanded with additional partner models so creators have more choice and flexibility. Adobe focuses on offering high-performing models, using independent third-party benchmarks together with Adobe evaluations shaped by community feedback. Select Chinese models are part of that set.

When one of these models is used for generation, your prompts and reference files are sent to the Chinese company that created the model.

Included models:

- Kling 3.0
- Kling 3.0 Omni

Partner generative AI models and Adobe AI models do not train on your content. Adobe also uses contractual protections and technical safeguards to help protect privacy and make the transmitted data comply with laws, including privacy regulations such as GPDR and the US DOJ Bulk Transfer Rule.
