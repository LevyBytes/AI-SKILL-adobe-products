# Access your generation history in other Adobe apps

Use Firefly generations inside other Adobe products, including Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign.

Firefly generation history connects with other Adobe apps, so generative content can be reused across Adobe products without interrupting the creative workflow. Through Adobe cloud storage, app-compatible generations are available in Adobe Photoshop (web and desktop), Adobe Illustrator, and Adobe InDesign.

An active Creative Cloud subscription is required, with access to Firefly and to the destination apps or web apps. Depending on the app and workflow, there are two ways to reach Firefly generations.
