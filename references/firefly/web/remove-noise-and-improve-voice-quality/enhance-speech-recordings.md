# Use Enhance Speech to turn recordings into studio-quality audio.

Enhance Speech improves recordings by reducing background noise, balancing volume, and increasing clarity. The processed audio sounds cleaner and sharper, similar to a recording made in a studio or professional booth.

Note

Premium users can use bulk upload and video upload. See the Enhance Speech plan details for more information.

On the Firefly homepage, choose Generate in the left panel; on the Audio tab, select Enhance Speech.

On Enhance Speech, upload files by dragging and dropping video or audio files, or by selecting Choose files.

Begin enhancing speech by dropping audio or video files, or by uploading them from your device.

Begin enhancing speech by dropping audio or video files, or by uploading them from your device. Tip

Learn about supported file formats and the maximum file sizes and durations for audio and video uploads here.

When several files have been uploaded, choose the video or audio from the list on the left.

Select one uploaded file to start enhancing its speech.

Use the sliders below the preview area to adjust speech and background levels separately:

- Speech: changes how much enhancement is applied to isolated speech so it becomes clearer and more prominent. Moving the slider left reduces speech enhancement and makes speech less prominent; moving it right increases enhancement and makes speech clearer and more distinct.
- Background: changes the loudness of isolated background noise, making it quieter or louder as needed. Moving the slider left lowers background noise volume, while moving it right raises it.

Use the Speech and Background sliders to balance speech enhancement with noise reduction.

Switch between the original and enhanced audio to compare the before and after results.

After the speech enhancement and noise reduction balance is right, select Download to save the processed file.
