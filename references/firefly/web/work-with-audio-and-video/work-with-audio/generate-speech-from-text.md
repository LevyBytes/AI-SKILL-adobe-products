# Generate speech from text

Use text prompts in Firefly to generate audio clips with different voices, tones, and accents.

Generate Speech (beta) creates natural-sounding audio clips and voiceovers. Controls such as accent, language, speed, and pitch let you tailor voice characteristics to your needs.

On the Firefly homepage, select Generate in the left panel, then choose Generate speech.

On Generate speech, paste the text to turn into speech, or choose Import text from a file and upload a DOCX or TXT file.

After the text is added, open the Speech settings panel on the left and use Model to choose Firefly Speech.

Tip

A partner model, such as ElevenLabs Multilingual v2, can also generate speech from text.

Use the Voice dropdown menu to choose a voice. Under Voice, adjust accent/language, pitch, and speed to give the voice a distinct style.

Use the Choose a language dropdown to select a language and delivery accent from the language list, such as English (US) and English (India).

Adjust accent, speed, and pitch to customize the selected voice. To change Speed and Pitch for generated speech, adjust these speech settings:

- Speed: drag the speed bar right to make spoken audio faster, or left to make it slower.
- Pitch: drag the pitch bar right to raise the spoken audio pitch, or left to lower it.

Tip

- At the bottom of the left panel, select the icon to play a sample of the voice with the selected speed, pitch, and accent.
- Select the icon to add the voice to favorites.

In the main text editor window, more changes can be made to the entered text:

- Play: previews selected text in the uploaded content before generation.

Use Play to quickly hear how the text sounds with the selected voice settings.

- Fix Pronunciation: corrects pronunciation and adds guidance for how specific words should sound.
- Find & Replace: selects words and replaces them.
- Add Tone: adds tonality and defines intonation for the generated speech.

To add more script text, insert a pause, or open writing tips, use the Generate bar and choose from these selections:

- Add Text: imports a TXT or DOCX file to add more text to the uploaded content.
- Add Pause: adds pauses so the audio track sounds more natural.
- Show Tips: opens writing tips for producing the best possible results.
