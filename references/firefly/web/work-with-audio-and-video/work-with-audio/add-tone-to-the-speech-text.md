# Add tone to the speech text

Add emotion and expression to selected uploaded text by applying tone to generated speech.

Generate Speech (beta) lets selected words or phrases carry a specified tone, helping the generated speech express the intended emotion and style while fitting its context.

Before you begin:

Add speech text or upload a text file, then set the voice, accent, and other options.

In the text editor, highlight the text and select the Add Tone icon.

Apply a tone to the text for greater impact, such as excitement to energize the reader or drama to increase engagement.

From the panel that appears, choose one of these categories.

- Neutral
- Positive
- Negative
- Expressive

Tone provides quick access to Neutral, Positive, Negative, and Expressive styles for refining speech generation tonality.

After tone is applied, the selected text appears underlined with a colored label before it.

- Grey indicates neutral tone
- Green indicates positive tone
- Red indicates negative tone
- Blue indicates expressive tone

The Text panel shows multiple tone labels in separate colors, making them easier to identify and adjust.

To change an applied tone, select the tone label and choose another available option.

Select Generate.

Preview the generated speech, then use Download to save it locally.

Before downloading, review the generated speech by playing it or scrubbing through it.
