# Validate an asset

In the Adobe Brand Intelligence web app, choose the Validate card.

Note

The reference web app provides a ready-to-use interface that shows on-brand validation in practice. With it, you can upload assets, check them against your brand guidelines, and inspect results in a straightforward environment.

The app is fully functional, but its main purpose is to serve as a reference implementation that illustrates how the product can be added to your own tools, workflows, or platforms.

Choose Browse files to upload, or use drag and drop for the files that need validation.

In the Validate context panel, choose the relevant options for Campaign, Channel, Product, and Audience.

Add assets and set validation context, including campaign, channel, and product.

Add assets and set validation context, including campaign, channel, and product. Note

After you choose the Campaign, the available choices in the Validate context panels are configured. Your dedicated Forward Deployed Engineering team in the Brand Control Center populates the other options according to your organization's requirements and standards.

At the bottom of the left panel, choose Validate. Let the validation check finish, then review the feedback in the Validation results panel.

In the Validation results panel, examine summary details such as total files checked and issues found.

Choose individual assets to inspect flagged issues. Then choose the issue number to see inline suggestions and the reason each item was flagged.

Examine issues in detail through visual pins and guided suggestions connected to brand guidelines.

Examine issues in detail through visual pins and guided suggestions connected to brand guidelines. Tip

For each issue, you can choose the links to view evidence from the brand guidelines.

Choose the more icon to either Reject the suggestion or Edit it so you can update your asset based on the suggestion.

When a suggestion is relevant, choose the approve icon so Adobe Brand Intelligence can learn from that feedback.

Each approval, edit, or rejection of a suggestion is fed back into the Adobe Brand Intelligence for continuous learning. This lets the Validate skill automatically flag issues, helping enterprises keep content on brand and compliant.
