# Understand Text to image effects for richer, more varied visual styles

In Text to image, Effects provides options that increase the visual appeal of generated images and help create varied sets of image variations. Once effects are chosen, Firefly adjusts elements such as colors, lighting, and camera angles to fit the selected style. You can also apply distinctive looks by exploring art movements and other styles.

Effects in Firefly are grouped into these categories:

- Popular
- Techniques
- Movements
- Effects
- Concepts
- Themes
- Materials

Within Effects, Color and tone, Lighting, and Camera angle can also be specified.

Combine effects to create visually appealing images that communicate your vision.
