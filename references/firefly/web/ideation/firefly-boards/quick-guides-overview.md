# Quick Guides overview

Quick Guides in Firefly Boards show how ready-made workflows can take a creative idea through to a finished output.

Starting an AI-based creative project can feel difficult. Quick Guides in Firefly Boards reduce that complexity by giving you structured, easy-to-follow direction. They help you produce specific creative effects without relying on guesswork or trial and error.

A Quick Guide is a predefined workflow that already lays out the steps, inputs, and intended results. Instead of opening a blank canvas and working out how to combine Firefly Boards AI capabilities yourself, you can begin from prepared assets and use them to build toward your creative vision.
