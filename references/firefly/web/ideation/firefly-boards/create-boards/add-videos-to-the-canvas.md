# Add videos to the canvas

Bring a video onto the canvas through the Import option.

To create a video with Adobe models or partner models, choose Generate video at the bottom of the page.

Pick a video generation model with the model selection option.

Enter the video description as text.

You can also choose the Sample from canvas icon to access generation options and settings.

- - Availability depends on the selected video model.

The generated video shows up as a filmstrip thumbnail. Drag it from there and drop it on the canvas.

After a video has been added to the canvas, selecting it reveals editing and adjustment controls.

Choose two or more videos on the canvas when you need arrange, align, or other editing actions.
