# Y2K by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use Y2K to restyle images with a bold 2000s retro aesthetic and playful, nostalgic fashion energy.

Upload an image you want transformed with a bold Y2K aesthetic.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Y2K from the Image tab.

Select Subject to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the image from the canvas.

Choose a canvas image for the Y2K with Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Y2K by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
