# Product photo by FLUX Kontext Max

Use the Product photo preset to place a product in another environment, such as a studio, a busy cafe, or a calm outdoor setting.

Upload an image of a product or object to the canvas.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Product photo.

If you already know the scene where the object should appear, describe that scene in text.

Select the Image field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
