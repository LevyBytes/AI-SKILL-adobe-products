# Polished by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use the Polished preset when you need a cleaner, more finished image treatment with a simple professional look.

Upload the image that should receive the polished treatment.

At the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Polished.

Select the Subject field to turn on the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
