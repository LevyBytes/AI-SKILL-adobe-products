# Jean dreams by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use Jean dreams to give a subject a denim-centered treatment. For fashion, lifestyle, or other creative concepts, the option creates a relaxed, stylish look built around denim.

Add an image of the subject that should receive the denim look.

At the bottom of the page, choose Presets, then choose Jean dreams from the Image tab.

Select Subject to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the image from the canvas.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Jean dreams by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
