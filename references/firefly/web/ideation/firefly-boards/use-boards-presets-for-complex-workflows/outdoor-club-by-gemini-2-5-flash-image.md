# Outdoor club by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use the Outdoor club preset to give portraits an athletic outdoor mood with a crisp, active visual style.

Upload an image of the subject that should receive the outdoor element and background.

At the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Outdoor club.

Select the Subject field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
