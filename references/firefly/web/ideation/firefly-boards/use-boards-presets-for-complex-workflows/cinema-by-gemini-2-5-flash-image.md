# Cinema by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

Use Cinema to turn a portrait into a warm, gold-toned image with nostalgic movie styling and an urban dramatic feel.

Upload an image with a subject onto the canvas.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Cinema.

Select the Subject field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
