# Ink wash by Firefly Image 4

Use the Ink wash preset to reinterpret an image with the softer artistic look of ink wash painting.

Upload the image that you want to convert into an ink wash painting style.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Ink wash.

Select the Image field to enable the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
