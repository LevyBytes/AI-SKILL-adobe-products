# Character and product by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image

When a product image and a character should appear together in one compelling scene, the Character and product preset can combine them. It blends details from the character and the product so they can interact in a realistic or imaginative composition.

Upload or add the character and product images you want to use.

At the bottom of the Boards page, choose Presets, then choose Character and product from the Image tab.

Select Character to activate the eyedropper tool, then choose the canvas image containing the character.

Select Product, then choose the canvas image containing the product.

Choose canvas images for the Character and product by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.

Choose Generate.

Image generated with the Character and product by Gemini 2.5 Flash Image preset.
