# Impasto painting by Firefly Image 4

Use the Impasto painting preset to remake images as expressive, textured artwork with thick brushwork and layered paint.

Upload the image that you want to turn into an impasto painting.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Impasto painting.

Select the Image field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
