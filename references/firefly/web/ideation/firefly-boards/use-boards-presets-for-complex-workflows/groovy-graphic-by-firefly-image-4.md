# Groovy graphic by Firefly Image 4

Use the Groovy graphic preset to give an image a retro graphic treatment with bold shapes and bright color.

Upload the image that you want to transform into a groovy retro graphic style.

From the bottom of the page, select Presets, then choose Groovy graphic.

Select the Image field to activate the eyedropper tool, and choose the image on the canvas.
