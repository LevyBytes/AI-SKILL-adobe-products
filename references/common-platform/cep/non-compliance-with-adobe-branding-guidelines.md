# Top 3 Rejection Reasons - _Non-compliance with Adobe Branding Guidelines._

Developers sometimes add Adobe brand assets before receiving permission, such as Adobe's "A" icon. That conflicts with the [Adobe Branding Guidelines](https://partners.adobe.com/content/dam/tep_assets/public/public_1/documents/Adobe-Creative-Cloud-Developer-Brand-Guide.pdf). Request approval from the Adobe Branding Team at <brand@adobe.com> before using Adobe assets. After permission is granted, those Adobe assets may be used on the website and in screenshots.
