# Two-finger scrub

With a two-finger gesture similar to scrolling, scrub backward and forward through clips and sequences in Project panel Icon View and in the Program Monitor panels, much like hover scrubbing. The gesture moves the clip or sequence playhead in a 1:1 relationship to the thumbnail or monitor width, for durations up to 30 minutes. For each added 30-minute duration, perform another scrub gesture; after each gesture ends, the playhead stays at its stopping position.

To perform a two-finger scrub on a clip in the Project panel, tap the clip first to select it. The thumbnail controls are displayed.

Swipe two fingers horizontally across the thumbnail in either direction to scrub through a clip.
