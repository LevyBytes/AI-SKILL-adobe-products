# Crossfade between audio clips

Use visual fade handles in Adobe Premiere to apply and customize crossfade transitions between neighboring audio clips.

Before you begin:

Start with a sequence, then place an audio clip on the timeline.

Expand the audio tracks for the planned crossfade vertically.

Verify that both clips touch each other and have been trimmed.

On the audio clip, drag its visual fade handle across the neighboring clip to make a custom crossfade.

Build crossfades between clips by dragging the interactive fade handles on clip edges.

Dragging the interactive fade handles on clip edges also builds crossfades between clips. Tip

Hold the Shift key to adjust one side of the crossfade effect. To change the curve or duration, hold the Option key and drag left or right.
