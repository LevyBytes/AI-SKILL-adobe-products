# Invite groups to collaborate

Enterprises can invite synced groups created with Adobe Admin console Active Directory. In the invitations field, type the group name. When a group is selected for invitation, every member of that group receives an invitation to collaborate.

Group invitations do not update dynamically. If membership changes after the invitation is sent, new members do not receive an invitation automatically, and former members are not removed. Members can be added or removed individually from the Team Projects.

Note

Group size is limited. A group with 150 or more members cannot be invited.
