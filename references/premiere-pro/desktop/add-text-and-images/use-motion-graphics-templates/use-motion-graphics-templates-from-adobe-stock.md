# Use Motion Graphics templates from Adobe Stock

Search Adobe Stock in the Graphics Templates panel, then customize the graphic you need in Adobe Premiere without changing workspaces.

Adobe Stock provides thousands of professionally designed titles, lower thirds, transitions, and graphics that can be used in Premiere projects.

Find templates in the Graphics Templates panel and drag them directly into the project when you are ready to customize them. This avoids building graphics and animations from scratch while still helping you create polished videos more quickly.

In the Graphics Templates panel, select Adobe Stock.

Enter the search term and press Enter. Premiere then shows matching Adobe Stock results.

License a Motion Graphics template by dragging it to the sequence, or use the Buy License and Download icon.

Tip

Select the Info icon below a Motion Graphics template thumbnail when you want more details or an animation preview.
