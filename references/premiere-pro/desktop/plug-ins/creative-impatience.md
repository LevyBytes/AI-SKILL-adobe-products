# Creative Impatience

Feathered Crop

This plug-in supports Adobe Premiere when running on Windows 7 64-bit and on macOS X 10.6.x or 10.7.x. It crops an image on the inside or outside and adds feathering to the cropped edges. A Graphic UI is included, with support for 8-, 16-bit and 32-bit depth. In Adobe Premiere, it operates in RGB and YUV color spaces.

Vignette

Vignette creates a vignette on the source clip. Rectangular and elliptical vignettes are both supported. Users can reposition and rotate vignettes, apply any available color and blending mode, and place the vignette on the inside or outside. The mask alone can also be rendered, replacing the source layer entirely. Adobe Premiere and Adobe After Effects are supported on Windows 7 64-bit and on macOS X 10.6.x and 10.7.x. The Adobe Premiere edition supports 8 bits and 32 bits (Maximum Bit Depth). In After Effects, support is currently limited to 8 bit and 16 bit depth.
