# Pixelan

DissolveMaster

DissolveMaster is a hardware-accelerated Premiere transitions plug-in for building dissolves and fades, including film dissolves, soft directional dissolves, blur dissolves, glow/flash dissolves, and dissolve transitions that were previously unavailable. For editors who mostly use cuts and dissolves, it adds subtle ways to guide the viewer's eye within a scene through a clean, unobtrusive dissolve style.

SpiceMaster

SpiceMaster supplies fully customizable soft and organic effects and transitions as creative, subtle alternatives to standard looks. It can also animate any effect or key in Premiere organically by using built-in or third-party effects. Titles, picture-in-picture effects, backgrounds, and graphics can also be animated organically. SpiceMaster gives full control over effect parameters and supplies thousands of presets ready for immediate use.

3D Six-Pack

This affordable, easy-to-use plug-in adds 250-plus 3D transitions to Premiere. 3D Six-Pack improves on existing 3D transitions with more realistic and natural replacements and adds long-requested new options. The set includes organic page-turns, rolls/slides, bursts, swirls, depth effects, 3D distortions, and natural transitions that reveal the next scene through wind, gravity, or other organic movement. Motion acceleration and transparency changes add a natural look to all transitions.

CreativEase

CreativEase consists of 11 affordable effects plug-ins with hundreds of fresh aesthetic video effects for Premiere users. It includes customizable color, blur, depth, grain, and time-based effects. The plug-ins can also handle everyday editing problems, including selective softening for facial blemishes, dreamy looks, color-accurate cartoon animation directly from video, glossy 3D titles, subtle film looks and film grains, frame-edge noise cropping without image distortion, and more.

FilmTouch 2 Pro

Pixelan's GPU-accelerated FilmTouch Pro plug-in contains 500+ professional film-look presets. Its controls can refine color subtleties or create a new evocative look from scratch. A large visual Effects Browser lets users compare and choose presets interactively. Favorites can be marked and organized by project/client, unwanted presets can be hidden, and custom looks can be saved. Scene-based and geometric masking is powerful but easy, allowing an existing correct color or luminance area to remain untouched, such as a face, car or other subject, sky, or logo's color.

Blur-Sharpen Tools

This set groups BlurBlender Pro, Smart Blur Pro, and Smart Sharpen Pro as three Pixelan GPU-accelerated plug-ins. BlurBlender Pro includes 650+ aesthetic blur effects tuned for hi-res material, including 4K, video/film, and titles/text. Custom controls, randomization features, and adjustable masking help place the blur precisely. Smart Blur Pro blur effects preserve scene detail, while Smart Sharpen Pro sharpening reduces artifacts. Both offer masking controls for applying the effect only where needed. All three use a large Visual Effects Browser.
