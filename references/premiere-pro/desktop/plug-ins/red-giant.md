# Red Giant

Denoiser

Footage can be cleaned with Denoiser, which removes unwanted noise, grain, and artifacts while keeping detail intact across shooting and lighting conditions. Smart defaults provide quick smoothing, and advanced controls are available for more difficult clips.

Knoll Light Factory

Knoll Light Factory 3.0 was created by John Knoll, Photoshop co-creator and "Star Wars" effects guru, and is a popular motion graphics tool. It creates high-fidelity flare simulations that mimic reflections from a bright light source as seen through a camera lens. Knoll Light Factory 3 adds realistic flare elements, animation behaviors that save time, a redesigned interface, and more than 100 designed presets.

Magic Bullet Looks

Magic Bullet Looks brings professional color finishing into a project quickly. Pro presets and camera-inspired controls make the desired look easier to achieve.

Magic Bullet Mojo

Mojo applies a modern Hollywood-style color treatment that warms actors' skin tones while cooling backgrounds and shadows, while keeping the talent in focus. It produces the look in seconds and includes easy customizable controls for different footage.

Magic Bullet Colorista II

Colorista II brings high-end color-system capability to the desktop. Users can do professional color grading inside favorite editing applications and move between those applications with consistent, high-quality results. The 3-Way Wheels adjust balance and luminance, a new Keyer supports precision edits, and Power Masks isolate an area for finishing.

Magic Bullet Cosmo


Magic Bullet Frames

Magic Bullet Frames applies sophisticated algorithms to ordinary interlaced video. The result is a smooth, professional, deinterlaced 24p film look. Users can choose motion-adaptive or field-blended methods for high-quality footage at high speeds directly in the editing timeline.

Magic Bullet Instant 4K

Instant 4K up-converts HD video to multiple 4K and UHD video formats. Preset resolutions help integrate HD video into a 4K production. During high definition (HD) to 4K/Ultra HD (UHD) scaling, Instant 4K algorithms generate missing pixels, adding sharpening and anti-aliasing for clear output inside the video application.

Magic Bullet Quick Looks

Quick Looks applies a chosen style to footage in seconds, whether the target is urban grunge, a desert sunrise, or film stocks from classic movies. It is a lighter version of the full Looks tool, with the same speed and quality at a lower cost. Users can define the video or film style from 100-plus look presets for moods ranging from wedding footage to Sundance-style projects.

Magic Bullet LUT Buddy

Magic Bullet LUT Buddy is built for professional colorists and connects desktop applications with high-end systems and hardware boxes. With Adobe Premiere, LUT Buddy can output LUTs and communicate with higher-end systems from DaVinci Resolve to Panasonic displays. For workflows already using 1D or 3D LUTs, the tool helps move values among compatible software and devices.

Magic Bullet Colorista Free

Magic Bullet Colorista Free is a free product that encourages users to set their colors free through a simplified, elegant color corrector that can be shared and used broadly. Core controls designed by experienced colorists include the 3-Way Wheels from Colorista II and numeric sliders for exact numerical adjustment. For advanced workflows, Colorista Free is able to exchange Color Decision List (CDL) values with CDL-compliant host apps and align color work to industry standards.

Presto

Red Giant Presto saves time by turning a live presentation event into a high-quality video. It imports a timeline with presenter footage into the editing application and aligns it with the original presentation slides. Presto handles production tasks such as tracking presenter movement, switching slides at the correct moment, and rearranging the layout instantly. The finished result is a professionally designed video that can be shared soon after the presentation ends.

PluralEyes

PluralEyes 3 automatically synchronizes audio and multi-camera video, preparing sync in seconds instead of days. This stand-alone application works directly with Premiere. Users can monitor sync through an interactive timeline and visual feedback, then apply built-in fine tuning controls. Version 3 is up to 20x faster than PluralEyes 2 and supports many file formats and codecs. PluralEyes 3 is intended to make post-production audio/video sync faster, less tedious, and more accurate.

Trapcode 3D Stroke

3D Stroke lets paths, logos, or lines glow, taper, and move through 3D space in complex ways. A built-in camera captures the action from any angle. It is used for animated logos and sophisticated write-on effects in broadcast TV work.

Trapcode Shine

Shine creates light effects from inside the editing timeline without the render times of specialized 3D applications. It can produce sunlight through clouds, headlights through fog, or horror movie titles. Users can select from 22 powerful presets or build custom light ray effects. 16-bit and 32-bit support helps maintain high-quality light effects for any project.

Trapcode Starglow

Starglow is a multidirectional glow effect designed for fast rendering. It can make multicolored, star-shaped glints like those produced by a camera filter. Each customizable glow can include up to eight directions, and every direction can use its own color map and streak length for many possible looks.

Red Giant Warp

Warp plug-ins provide control over shadows, reflections, glows, and corner point warps. The Shadow tool creates shadows for text or greenscreen subjects, while the Reflection tool builds mirror-like reflections. Radium Glow adds refined glows and glimmers. The Corner Pin tool improves realism for warped images, can import tracking data from mocha for After Effects, and supports secondary transforms and motion blur.

Red Giant Text Anarchy

Text Anarchy treats text as a major compositional element, whether it is used for visual background noise, texture, or flashing words that reinforce a theme. It can generate effects from Bezier paths, particle arrays, and matrix-style patterns while retaining fine typographic control. The package also includes the Geomancy Shapes plug-ins.

Red Giant ToonIt

ToonIt turns video into a cartoon look quickly with algorithms for cartoon shading and line generation. Its five plug-ins avoid time-consuming methods such as rotoscoping and hand painting while still letting users control styles, shading, and outlines precisely. Version 2 adds the Heat Vision plug-in, which mimics the look of the Predator movies, plus 14 new styles, additional presets, and faster output.

Universe

As a free community, Red Giant Universe provides access to fast free tools for editing, visual effects, and motion design. Every effects and transitions tool in the Universe library is GPU-enabled for speed and works in multiple host applications on both Mac and Windows. The library keeps expanding with new effects and transitions, and existing tools are updated often.
