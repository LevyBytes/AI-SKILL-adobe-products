# ACCEVOLUTION

Transition Designer Free

The free edition supplies a high-quality wipe transition and many adjustable parameters so each transition can be tuned to the edit. It includes angle control for the wipe effect and remains GPU-accelerated.

Transition Designer Lite

Transition Designer Lite adds a transition plug-in with Wipe, Push, Dissolve, and Rumble effects plus multiple variations. You can feather edges, alter directions, or add Glow or Vaporize to every transition except Rumble.

Transition Designer Full

Transition Designer Full is positioned for professional editors facing deadlines and demanding clients. Instead of cheap-looking transitions, it offers a sophisticated toolset meant to support polished storytelling.

Split Screen Designer

Split Screen Designer creates polished split-screen effects quickly and at high quality. No programming skills are required, and operations can be done with a simple mouse click. Keyframes provide precise control over every split design, so users can generate custom split screen effects in a few clicks and build Premiere looks comparable to high-end TV work.

FX Tiles

3D filters and transition effects with flexible animation control plus many included presets.
