# Digital Anarchy

Beauty Box Video

As an award-winning skin smoothing plug-in, Beauty Box Video works in After Effects and Premiere. It provides digital makeup in post-production by using state-of-the-art face detection and smoothing algorithms for actor or model retouching. Beauty Box detects skin tones automatically and removes blemishes and wrinkles while preserving important facial detail. It is easy to use, and its retouching is intended to look realistic and attractive. Because HD and 4K reveal more blemishes and makeup issues, Beauty Box addresses those problems while keeping talent and clients looking good.

Flicker Free

Flicker Free provides deflickering for video. It addresses flicker in time lapse footage, slow motion clips, and rolling artifacts from LED lights, fluorescent lights, or computer monitors. Presets cover common flicker cases, while a small set of parameters lets users adjust the correction as needed. The demo can be tested on user footage.
