# NewBlueFX

Elements 3 Ultimate

Compatibility: Adobe Premiere, CS6/6.5, and Adobe After Effects 7.0+

NewBlue Elements 3 Ultimate provides over 250 presets across 21 video effects for simplifying multi-step compositing work. It can build split screens, create drop shadows, place a viewfinder overlay, and key green screen footage within the editing application. Whether the task is a quick backdrop, a wild car chase simulation, or experimentation with textures and mattes, NewBlue Elements 3 Ultimate keeps detailed control available.

Essentials 3 Ultimate

Compatibility: Adobe Premiere, CS6/6.5, and Adobe After Effects 7.0+

NewBlue Essentials 3 Ultimate provides over 275 presets in 24 workflow-oriented video effects for stabilizing shaky footage, removing flashes, and sharpening detail. The toolkit is designed for everyday post-production needs such as color correction and stabilization. Included tools cover Noise Reducer, Lens Corrector, Stabilizer, and Flash Remover, while image-enhancement options include Skin Touch Up, Detail Enhancer, and more.

Filters 3 Ultimate

Compatibility: Adobe Premiere, CS6/6.5, and Adobe After Effects 7.0+

NewBlue Filters 3 Ultimate includes over 250 presets in 22 video effects for quickly improving video with distinctive looks such as Fish Eye and Day for Night, as well as color enhancers including Colorize and Selective Tint. ColorFast provides a streamlined correction and grading workflow for finding precise color balance, while looks from Film Color and Old TV through Tilt Shift and Dream Glow help set mood without slowing the edit.

Stylizers 3 Ultimate

Compatibility: Adobe Premiere, CS6/6.5, and Adobe After Effects 7.0+

NewBlue Stylizers 3 Ultimate adds colorful art, paint, and light treatments through over 250 presets in 23 imaginative video plug-ins. Cartoonr, Air Brush, and Watercolor provide painting styles. Light-driven effects, from Neon Lights to Glow Pro, create accents and atmosphere. Metallic, Impressionist, and Paper Collage explore more surreal filters. NewBlue Stylizers 3 Ultimate spans impressionist treatments, energetic blurs, and flickers for matching a desired look.

Transitions 3 Ultimate

Compatibility: Adobe Premiere, CS6/6.5, and Adobe After Effects 7.0+

NewBlue Transitions 3 Ultimate offers over 400 presets in 37 plug-ins for scene-to-scene video transitions built around 3D, light, color, and motion. The set spans Cartoonr Plus, Color Wash, Neon Lights, Metallic, Shake, Shredders, and many other stylized options for different moods and situations. Duochrome can make retro metallic imagery, while Colored Pencil can simulate a drawn image at the end of one scene that changes into the next. The collection is intended to keep the audience engaged with varied, stylized transitions.

TotalFX

Compatibility: Adobe Premiere, CS6/6.5, and Adobe After Effects 7.0+

NewBlue TotalFX provides over 1400 presets in 129 plug-ins, covering titling, transitions, and video effects. It combines advanced compositing from NewBlue Elements Ultimate, productivity-focused tools from NewBlue Essentials Ultimate, color correction methods and looks from NewBlue Filters Ultimate, creative treatments from NewBlue Stylizers Ultimate, scene-to-scene transition techniques from NewBlue Transitions Ultimate, and the dynamic titling features of NewBlue Titler Pro 3 Ultimate, including additional Styles and Lower Thirds. This broad professional suite is built to cover nearly every editing task.
