# StageTools

MovingPicture

MovingPicture lets producers and editors pan and zoom across high-resolution still images. It accepts scanned images up to 8,000 pixels and can repeat pans and zooms exactly from a Windows or Mac computer or through a plug-in to Adobe Premiere.

Broadcasters, documentary film producers, schools, and corporate and event videographers use MovingPicture. Client examples include ABC, CBS, FOX, NBC, BBC, PBS, Discovery, Lifetime, A&E, and documentary filmmaker Ken Burns.
