# PF_OutFlag_REFRESH_UI

`PF_OutFlag_REFRESH_UI`

Refresh the whole of the effect controls, composition, and layer windows. Set during `PF_Cmd_EVENT`, `PF_Cmd_RENDER`, and `PF_Cmd_DO_DIALOG`. When you refresh custom UI under `PF_Cmd_EVENT`, our recommendation is the new redraw mechanism with its finer granularity.
