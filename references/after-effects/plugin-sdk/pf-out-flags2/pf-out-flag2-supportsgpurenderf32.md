# PF_OutFlag2_SUPPORTS_GPU_RENDER_F32

`PF_OutFlag2_SUPPORTS_GPU_RENDER_F32`

New in 16.0 Setting it at `PF_Cmd_GLOBAL_SETUP` declares GPU support: the effect then receives GPU selectors and shows a GPU-supporting badge in the GUI. When `PF_Cmd_GPU_DEVICE_SETUP` runs, these flags spell out what a particular device and framework can render.
