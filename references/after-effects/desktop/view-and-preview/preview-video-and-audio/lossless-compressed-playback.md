# Lossless Compressed Playback

> Adobe After Effects reference. Original prose; identifiers preserved verbatim.

Search Last updated on Jan 21, 2026

Learn about how Lossless Compressed Playback helps you preview longer timeline segments with minimal disk usage and no loss in visual quality.

With Lossless Compressed Playback you can preview stretches of your timeline that run longer while taking up far less disk space, all without giving up any visual quality. Cached frames get compressed automatically by After Effects in a lossless format, so full image fidelity survives even as disk usage is trimmed.

The whole thing happens quietly in the background. While you work, compressed frames are written to disk by After Effects, which lets more frames fit inside your disk limit and stretches playback duration — none of it disturbing your workflow.

The feature ships turned on, though you can switch it off should you need to.

Select After Effects > Settings > Disk for macOS.

For Windows, select Edit > Preferences > Disk.

Under the Disk Cache section, clear the Enable Compressed Frames (Lossless) option.

2. Turn on the Enable Compressed Frames (Lossless) option to preview longer stretches of your timeline while consuming far less disk space.
