# Customize the appearance of expressions in the Expression Editor

Expression Editor in the Timeline panel

Expression Editor in the Timeline panel

The look of expressions shown in the Expression Editor — the one docked in the Timeline panel — is yours to adjust through the Scripting & Expressions preferences.

Reach Expression Editor by one of these routes:

- macOS: Select After Effects > Settings > Scripting & Expressions.
- Windows: Select Edit > Preferences > Scripting & Expressions.
