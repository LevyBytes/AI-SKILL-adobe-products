# The Expression Language menu

Living in the Timeline panel, the Expression Language menu holds After Effects–specific language elements that you can drop into an expression. Treat it as a reference for which elements are available — it's handy for confirming valid elements and getting their syntax right. Choose any object, attribute, or method from it, and After Effects drops that selection straight into the expression field at the insertion point. When text in the expression field is already selected, the inserted expression text takes its place. When the insertion point sits outside the expression field, the inserted text overwrites everything in the field.

Lean on the Expression Language menu to build and maintain expressions with less effort.
