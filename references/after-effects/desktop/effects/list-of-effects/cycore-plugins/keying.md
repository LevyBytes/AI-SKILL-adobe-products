# Keying

Got an unwanted element in your shot — a wire or some small object? The Simple Wire Removal effect is built to take it out. Its controls let you paint across the offending item so it melds into what surrounds it. The effect pays off most on locked-off shots, where that object holds the same spot frame to frame.

Pictured: the CC Simple Wire Removal effect in After Effects clearing a highlighted region to expose the layer beneath — a horizontal line laid out from Point A and Point B, thickness at 100, and the Removal Style set to Fade.
