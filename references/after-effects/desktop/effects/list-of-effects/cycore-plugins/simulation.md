# Simulation

Reach for the CC Simulation effects when you want lifelike touches such as storms, fire, or smoke dropped into a scene. They build a richer mood through animated elements that read as natural.

- CC Bubbles: Conjures bubbles drifting up through liquid, a great match for underwater shots or a glass of soda.
- CC Drizzle: Lays down a faint rain look, the kind of gentle drizzle that drifts over the comp.
- CC Hair: Mimics strands — hair, thread, or fiber — that sway naturally with wind or movement.
- CC Mr. Mercury: Yields a flowing, dripping liquid look reminiscent of mercury or other heavy fluids.
- CC Particle Systems II: Builds looks such as fire, smoke, explosions, or dust out of particles.
- CC Particle World: Drops in particle-driven looks where the particles roam freely through 3D space, with tunable traits like color, speed, and size.
- CC Pixel Polly: Shatters a layer into little shards or pixels so it appears to fall apart.
- CC Rainfall: Lays down falling rain, with drops descending from the top edge of the comp.
- CC Scatterize: Breaks a layer into smaller bits and flings them at random inside a set region.
- CC Snowfall: Drops a snow look, flakes drifting down from above like a winter scene.
- CC Star Burst: Fires particles out from a central point, like the opening of an explosion or an energy blast.
