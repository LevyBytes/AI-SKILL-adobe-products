# Transition

In After Effects, the CC Transition effects are there to build polished, inventive hand-offs from one clip to the next. The set spans dissolves, wipes, and slides, so moving between scenes carries extra style and visual interest.

- CC Glass Wipe: Lays in a transition that mimics a streak or smudge being wiped clear, exposing the clip beneath.
- CC Grid Wipe: Lays in a transition whose grid pattern slowly uncovers the next clip or image.
- CC Image Wipe: Lays in a transition that wipes an image or texture across the screen as a mask, exposing the layer below.
- CC Jaws: Lays in a transition that mimics a chomp — a giant toothy mouth "eating" the current scene to expose the next.
- CC Light Wipe: Runs a light source sweeping over the screen, wiping the current scene away and bringing up the next.
- CC Line Sweep: Runs a single moving line across the screen that gradually uncovers the clip behind it.
- CC Radial ScaleWipe: Lays in a transition that wipes with a zoom starting from the center or an edge, scaling into the next scene.
- CC Scale Wipe: Lays in a transition that pairs a swipe with a zoom to glide smoothly into the next scene.
- CC Twister: Lays in a swirling, twisting move that distorts the current scene as it crosses into the next.
- CC WarpoMatic: Lays in a stretchy warp that bends and distorts the current scene while it morphs into the next.
