# Utility

What the Utility effect hands you is an assortment of tools — fusing several layers or elements, tuning brightness and contrast, dialing in color balance, and more.

- CC Overbright: Lays on an effect that pushes the brightness of highlight regions, yielding an overexposed or glowing read while leaving midtones and shadows alone.
