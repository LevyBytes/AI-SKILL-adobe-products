# Paint a selection with Quick Selection tool

In Photoshop, the Quick Selection tool lets you create fast, precise selections by painting over the area you want.

Choose the Quick Selection tool from the toolbar.

In the Options bar, choose a selection mode: New selection, Add to selection, or Subtract from selection.

From the pop-up menu, choose these Brush settings.

- Size: changes the brush tip diameter.
- Hardness: controls how sharp the brush edges are.
- Spacing: sets the distance between brush marks within a stroke.
- Angle: rotates the brush tip to a specific angle.
- Roundness: adjusts the brush tip shape by changing its roundness.
- Size: makes brush behavior respond to pen pressure or the stylus wheel.

To adjust brush sensitivity, choose Size from the drop-down menu.

Use these settings to fine-tune the selection.

- Sample All Layers : builds the selection from all layers rather than only the currently selected layer.
- Enhance Edge: improves edges automatically by making them smoother and more refined.

Paint across the desired area to expand the selection along detected edges.

Choose Select and Mask for additional selection refinement.
