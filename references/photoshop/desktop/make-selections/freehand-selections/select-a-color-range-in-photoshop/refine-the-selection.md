# Refine the selection

Choose Select > Color Range.

In the Selection Preview drop-down menu, choose one of these options:

- None: shows the original image.
- Grayscale: white = selected, black = unselected, gray = partial.
- Black Matte: shows selected pixels while unselected areas appear black.
- White Matte: shows selected pixels while unselected areas appear white.
- Quick Mask: displays unselected areas with a rubylith overlay.

To restore the original selection, hold Alt (Windows) / Option (macOS) and choose Reset.
