# Overview of Adobe Photoshop (beta) on desktop

See how Adobe Photoshop (beta) provides advance access to features before they ship broadly.

Photoshop (beta) installs as its own app and can run next to your standard Photoshop installation. It lets you try features that remain under development before they reach the regular product. Adobe uses this pre-release channel to collect direct user feedback ahead of release. With Photoshop (beta), upcoming features can be evaluated without interrupting production work. Both apps can stay installed, and you can move between them whenever needed.
