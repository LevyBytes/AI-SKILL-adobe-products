# Draw curved segments

With Pen tool, click where the curve should begin, then drag to pull out direction handles that define the curve.

Place the cursor at the intended curve endpoint. Drag to continue with another curve, or click once when the segment should finish as a corner point.

Tip

To reshape during drawing, drag a direction handle while holding Alt (Windows) or Option (macOS).

To make an S-curve, drag the second point opposite the direction of the first point's direction handle.

Close the path with a curve by dragging the starting point.
