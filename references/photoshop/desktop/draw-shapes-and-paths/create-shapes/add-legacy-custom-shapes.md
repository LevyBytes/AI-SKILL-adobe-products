# Add legacy custom shapes

For older-project compatibility, open legacy custom shapes from earlier Adobe Photoshop versions through the Shapes panel.

Choose Window > Shapes to display the Shapes panel.

In the upper-right corner of the Shapes panel, choose the hamburger menu icon.

From the subpanel, choose Legacy Shapes and More.

Look through the Shapes panel and choose the legacy shapes you need.

Note

After choosing shapes, draw them on the canvas with the Custom Shape tool.
