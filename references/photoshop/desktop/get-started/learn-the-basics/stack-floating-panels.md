# Stack floating panels

Group related floating panels into stacks to keep the Adobe Photoshop workspace organized.

In Photoshop, pulling a panel out of its dock turns it into a floating panel that can sit anywhere in the workspace. Dropping it into a drop zone docks it again. Floating panels or panel groups can also be stacked, letting them travel together when you drag the top panel's title bar.

Drag the tab of a floating panel.

When a blue outline appears, drop the tab on the lower edge of another panel. Place the tab over the narrow drop zone between panels rather than on the title bar.

To revise the stack order, drag a panel by its tab and move it upward or downward.

Stack floating panels to keep the Photoshop workspace orderly and efficient.

Use stacked floating panels when related Photoshop controls should move together.

To let a panel float on its own, drag its tab out of the stack.

Tip

Double-clicking a tab minimizes or maximizes a panel, group, or stack. Resize it by dragging any panel edge.
