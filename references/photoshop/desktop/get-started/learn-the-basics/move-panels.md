# Move panels

Move panels in Adobe Photoshop to keep the workspace arranged for the way you edit.

Choose the panel by selecting its tab.

Move the panel to another location by dragging its tab. Blue drop zones appear during the move to show valid placement areas.

Arrange panels in Photoshop around your workflow so image editing stays efficient.

Move Photoshop panels into useful positions for an editing workspace that suits the task.

To dock the panel, drop it onto a blue zone. Without a drop zone, the panel remains floating.

Hold Ctrl on Windows or Command on macOS while moving a panel to keep it from docking.

Press Esc during the move to cancel the operation.

For a free-floating panel, drag it away from docks and drop zones into an empty part of the workspace.

Tip

Choose Window > Workspace > Lock Workspace to avoid accidental panel movement when working with a stylus.
