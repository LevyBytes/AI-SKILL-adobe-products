# Restore saved workspaces

From the Application bar, open Choose a workspace.

For one preset or custom workspace, choose Reset &lt;Workspace Name&gt;.

For every Photoshop preset workspace, use Edit > Preferences > Workspace (Windows) or Photoshop > Settings > Workspace (macOS). After that, choose Restore Default Workspaces.

This restores all Photoshop preset workspaces, such as Essentials and Photography, to their original layout.
