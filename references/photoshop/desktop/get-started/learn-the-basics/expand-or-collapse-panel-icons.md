# Expand or collapse panel icons

Collapse panels into icons, or expand them again, to manage space efficiently in Adobe Photoshop.

Icon-collapsed panels keep the workspace tidy while leaving tools within reach, which is especially useful on smaller screens. Photoshop supports fully expanded panels, icon-only collapsed panels, and collapsed panels with labels; some default workspaces already use icon-collapsed panels.

Choose one panel icon when only that panel should expand.

Use the double arrow at the dock's upper-right corner to collapse every panel into an icon.

To show only the icons, narrow the dock until labels are no longer visible.

Make the dock wider to show the labels again.

Keep important panels expanded and collapse the rest for quicker workspace access.

Expand the panels you use most, then leave less-used panels collapsed for faster access.

Use the double arrow in the panel's title bar to collapse the panel back to an icon.

Tip

Selecting a panel's tab or icon can also collapse that panel.

Move a floating panel or panel group into the icon dock by dragging it with the title bar. Once placed there, the panel becomes an icon in the dock.

Reorder a panel icon by dragging it up or down in the dock, or drag it outside the dock to make it float.

Icons moved to another dock take on the style of that dock.
