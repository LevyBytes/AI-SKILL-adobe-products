# Arrange and group panels

Arrange panels and create custom panel groups in Adobe Photoshop to keep workspace controls organized.

Photoshop panels keep important tools close at hand, which helps work stay organized and efficient. Grouping and arranging panels lets you tailor the workspace for creative tasks, including complex projects. For example, organized panels can speed up poster editing.

Open Window and choose the panels required for the task. Photoshop places the selected panels in the workspace.

To add a panel to a group, drag its tab to the highlighted drop zone inside that group.

Arrange the panels to make the workspace cleaner and more productive.

Use panel organization to simplify the workspace and improve productivity.

To change the order of panels in a group, drag a panel tab to a different position within that group.

To take a panel out of a group and make it float, drag the panel by its tab outside the group.

Move a group by dragging the title bar that holds the panel tabs.
