# Use Content Credentials

In Adobe Photoshop, Content Credentials can carry attribution details and the image's edit history with exported files.

When you export from Photoshop, Content Credentials attach authorship information and editing history to the image. That added context helps audiences understand the work's background and supports credit as the image moves online.

Select Window > Content Credentials (Beta).

Select Enable Content Credentials.

Tip

To make Content Credentials active by default on new documents, open Photoshop > Settings > History & Content Credentials. Turn on Enable for saved documents with Content Credentials.

Select Enable for new and saved documents with Content Credentials so edits are recorded from the beginning through the end.
