# Icons for Your Plugins

Icons appear in two separate places. The plugin-level icon, outside the entrypoints, appears in the "Plugins panel". Photoshop applies some automatic treatment to that icon, and its size is 24x24, or 48x48 at 2x. By contrast, a panel icon inside `entrypoints` appears in toolbars when the panel is minimized, and Photoshop does not add extra treatment. The required size is 23x23, or 46x46, transparency is allowed, and the icon should track the Ps theme when visual consistency with other tools and panel icons matters. A plugin may include several panels, and each panel needs an icon that users can tell apart when choosing which panel to show. Panel icons are declared inside an `entrypoint` object in the manifest. These panel icons are needed in addition to the plugin-wide `icons` array at the root of `manifest.json`.
